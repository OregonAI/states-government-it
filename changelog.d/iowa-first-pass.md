Iowa added: 16 statutes (15 sections of Iowa Code chapter 8, the Department of Management's Budget and Financial Control Act, which since 2024 Acts, ch 1185 holds the state's information technology law in subchapter XI: 8.5, 8.76 to 8.78, 8.80 to 8.85, 8.88 to 8.90, 8.92 and 8.93, among them the chief information officer's powers (8.77) and the cybersecurity statute (8.93); and chapter 715C, the breach notification statute, which binds state agencies by its definition of 'person'), 2 rule chapters of the Office of the Chief Information Officer (Iowa Admin. Code 129-8, information technology governance, and 129-10, procurement of information technology), the 4 policies on the State of Iowa policy portal (including the Generative AI Policy), and 27 standards and policies of the OCIO standards library. 6 stubs: Executive Orders 87, 73, 61, 66 and Culver 26, whose PDFs carry an embedded OCR layer that the shared OCR procedure refuses, and Executive Order 18 (2026), whose scan has a rotated page that the shared OCR procedure cannot be told to re-read (a shared change is needed for both). 6 watch entries (the OCIO standards index, the policy portal list, the Legislature's executive order list, the Chief Information Officer agency's rules, the chapter 8 section index and the repealed chapter 8B). No enacted Iowa statute on AI use by state agencies was found (Senate File 2417 binds private operators of conversational AI). New state module src/states/iowa.py reads the policy portal's HTML pages and checks the identity and version of Code sections, Administrative Code chapters, executive orders, standards and policies.
