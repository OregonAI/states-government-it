Vermont added: 6 statutes (3 V.S.A. chapter 10 Federal Tax Information, 3 V.S.A. chapter 56 Agency of Digital Services, 3 V.S.A. chapter 69 Division of Artificial Intelligence, 22 V.S.A. chapter 16 The Vermont Web Portal, 20 V.S.A. chapter 208 Cybersecurity, and 9 V.S.A. chapter 62 Protection of Personal Information, each from the Legislature's full-chapter page), 3 executive orders (06-17 creating the Agency of Digital Services, 18-17 Governor's Cybersecurity Advisory Team, 04-26 Vermont AI Economic Taskforce), and 6 Agency of Digital Services and related instruments: the Information Security Foundations Policy, Cybersecurity Standard Update 2023-01, the AI Code of Ethics, the Guidelines for Use of Generative AI, the IT Procurement Guideline, and the vermont.gov Accessibility Policy page. 2 stubs (the ADS Records Management Policy, whose first page is a scan with a poor text layer, and the vendor AI usage disclosure, a Word clause with a questionnaire and check boxes); 7 watch entries (four ADS pages, the Governor's executive order list, the vermont.gov policies page and the digital accessibility hub). No administrative rules: the State publishes them only through a Lexis script page with no text. Every row of the ADS policy, cybersecurity and AI pages was reconciled and the left-out items recorded; the ADS governing statutes page cites sections that are repealed (3 V.S.A. 2283b, 22 V.S.A. 901, 32 V.S.A. 315). New state module src/states/vermont.py reads the Legislature's full-chapter pages (title, chapter and subchapter headings, section captions, one line per paragraph), reads a vermont.gov page, and checks statute identity by title and chapter and an order's identity by its own heading line.
