Delaware added: 5 statutes from the Delaware Code Online (Title 29 chapter 90C, the Department of Technology and Information, as its four subchapter pages: general provisions with the CIO, internet privacy, the Cyber Security Advisory Council and the Artificial Intelligence Commission; and Title 6 chapter 12B, computer security breaches) and 31 documents from DTI's PDFs (29 from the Standards and Policies library: 17 Enterprise Policies, including the Information Security Policy and the Generative Artificial Intelligence Policy, and 12 standards and guidelines; and two the library page does not list: the GenAI examples and Prohibited GenAI Tools list that the AI policy links, and DTI policy DTI-0065.02 on confidentiality and integrity of data), each with its printed revision and review date as its source version; 6 stubs (the Enterprise Services Guide and the Systems Architecture Standard, available only on the State network; the blank Cloud Services and Data Usage Agreement; and three executive orders, Markell 20, Carney 18 and Hall-Long 3, whose scans carry a text layer the shared OCR procedure refuses); 3 watch entries (the library index, the Governor's executive order list and the chapter 90C index). The Administrative Code (a JavaScript application) holds DTI's one rule, a FOIA procedure, left out. New state module src/states/delaware.py reads the Delaware Code Online pages.
