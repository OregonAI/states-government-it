Missouri added: 11 statutes from the Revisor of Statutes' one-section pages (sections 37.005, 37.110 and 37.920 of the Office of Administration chapter, the Missouri GIS Advisory Council sections 37.1300, 37.1310 and 37.1320, the Cybersecurity Mission Act sections 41.1015 to 41.1018 and the breach-notification section 407.1500), 4 executive orders from the Secretary of State's text pages (03-26, 05-07 and 06-34 on the Office of Information Technology, the Information Technology Services Division and the Chief Information Officer, and 26-02 on artificial intelligence in state government), 7 Office of Administration policies (ITPS-001 laptop encryption, SP-18 data security training, SP-19 acceptable use of artificial intelligence, and the IT-related OA policies C-09, C-12, C-16 and C-19) and the ITSD Change Control Policy, and all 97 items of ITSD's Security Domain Approved Standards library (3 disciplines, 22 technology areas and 72 compliance components, PDFs embedded in the viewer pages the library links). 1 stub (1 CSR 10-2, the Commissioner of Administration's privacy rule, a two-column PDF the shared reader cannot put in reading order) and 9 watch entries (the Approved Standards, EPMO, Statewide Policies and OA Administrative Policies pages, the Secretary of State's and the Governor's executive-order lists, Title 1 of the Code of State Regulations and the Revisor's chapter 37 and 41 section lists). All official indexes were reconciled row by row, every executive-order page from 1980 to 2026 was read for IT subjects, and the left-out record carries the counts and reasons. New state module src/states/missouri.py reads the Revisor's section pages and the Secretary of State's order pages.
