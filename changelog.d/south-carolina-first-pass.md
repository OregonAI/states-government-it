South Carolina added: 3 statutes from the General Assembly's Code of Laws chapter pages (Title 1 chapter 11, the Department of Administration and the State's central IT function, taken whole; Title 11 chapter 53, the South Carolina Enterprise Information System; Title 30 chapter 2, the Family Privacy Protection Act and the personal identifying information privacy articles), 2 executive orders (No. 2017-08 on critical infrastructure cybersecurity and No. 2022-03 on IT shared services, both from the Governor's text-alternative PDFs) and 11 policies and standards from the Department of Administration's Governance and Resources library: the Statewide Information Security Program Policy, the Mobile Device Protection Policy, the SCDIS-501 media disposal procedure, the end-user computing, hyper converged infrastructure and email standards, the Enterprise Technology Architecture and its principles, the IT shared services governance overview and the data classification and data handling guidelines. 1 stub (SCDIS-200, an Excel workbook) and 3 watch entries (the Governance and Resources page, the Governor's executive order list and the AI Center of Excellence page). The library was reconciled row by row (26 rows) and all 443 rows of the Governor's list were read; the left-out record carries the reasons, including the draft-marked IT Governance deck, the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act sections that bind agencies (owner decision) and the appropriations provisos that are the legal basis of the State's IT, cybersecurity and AI instruments (an owner decision) and the Code of Regulations chapter 19, which holds no IT agency rules. New state module src/states/south-carolina.py reads the chapter pages and checks the 'Executive Order No. N' heading.
