Massachusetts added: 4 statute sections under the owner decision of 2026-11-13 (a provision in a non-IT chapter is in scope when its own focus is the governance of state IT): G.L. c. 6A section 7A (EOTSS as the commonwealth's lead IT organization), c. 30 section 63 (notice of each agency's personal data system), c. 36 section 40 (compatibility of registries of deeds' technology purchases) and c. 214 section 3B (remedies against a c. 66A holder). Each is the chapter heading and that one section, read from the Legislature's section page after the chapter's table of contents was checked to link it. Declined with reasons in the left-out record: c. 6A section 109 (race and ethnicity data collection) and c. 214 section 1B (general privacy right). The state module gained one-section statute entries: a snapshot of the chapter page and the section page, an identity check that the text holds exactly the cited section, and a refusal of a repealed section.
