Wyoming: one statute slice added under the owner's decision of 2026-11-13 (a provision of a non-IT chapter is in scope when its own focus is the governance of state IT): W.S. 40-21-117 through 40-21-119, the sections of the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act chapter addressed to governmental agencies (agencies decide how they create, retain, send, accept and sign electronic records; the Department of Enterprise Technology Services makes the rules for state agencies and promotes interoperability), cut from the Legislature's Title 40 PDF. The state module now cuts the shared section-range slice (`slice: {section_start, section_end_before}`), drops the chapter heading that sits before the end section, and checks that the slice opens on the first cited section, ends on the last and is not repealed. Left out with the measured reason, in the left-out record: the rest of the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (40-21-101 to 40-21-116, general transaction law), the Public Records Act (the general right of access; reason in docs/research/wyoming-authority-2026-11-13.md), the public finance website act (9-2-3219 to 9-2-3221, fiscal transparency) and the county recording sections (34-1-404, 34-1-405, local government).
