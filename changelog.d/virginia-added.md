Stubs: Executive Order 46 (2025, DeepSeek; the Library of Virginia serves a script viewer) and the Enterprise Data Standards workbook (a spreadsheet). A Virginia module reads the Code of Virginia pages and VITA's web-page policies, checks their identity and reads the effective date from the left column of the two-column publication table, and leaves it empty where a document prints a placeholder or a list of dates. No rules are in force (VITA is 1VAC40 and Title 1 lists no Agency 40 chapters), and no statute that binds state agencies on AI.
