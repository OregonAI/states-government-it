South Carolina: 5 provisions whose own focus is the governance of state IT, taken as section slices on the owner's decision of 2026-11-13 (a provision that is not itself about IT is in scope when it governs state IT): 1 procurement-code section (S.C. Code 11-35-820, the Information Technology Management Office), 1 slice of the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act sections that bind agencies (26-6-170 to 26-6-195), 1 records slice (30-1-90 alone, retention schedules including electronic records; 30-1-80, the general records management program, is left out as not IT-focused) and 2 procurement regulations from Code of Regulations chapter 19 (19-445.2027 Electronic Commerce and 19-445.2115 Information Technology Procurements). The state module cuts a slice `{section_start, section_end_before}` from a chapter page (HTML) and from a whole-file PDF (regulation, or a proviso of the Appropriations Act, dropping the proviso PDF's running page header), and refuses a slice whose first or last heading is not the cited one, a repealed or deleted single section and text that prints an effective date after the day it is run. Nine provisos of the FY 2026-27 Appropriations Act (93.9, 117.95, 117.96, 117.101, 117.106, 117.117, 117.120, 117.194, 117.209) were verified and are held back because the shared authority forms cannot parse a proviso citation (docs/research/south-carolina-provisos.md). The left-out record is updated where the owner's decision supersedes its reason.
