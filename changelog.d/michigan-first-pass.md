Michigan added: 5 statutes from the Legislature's per-act PDFs (the Management and Budget Act, 1984 PA 431, the enabling act of DTMB and the CIO; the Cyber Civilian Corps Act, 2017 PA 132; the Identity Theft Protection Act, 2004 PA 452, whose breach notice binds agencies; the Social Security Number Privacy Act, 2004 PA 454; the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act, 2000 PA 305, with DTMB's role for state agencies) and 7 executive orders from the Legislature's archive (1994-15, 1995-10, 2001-3, 2002-14, 2005-2, 2006-19, 2009-55 creating DTMB); 4 stubs (Executive Orders 2016-24 Enterprise Information Management and 2022-11 Center for Data and Analytics, whose PDFs carry only a machine-OCR text layer that failed the figure bar; the DTMB policy library and the Governor's orders page, both on michigan.gov, which answers HTTP 403 to every request of the Fetcher); 3 watch entries (the Legislature's executive order and executive reorganization order lists, LARA's list of all administrative rule sets). No administrative rule on IT exists in the Michigan Administrative Code list. The DTMB IT policy library could not be read, so the state's policies and standards (including any AI guidance) are not in the corpus; the left-out record says what was found instead and what a reviewer must add.
