Florida added: 1 statute (Chapter 282, Florida Statutes, the whole chapter from the Legislature's "View Entire Chapter" page: the Florida Digital Service and the State Cybersecurity Act, with Parts II and III riding along), 1 executive order (22-216, Strengthening Florida Cybersecurity Against Foreign Adversaries), 3 Florida Digital Service documents as policies (the Prohibited Applications on Government Devices List of April 30, 2026, the Rule Chapter 60GG-1 FAQ and the Net Present Value guide); 6 Florida Administrative Code chapters (60GG-1 to 60GG-5 and 60FF-3) as stubs because flrules.org serves each chapter only as a legacy Word .doc and nothing on the build machine reads one; 10 watch entries (six chapter pages, two division pages, the Governor's all-years order list, the digital.fl.gov sitemap). The Governor's order list was read for every year (its default view shows 2026 only); the Florida DOGE order 25-44 expired on March 31, 2026 and is left out. New state module src/states/florida.py reads the chapter page and the Governor's order number line.
