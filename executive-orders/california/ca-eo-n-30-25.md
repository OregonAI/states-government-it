## Full text

                      EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT
                        STATE OF CALIFORNIA

                            EXECUTIVE ORDER N-30-25

        WHEREAS California is the 4th largest economy in the world and the home
of Silicon Valley, which is synonymous with bold innovations that have improved
daily life and business models worldwide; and

      WHEREAS thousands of California startups, technology companies, and
cloud-service providers have ushered in a new age of digital services that has
made business faster, more dynamic, and better able to deliver seamless
customer experiences; and

       WHEREAS this revolution in digital services has created higher customer
expectations for Californians that federal, state and local government meet
their needs as quickly and intuitively as private sector businesses do; and

       WHEREAS California is a leader in Generative Al (GenAI) testing and
solution development, with 32 out of 50 of the top Al companies headquartered
in California, all benefiting from the deep well of talent and our globally
recognized innovation ecosystem; and

      WHEREAS California state government is advancing public sector use of
advanced analytics and data science through the work of departments such as
the California Department of Technology (CDT), the Center for Data Insights and
Information (CDII}, and the Office of Data Innovation (ODI}; and

      WHEREAS I have prioritized efforts to innovate and improve efficiency in
the delivery of governmental services by agencies and departments within my
Administration, including:

   • During my first week in office, I issued Executive Order N-4-19, which
     established a new procurement process known as the Request for
     Innovative Ideas (RFl2) to solicit proposals from the private sector to solve
     a problem identified by a state agency, with the first RFl2 focused on
     addressing the State's wildfire crisis and resulting in the delivery of wildfire
     monitoring cameras across the state that were operational for the 2020
     wildfire season;
   • In 2019, newly appointed leadership at the Department of Motor Vehicles
     (DMV) overhauled department practices via a DMV Strike Team to
     manage a surge in customer wait times related to the implementation of
     the Federal REAL ID Act, and since that time, DMV has expanded online
     services, created a virtual field office, added a credit card payment
     option, and developed a mobile driver's license option, while reducing
     and maintaining wait times at consistently low levels;
   • In partnership with the Legislature, I established ODI in 2019 as an office of
     technology experts dedicated to supporting other state agencies and
     departments to utilize data, technology, and principles of human
     centered design that are common in the private sector to improve the
     delivery of services to Californians; and
   • The California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CalFIRE) has
     developed and deployed cutting-edge technology to model wildfire risks
     and support wildland firefighting, including:
         o developing sophisticated data modeling to predict geographic
            areas where risk of fire starts is greatest and incorporating this
            information into the prepositioning approval process;

o partnering with the U.S. Department of Defense to use satellites for
wildfire detection;

o investing in LIDAR technology to create detailed 3D maps of high-
risk areas, which helps firefighters better understand and navigate
complex terrains;

o leveraging Al-powered tools to identify fires faster and developing
the Fire Integrated Real-Time Intelligence System (FIRIS) to provide
real-time mapping and modeling of wildfires;

o doubling deployment of drones for critical tasks like aerial ignition
during prescribed burns, wildfire containment, and real-time
assessments; and

o expanding capabilities for incident reporting at fire.ca.gov,
updating the incident map with near real time information about
firefighting aircraft activity, 3-D maps with evacuation orders, local
shelter information, road closures, and more;

e In 2023, 1 issued Executive Order N-12-23, which directed the Government
Operations Agency, the California Department of General Services, and
the CDT to update the state’s project approval, procurement, and
contracting processes for GenAl; CDT recently updated its statewide
policies, including safeguards required by my Executive Order to ensure
the responsible use of GenAl; and CDT also implemented a Project
Delivery Lifecycle (PDL) to update the state’s project approval process for
GenAl projects to meet the requirements of my Executive Order, offering
opportunities to expand that new procurement process to non-GenAl
projects; and

e The California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) has
streamlined processes and harnessed technology to drastically improve
efficiency, improving revenue collection on existing taxes while using
fewer resources, cutting the department's cost to administer a dollar of
revenue by more than 24 percent since 2019; and

WHEREAS the efforts to date are important, but state government can and
should do more to meet Californians’ high expectations; and

WHEREAS this transformation will require re-engineering state government
with a focus on human-centered design and user experience; data collection
to establish or confirm baselines and transparently track progress; and the
subject matter expertise of program leaders and frontline staff who serve
Californians daily; and

WHEREAS California led the nation in safely testing and implementing
GenaAl in the public sector, harnessing the power of transformative new
technologies to drive efficiency and effectiveness at scale; and

WHEREAS California has recently launched a first-in-the-nation deliberative
democracy program, Engaged California, to hear directly from Californians and
to inform policymakers about the issues that affect them the most; and

WHEREAS California, endeavors to attract, retain, and develop employees
who will support a modern and responsive state government and provide an
excellent customer experience to all Californians, and our state workforce has
deep expertise in the programs they run that can inform improvements to how
the State delivers services to Californians.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, GAVIN NEWSOM, Governor of the State of California,
in accordance with the authority vested in me by the State Constitution and
statutes of the State of California, do hereby issue the following Order to
become effective immediately:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED THAT:

1. The Government Operations Agency (GovOps) shall work with the
Department of Human Resources (CalHR), the Department of
General Services (DGS), the Department of Technology (CDT), and
the State Personnel Board (SPB) to improve operational
effectiveness, boost efficiency, and enhance customer experience,
enabling innovation in delivery of services and prioritizing at least
the following areas:

a. CalHR, in consultation with SPB, shall identify options to
streamline and modernize civil service hiring, modernize state
exams, and remove or improve administrative processes that
are unnecessary to meet legal requirements under civil
service and other applicable rules.

b. CDT shall identify options to simplify IT project approval and IT
procurement, develop shared IT contracting vehicles to
reduce costs, and establish an enterprise marketplace for
business tools, including tools utilizing artificial intelligence.

c. DGS shall identify options to further streamline procurement
practices to ease administrative burdens on both state
agencies and potential vendors, expedite necessary
contracts, and lower costs, including assessing changes to
streamline the approval process for Inter-Agency
Agreements.

GovOps shall provide an update to the Governor's Office on
implementation of this Paragraph, including any recommendations
to pursue options identified by the departments, no later than
August 15, 2025.

2. Each agency within my Administration shall, within 90 days of this
Order, submit to the Governor’s Office at least one proposal for a
no-cost or low-cost idea to improve efficiency of operations within
the agency or a constituent department, board, or office within the
agency.

3. The Office of Data Innovation (ODI), in partnership with CalHR, shall
leverage the Engaged California deliberative democracy platform
to engage the state workforce to source innovative ideas to
improve efficiency, effectiveness, and customer engagement in
state programs. ODI shall launch a pilot project pursuant to this
Paragraph no later than August 15, 2025.

4. ODI shall establish the Innovation Fellows Program (IFP) to create a
sustainable framework for incorporating a focus on innovation
around efficient and effective delivery of services in state
government, as follows:

a. The IFP will be comprised of “Innovation Fellows,” supervisors
and exempt staff selected from departments and agencies,
who will focus on fixing unique challenges facing their
departments and agencies through innovative ideas to solve
those challenges. The Fellows will receive training and support
through the IFP, including from the other Fellows to identify

the challenge, develop ideas to address it, and present the
recommendation to agency or departmental leadership, as
appropriate, for potential adoption.

b. Department executives shall support the IFP by granting
identified Innovation Fellows the opportunity to participate in
the IFP on a dedicated basis, independent of their other
duties.

c. ODI shall invite each agency within my Administration to
select at least one person to participate in the initial cohort of
Innovation Fellows and launch that cohort no later than
August 15, 2025.

All State agencies subject to my authority shall, and all other public
entities are requested to, assist ODI in administering the IFP.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that, as soon as hereafter possible, this Order be
filed in the Office of the Secretary of State and that widespread publicity and
notice be given of this Order.

This Order is not intended to, and does not, create any rights or benefits,
substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity, against the State of
California, its agencies, departments, entities, officers, employees, or any other
person.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF | have
hereunto set my hand and caused
the Great Seal of the State of
California to be affixed this 15th day
of July 2025.

GAVIN NEWSOM
Governor of California

ATTEST:

YARLEY N EBER, PH. D
ecretary of Stafe
