# No. 166: Providing for the establishment of an electronic data processing center and planning unit in the Executive Office for Administration and Finance (Executive Order No. 166)

## At a glance

Directs the consolidation of state data-processing functions in an Administration and Finance unit with a users' advisory group (Mass Register No. 171, 08/10/1979). The page prints no rescission. Evidence that it is overtaken: EO 588 (08/26/2019) recites that Chapter 64 of the Acts of 2017 reorganized the Commonwealth's information technology function under EOTSS, and orders 510, 532 and 549 restructured the same function after 166 (the A&F data-processing unit it created is not named by any later order read); OWNER DECISION whether it is still in force in substance or is replaced by statute and moves to left_out.

## Full text

Executive Order
No. 166: Providing for the establishment of an electronic data processing center and planning unit in the Executive Office for Administration and Finance
Date:
08/10/1979
Issuer:
Edward J. King
Mass Register:
No. 171
WHEREAS, the Governor's Management Task Force, established by my Executive Order No. 152 to conduct a detailed management survey of the administration of state government operations and services, has recommended that a new office be created which would improve the management and operations of EDP functions and planning; and
WHEREAS, it is the policy of this administration to promote efficiency and cost-effectiveness in the administration of the public's business; and
WHEREAS, the State Government has a special responsibility to ensure that in the performance of requisite services and functions, planning and oversight are employed to ensure that the public's funds are spent in a responsible manner; and
WHEREAS, the Commonwealth expends significant sums each year in EDP-related procurements; and
WHEREAS, I have determined that consolidation of the data-processing functions of the Administration and Finance Computer Services Center and the Department of Public Welfare Office of Management Services, would result in economy and efficiency in the delivery of data-processing services, and is consistent with the goals and policies set-out herein; and
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Edward J. King, Governor of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, acting under the authority vested in me by the Constitution and Statutes of the Commonwealth, do hereby adopt this order and direct that this order be recognized and complied with by the Executive Branch of the Government.
I hereby direct the Commissioner of Administration, as my executive officer for administrative, policy and planning functions within the Executive Department, to establish, within the Executive Office for Administration and Finance, an administrative unit for the purpose of performing EDP policy-making and planning functions, and to manage and operate the data center which I hereby direct to be created from the consolidation of the Administration and Finance Computer Services Center and the Department of Public Welfare Office of Management Services.
To facilitate the consolidation ordered above, I direct the Commissioner of the Executive Office for Administration and Finance, the Secretary of the Executive Office of Human Services, and the Commissioner of Public Welfare, to cooperate in the drafting of an interagency agreement, to be entered into without delay, accomplishing the above consolidation. The agreement shall include, but not be limited to, assignment of Department of Public Welfare personnel and appropriate re- sources to Administration and Finance; payroll and other record-keeping functions, and record- keeping functions necessary to ensure federal reimbursement.
To ensure the continued satisfactory performance of necessary data processing services, the administrative unit established shall include provisions for a User's Advisory Group, to advise in the areas of operations priorities and policies; security; scheduling; and systems development priorities.
Edward J. King
Governor
Michael Joseph Connolly
Secretary of State
