# Banning Tencent for State Government (Executive Order 2023-06)

## At a glance

Governor Noem's order barring state employees, agencies and state contractors from downloading or using any application or website owned or controlled by Tencent Holdings, including WeChat, on government devices or state-owned or state-leased equipment, and directing the Commissioner of the Bureau of Information and Telecommunications to designate other applications, websites and services detrimental to state security (consistent with Executive Order 2022-10) and to keep a public list of them. The Commissioner's designations are the authority for later bans of other applications. In force: not among the orders rescinded by Executive Order 2025-08.
