# Integrating Information Technology Resources and Creating an Enterprise Information Technology Governance (Executive Order No. 3-2025)

## At a glance

Governor's order integrating executive branch IT under the Department of Administration and the State CIO: overall responsibility for management and operation of all IT resources of executive branch agencies (exempting the Department of Justice, Secretary of State, University System, State Auditor, Office of Public Instruction and Public Service Commission), IT staff functionally reporting to the State CIO, a hiring freeze on IT positions unless the State CIO approves, a central Integrated Management Office, budget alignment, and agency access to IT inventories, systems, data and contracts. Effective July 1, 2025 until amended or rescinded.

## Full text

                                  STATE OF MONTANA
                               OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR
                              EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 3-2025

EXECUTIVE ORDER INTEGRATING INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY RESOURCES
AND CREATING AN ENTERPRISE INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY GOVERNANCE

WHEREAS, the State of Montana invests significant resources in information technology (IT)
to improve government services, protect individual privacy, and enhance the quality of life for its
citizens;

WHEREAS, effective use of information technology requires organized, deliberate, and cost­
effective management to ensure efficient use of public funds and maximize value for Montanans;

WHEREAS, centralizing IT governance, management, and support under the Department of
Administration and the State Information Technology Services Division will streamline planning,
procurement, security, and support, reduce duplication, and enable standardization across
executive agencies;

WHEREAS, standardizing IT tools, policies, and security practices reduces risk, strengthens
cybersecurity, and improves service delivery to citizens;

WHEREAS, integrated IT support creates economies of scale, improves accountability, and
allows agencies to focus resources on core services rather than IT administration;

WHEREAS, an enterprise approach to IT enables better information sharing, system
compatibility, and coordinated investment, while enhancing the overall security posture of the
State;

WHEREAS, the Department ofAdministration and the State Chief Information Officer are
responsible for setting IT strategy, overseeing implementation, and ensuring compliance with
state law and policy; and

WHEREAS, an integrated IT strategy will improve organizational and operational efficiency,
streamline data collection and sharing, and enhance the security of State of Montana information
systems.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, GREG GIANFORTE, Governor of the State of Montana, by virtue of
the authority vested in me by the Constitution and the laws of the State of Montana, do hereby
INTEGRATE INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY RESOURCES, and order as follows:

   1. The Montana Department of Administration, State Information Technology Services
      Division ("DOA/SITSD") led by the State Chief Information Officer ("State CIO"), has
      overall responsibility for the management and operation of all IT resources for all
      Executive Branch Agencies. The Department of Justice, the Secretary of State, the
      University System, the State Auditor, the Office of Public Instruction, and the Public

   Service Commission are exempt from this executive order. The CIO, with the approval of
   the Governor, may exempt other agencies from this executive order.

2. DOA/SITSD has the authority to make enterprise decisions regarding restructuring and
   operational matters related to IT integration, delivery of shared services, IT project
   management, and other responsibilities for Executive Branch Agencies within the scope
   of this executive order.

   •   All Executive Branch Agency personnel engaged in IT functions, including project
       managers and business analysts, are directed to functionally report to the State CIO.
   •   Day-to-day IT operations remain temporarily within agencies; however, all strategic
       direction, hiring decisions, and major IT decisions must be coordinated through the
       State CIO.
   •   There is a hiring freeze on all IT positions, unless approved through the State CIO.
       The State CIO, with approval from the Governor, has the authority to end the hiring
       freeze.
   •   DOA/SITSD is authorized to:
       o Direct IT integration activities across agencies,
       o Restructure IT delivery models,
       o Transition and reclassify positions across the enterprise,
       o DOA will oversee all human resources policy application, decision-making, and
           related activities, and
       o Consolidate IT contracts and infrastructure.
       o Deliver quarterly progress updates to the Governor's Office, including:
               • Integrated Management Office (IMO) project plan and project milestones
               • Stakeholder engagement and change management
               • Budget impacts and the development of a new funding model

3. A centralized IMO created under DOA/SITSD shall operate as follows:

   •   DOA/SITSD shall appoint members to the IMO, including:
       o Agency representatives,
       o A member representing the State Procurement Division,
       o A member of DOA Human Resources,
       o A member of State Human Resources, and
       o A member from Office of Budget and Planning.
   •   The IMO is responsible for:
       o Coordinating cross-agency teams,
       o Leading the multi-phase IT integration effort,
       o Developing implementation plans,
       o Establish the governance framework, and
       o Ensuring minimal disruption to services.

4. The Office of Budget and Program Planning, based on DOA/SITSD recommendations
   and approval from Governor, shall:

   •   Align budget authority to support integration,

       •   Create or move positions from Executive Branch Agencies to DOA/SITSD in support
           of this Executive Order.
       •   Withhold or reallocate IT-related appropriations as necessary to achieve the objectives
           of this Executive Order.

   5. All Executive Branch Agencies' full cooperation is mandated to take all steps necessary
      to implement this Executive Order, including:

       •   All Executive Branch Agencies must provide full access to IT inventories, systems,
           personnel, data, and contracts to DOA/SITSD.
       •   Agencies are expected to support DOA/SITSD AND IMO-led assessments, planning
           efforts, and implementation phases of this Executive Order.

Exempt agencies are strongly encouraged to implement this Executive Order.

This Executive Order shall be implemented consistently with applicable law. This Executive
Order is not intended to, and does not create, any right or benefit, substantive or procedural,
enforceable at law or in equity by any party against the State of Montana, its departments,
agencies, or entities, its officers, employees, or agents, or any other person.

This Executive Order shall take effect on July 1, 2025, and shall remain in effect unless further
amended or rescinded by the Governor.

                                              the State of Montana this '16 day of June 2025.
