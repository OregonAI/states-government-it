    # 20-02              Major IT Development Process (MITDP) Procedures

Purpose: Establishes procedures for system development plans, system implementation, and oversight of Major IT
Development Projects (MITDP), for appropriations made to an MITDP; requires that Units comply with standards
adopted by the Department of Information Technology (Department); and sets forth that standards for these MITDP
procedures may be amended from time to time, as deemed necessary by the Department.

Policy Statement: The Maryland legislature has established a MITDP program. The Department Secretary reviews
technology development projects and, based upon certain criteria designated in statute, may designate a project
as an MITDP. The Secretary is responsible for both oversight of the implementation of the MITDP projects
and the administration of the MITDP fund. For each MITDP, a covered Unit must submit an ITPR that details,
among other things, the budget request for the MITDP. When appropriated, funds for MITDPs are designated as
such, and general funds are appropriated in the MITDP fund. The Department and covered Units are required
to follow all statutory requirements associated with MITDPs. The Department has adopted an MITDP
standards manual that governs the administration of MITDPs. Covered Units are required to comply with this
MITDP standards manual.

Applicable Law & Other Policy: MD State Finance and Procurement Code Ann. § 3A-301-309. The
Department’s “State of Maryland Re-Baselining Policy and Process”, “State of Maryland Independent Verification and
Validation (IV & amp; V) Policy and Process”, and “State of Maryland Agile System Development Lifecycle (SDLC) Policy”
are discontinued and are superseded by this Policy.

Scope and Responsibilities: All executive branch units of state government, except those identified in Maryland
Code, SF&P § 3A-302. Agency executives, managers and staff shall ensure compliance with this policy.

Key Terms:
Department of Information Technology (DoIT): An executive branch unit of Maryland state government, organized
according to Maryland Code, State Finance and Procurement Article, § 3A.
Information Technology Project Request (ITPR): A request submitted by a covered Unit to the Department regarding a
proposed information technology project for major IT requests.
Major Information Technology Development Project (MITDP): Any technology development project meeting the criteria
as established in Maryland Code, SF&amp;P Article, § 3A-301.
Policy: A statement of jurisdiction and methods to guide agencies in the management of IT resources and services.
Units: All executive branch units of state government, except those identified in Maryland Code, SF&P § 3A-302.
