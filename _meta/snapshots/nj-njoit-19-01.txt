                                                                Policy Circular
Enterprise Apple Developer Account                                            19-01-NJOIT
PURPOSE

This Circular outlines the process for Executive Branch departments and State agencies to follow
to upload applications to the Apple Store exclusively to the centralized State of New Jersey's
Enterprise iOS Developer Account

SCOPE

All Executive Branch departments and State agencies (Agencies)are directed to cooperate fully
with the NJOIT and the CTO to implement the provisions of the Policy, and to ensure effective
use of information technology within the Executive Branch of State Government.

POLICY

The Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and The New Jersey Office of Information Technology
(NJOIT) have created an enterprise Apple Store account for the centralized submission and
upload of applications. Centralized submission ensures that the State of New Jersey is
designated as the publisher for applications developed or Agencies by contractors, as well as
applications developed by Agency teams themselves.

Use of NJOIT's centralized enterprise Apple account does not transfer ownership of the
application to NJOIT. The Agency or its contractor(s) remain solely responsible for the content of
the application, As well as the upgrading, monitoring, and maintenance of the application.
While the Apple Developer Agreement does not permit contractors (Service Providers) to
upload applications directly to the Apple Store on behalf of the State, contractors may still
maintain and administer the applications consistent with the terms and conditions in the Apple
Developer Agreement.

PROCEDURE

The Director of the Department of the Treasury, Division of Purchase and Property (Director) has
authorized and delegated acceptance of the Apple Developer Agreement terms and conditions

to the CTO Under the authorization and delegated authority from the Director, the CTO has
created and maintains the centralized enterprise Apple Store account for the Executive Branch.

1.     Agencies may submit applications for upload to the State's Apple Store account once
       the Agency determines that the application is adequately tested, complete, and in
       compliance with the Apple Developer Agreement terms .and conditions, as well as the
       corresponding Documentation and Program Requirements, as these terms are defined
       in the Apple Developer Agreement. The Apple Developer Agreement, Documentation,
       and Program Requirements are available upon request by email to the address set
       forth below.

2.     NJOIT may approve requests from Agencies either to establish user accounts in the
       enterprise Apple account for Agency, or for contractor management of applications
       and/or for the submission of new applications. Agency requests should specify the role
       and permissions required for each user account, following the principle of least
       privilege. Agencies are solely responsible for managing their approved users, as well.as
       the content submitted under authorized user accounts.

3.     Any costs incurred to the enterprise Apple Store account shaII be reimbursed to NJOIT
       by the Agency incurring the cost.

4.     All existing published applications must be transferred to this new centralized
       enterprise Apple Store account.

RELATED FORMS AND DOCUMENTS

Contact sonj-apple-dev-admin@tech.nj.gov for a current copy of the Apple Developer
Agreement.

COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Exceptions

There are no exceptions to this Policy. Applications published to the Apple Store outside the of
the State of New Jersey' Enterprise iOS Developer Account may be rejected by Apple.

AUTHORITY

as "The Office of Information Technology Reorganization Act."
