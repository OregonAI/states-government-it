Name                    Operational Controls
                        Operational Controls are procedures implemented and executed by people, as
                        opposed to systems, to improve the security of a system or group of systems.
Description
                        They often require technical or specialized expertise and may rely upon
                        management activities as well as technical controls.
Rationale               Provides consistent controls to secure the enterprise.
                        Promotes standardization, structure, and consistent behavior;
Benefits
                        Defines responsibilities related to security operations.
