I. AUTHORITY

   Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

   The State of Idaho must continually embrace up-to-date computer networking practices
   and technology to receive maximum benefit and affordability of services. Wide Area
   Network (WAN) hardware and services are utilized by virtually every agency and
   represent a major part of the interagency connectivity.

III. DEFINITIONS

   There are no definitions for this policy.

IV. POLICY

   State agencies are required to use the Office of Information Technology Services (ITS)’s
   statewide hardware and service contracts for WAN services. State agencies shall share
   systems and services whenever determined feasible and cost effective by the Office of
   Information Technology Services (ITS) in conjunction with ITA.

   The Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) shall establish a review and
   approval process for installing, upgrading, or enhancing the State’s Wide Area Network.
   The Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) must approve final design
   specifications, connectivity design, and implementation for the State’s Wide Area
   Network.

VII. CONTACT INFORMATION

   For more information, contact the ITA Staff at (208) 605-4064.

VIII. INTENT

   The Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) shall coordinate and manage for all
   agencies and institutions statewide:

   1. Provisioning of WAN hardware, software, and service contracts;

   2. Preventive and corrective maintenance; and

   3. Reconfiguration of the WAN.
