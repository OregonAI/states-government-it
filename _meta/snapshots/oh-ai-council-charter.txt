State of Ohio Artificial Intelligence (AI)
           Council Charter
GENERATIVE AI GOVERNING BODY
AI Council - January 2024

I. PURPOSE

The multi-agency Artificial Intelligence (AI) Council will provide statewide oversight for the
use of Generative AI solutions. In addition, the council will implement the state’s
governance framework for Generative AI solutions and serve in an advisory capacity to
the Governor’s Office, the DAS Director, and the State CIO.

II. SCOPE AND OBJECTIVES

Scope: The AI Council will facilitate Generative AI solution development efforts within the
State of Ohio. The multi-agency council will work towards innovation and collaboration while
ensuring responsible implementation and continuous monitoring of the solutions in
production.
Objectives: The AI Council has the following objectives:
  1. Ensure the State of Ohio is a leader in responsible and accountable Generative AI.
  2. Guide state agencies in the ethical, transparent, and unbiased use of AI solutions.

III. WORK PRODUCTS

AI Council work products include the following:
      1. Examining the social, economic, and legal impacts of AI adoption on the
         workforce, Ohioans, and business operations.
      2. Defining the legal requirements for the use of third-party AI services, contracts,
         licenses, agreements, and specific AI solution use cases.
      3. Establishing the framework for evaluating and authorizing the use of AI
         technology (e.g., architecture frameworks, software, infrastructure, and relevant
         tools).
      4. Directing the establishment of and the ongoing use of a statewide sandbox
         environment to safely explore the use of Generative AI to enhance the experience
         of the workforce and Ohioans.
      5. Developing and maintaining a statewide central repository that captures approved
         Generative AI use cases.
      6. Documenting protocols and procedures for assessing and handling inquiries or
         incidents regarding AI system anomalies.
      7. Auditing AI current and future solutions to ensure alignment with statewide policy
         requirements.
