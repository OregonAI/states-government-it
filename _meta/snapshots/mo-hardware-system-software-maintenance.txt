Name                       Hardware and System Software Maintenance
                           Hardware and System Software Maintenance controls are used to monitor the
                           installation of, configuration and updates to, hardware and software to ensure
Description                that the system functions as expected and that a historical record is
                           maintained of changes.
                           Implementing hardware and system software maintenance controls
Rationale                  contributes to systems functioning as intended, in a secure manner.
                              •   Establishes a change management system and managerial approval to
                                  document:
                                        o    Who will perform maintenance and repair activities
                                        o    Who performed maintenance and repair activities
                                        o    When maintenance and repair will be preformed
                                        o    Procedures for performance of emergency repair and
Benefits                                     maintenance
                                        o    The monitoring of installations and updates to hardware and
                                             software
                                        o    Prevents unexpected changes that may cause security breaches

                              •   Establishes management of hardware and system software warranties
                                  and upgrade policies
