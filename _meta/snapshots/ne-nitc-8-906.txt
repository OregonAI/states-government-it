                                                                               Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-906. Data destruction.
    Agency data must be disposed of in accordance with the Records Management Act and any
related records retention schedule. Disclosure of sensitive information through careless disposal
or re-use of equipment presents a risk to the state. Formal procedures must be established to
minimize this risk. Storage devices such as hard disk drives, paper or other storage media (e.g.,
tape, diskette, CDs, DVDs, USB drives, cell phones, and memory sticks) regardless of physical
form or format containing HIGH IMPACT or MODERATE IMPACT information must be
physically destroyed or securely overwritten when the data contained on the device is to be
disposed. These events should include certificates of destruction. State and agency asset
management records must be updated to reflect the current location and status of physical assets
(e.g., in service, returned to inventory, removed from inventory, destroyed) when any significant
change occurs.
