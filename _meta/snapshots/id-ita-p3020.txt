I. AUTHORITY

   Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

   Idaho State government must be able to reliably, and economically communicate across
   agencies and with Idaho citizens to conduct State business. A unified, statewide
   communications network provides for the basic infrastructure allowing State agencies to
   do business with each other, other local and federal government agencies, and the public.

III. DEFINITIONS

   There are no definitions for this policy.

IV. POLICY

   All State agencies shall adopt compatible network protocols for connectivity,
   interoperability, and data sharing. Additionally, State agencies shall develop an
   architecture that allows for highly available and high performing communications that is
   common across all agencies. Current and future network implementations and
   acquisitions shall also be based on standard network connections and protocols.

   ITA endorses the Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) for the
   standard used on the statewide network. New information technology will be acquired,
   developed, and implemented to conform to this standard.
