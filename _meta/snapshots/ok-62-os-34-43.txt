§62-34.43. Detailed information technology budgets
     A. All agencies of the state shall submit to the Chief Information Officer of the Office
of Management and Enterprise Services their detailed information technology budgets for
approval.

     B. All disbursements made from treasury funds by any state agency for the above-
named expenses shall be budgeted to and disbursed from a special allotment account to
be created by the Director of the Office of Management and Enterprise Services.

agency data processing costs for cost allocation purposes.
