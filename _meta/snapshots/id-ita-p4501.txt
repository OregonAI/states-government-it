I. AUTHORITY

Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

This policy is designed to minimize vulnerabilities to agencies’ information and information
assets by mandating the security principles of least privilege and least functionality.

III. DEFINITIONS

   See ITA Guideline G105 (ITA Glossary of Terms) for definitions.

IV. POLICY

   Agencies shall implement the security principles of least privilege and least functionality
   within their agency operations.

V. EXEMPTION PROCESS

   Refer to ITA Policy P1010 (Information Technology Policies, Standards, and Guidelines
   Framework).
