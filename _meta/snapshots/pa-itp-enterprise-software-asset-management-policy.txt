1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This document replaces, in its entirety, the Asset Management Policy, dated January 6, 2025.

3. Purpose
This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the policy and practices for the efficient
management of Commonwealth Enterprise Software.

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

   5.1 Licensing Assignments
   Commonwealth employees and contracted resources shall be issued the version of software
   license necessary based on their job duties and requirements. Office of Administration, Office
   for Information Technology (OA/IT) shall maintain guidance for commonly used Enterprise
   Software that outlines the license or type of license that shall be assigned to users by default.
   This guidance is available in the Software Licensing Guideline.

   If the guidance in the Software Licensing Guideline is inconsistent with any contractual

   agreements, the provisions of the contractual agreement shall take precedence.

   5.2 Software License Reclamation
   OA/IT shall conduct regular reviews of Enterprise Software license utilization to identify
   instances where the software is unused or underutilized. Once identified, any software that meets
   the criteria for being unused or underutilized shall have the software uninstalled or the license
   unassigned. These licenses shall then be returned to the License Pool and reassigned to other
   users requiring that software.

   If a user determines that a reclaimed software license is needed, they shall submit a request
   to have the software reinstalled, or the license reassigned via the Enterprise IT Service
   Management tool. The request shall include justification as to why they require the
   software.

   A list of Enterprise Software that is subject to the license reclamation process, along with the
   criteria for each to be considered unused or underutilized, and the actions to be taken are
   available in Enterprise Software Reclamation Standard.

   5.3 Enterprise License Procurement
   Only OA/IT shall purchase licenses that are available on Centralized Licensing Agreements
   (CLA) and charge agencies for use of licenses that are included in the agreement . Agencies
   shall be charged based on their assignment or consumption of the licenses in accordance
   with a chargeback methodology, such as Shared Service Apportionment (SSA) or other
   chargeback methods as approved by the Financial Management Oversight Committee
   (FMOC).

   A listing of the current CLAs, the products available, agencies they apply to, and the
   onboarding processes, is set forth in the Software Licensing Guideline.

6. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

7. Exception from Policy
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this policy, a request for a policy
exemption shall be submitted via the enterprise IT Policy Exemption process. Refer to IT
Policy Governance Policy for guidance.
