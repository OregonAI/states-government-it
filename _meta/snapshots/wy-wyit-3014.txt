                             PERSONNEL SECURITY
                                           WyIT - 3014

 PURPOSE: Ensure that personnel security safeguards are applied to the access and
 use of information technology resources and data.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

IV.    POSITION RISK DESIGNATION

       All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

       ● Assign a risk designation to all positions.
       ● Establish screening criteria for individuals filling those positions.
       ● Review and update position risk designations annually.

 V.    PERSONNEL SCREENING

       All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

                                                                                  WyIT - 3014     Page 1

        ● Screen individuals prior to authorizing access to the information systems.
        ● Rescreening of personnel should not exceed a 10-years period, unless otherwise
          required more frequently by additional governing requirements.
        ● Ensure personnel screening and rescreening activities reflect applicable state
          and federal laws, directives, regulations, policies, standards, guidance, and
          specific criteria established for the risk designations of assigned positions.

 VI.    PERSONNEL TRANSFER

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Review and confirm ongoing operational need for current logical and physical
          access authorizations to information systems/facilities when individuals are
          reassigned or transferred to other positions.

VII.    ACCESS AGREEMENTS

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Develop and document access agreements for information systems.
        ● Review and update the access agreements annually.
        ● Ensure that individuals requiring access to information and information systems:
          ○ Sign appropriate access agreements prior to being granted access.
          ○ Re-sign access agreements to maintain access to information systems when
             access agreements have been updated or annually.
        ● Access agreements include, for example, nondisclosure agreements, acceptable
          use agreements, rules of behavior, and conflict-of-interest agreements.
        ● Screen individuals prior to authorizing access to the information systems.
        ● Rescreen individuals every 5-years, unless otherwise required more frequently
          by additional governing requirements.
        ● Ensure personnel screening and rescreening activities reflect applicable state
          and federal laws, directives, regulations, policies, standards, guidance, and
          specific criteria established for the risk designations of assigned positions.

VIII.   PERSONNEL TERMINATION

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy, upon
        termination of individual employment shall:

                                                                                  WyIT - 3014     Page 2

    ●   Disable information system access immediately.
    ●   Terminate/revoke any authenticators/credentials associated with the individual.
    ●   Retrieve all security-related information system-related property.
    ●   Retain access to information and information systems formerly controlled by
        terminated.

REFERENCES: This policy has no supporting references to websites, other policies,
federal or state statutes.
