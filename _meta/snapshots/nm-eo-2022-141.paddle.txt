WHEREAS, the State of New Mexico faces increasingly sophisticated and malicious
cyber campaigns that threaten our State's security and privacy;
WHEREAS, the State is dedicated to identifying, deterring, and protecting against these
threats by investing in cyberinfrastructure and building a robust detection and response system to
protect the health and welfare of the people of this State; and
WHEREAS, a robust cyberinfrastructure requires cooperation between federal, state,
local, and tribal governments, as well as the private sector, to ensure swift, adaptive, and robust
action against any current or future cyber threat which may interrupt the delivery of essential
services, endanger the essential functions of government, or otherwise threaten protected or
personal data.
NOW, THEREFORE, I,Michelle Lujan Grisham, Governor of the State of New Mexico,
by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and the laws of the State of New Mexico
do hereby ORDER and DIRECT as follows:
1.
The New Mexico Cyber Security Planning Committee (the "Committee") is hereby
established. The Chair of the Committee shall be the Secretary of the Department of Information
Technology or his designee. The Committee shall consist of the following members appointed by
the Governor, who serve at the pleasure of the Governor:

2.
The New Mexico Department of Information Technology is designated as the State
Administrative Agency (“SAA”) for purposes of submitting the State and Local Cybersecurity
Grant Program (the “SLCGP”) application to the United States Department of Homeland Security
and the Federal Emergency Management Agency.
3.
The Chair shall convene, within 7 days of the date of the appointment of the last
required Committee member, the initial meeting of the Committee; establish a meeting schedule
sufficient for the Committee to timely complete its mandated tasks; establish Committee sub-
committees and working groups to facilitate completion of Committee tasks; and ensure
compliance with the Open Meetings Act, as necessary.
4.

bodies;
iv.
