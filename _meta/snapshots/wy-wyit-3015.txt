                                  PHYSICAL MEDIA
                                           WyIT - 3015

 PURPOSE: This policy ensures that Information Technology (IT) controls access to and
 disposes of media resources in compliance with IT security policies, standards, and
 procedures.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by the WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

IV.    MEDIA ACCESS

       All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

       ● Restrict access to digital media storage devices to authorized users with a valid
         need to know.
       ● Mark information system media indicating the distribution limitations, handling
         caveats, and applicable security markings of digital and non-digital information
         media.

  V.    MEDIA STORAGE

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Specify that staff will physically control and securely store media within defined
          controlled areas.
        ● Protect information system media until the media are destroyed or sanitized
          using approved equipment, techniques, and procedures.

 VI.    MEDIA TRANSPORT

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Protect and control media during transport outside of controlled areas.
        ● Maintain accountability for information system media during transport outside
          of controlled areas.
        ● Document activities associated with the transport of information system media.
        ● Restrict the activities associated with the transport of information system media
          to authorized personnel.

VII.    MEDIA SANITIZATION

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Sanitize prior to disposal, release out of organizational control, or release for
          reuse in accordance with applicable federal and organizational standards and
          policies.
        ● Employ sanitization mechanisms with the strength and integrity commensurate
          with the security category or classification of the information.

VIII.   MEDIA USE

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Prohibit the use of information system media on affiliate-owned equipment
          using unapproved security safeguards.
