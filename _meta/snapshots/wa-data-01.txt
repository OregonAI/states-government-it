                                     OPEN DATA PROGRAM
See Also:
RCW 43.105.054 WaTech Governance
RCW 43.105.205 (3) Higher Ed
RCW 43.105.020 (22) “State agency”

1. Funded through the state Enterprise Data Management allocation, WaTech will
   provide the state Open Data Program, which will:

        a. Procure and maintain the state’s open data portal at https://data.wa.gov, which:

               i. Each agency uses at no additional cost as their own open data portal to
                  share and access category one public data, metadata, visualizations and
                  narratives about data.

               ii. Supports diverse usability needs for the public, business, and other
                   partners who reuse agencies’ shared data.

               iii. Supports agencies’ diverse open data sharing needs.

               iv. May also serve as the open data portal for small local governments,
                   independent library districts, and tribal governments with a presence within
                   Washington state boundaries and that do not already have an open data
                   portal.

        b. Facilitate public and other entities’ open data access and use at no additional
           cost to agencies, the public, or other entities.

        c. Promote the state’s open data collection.

        d. Convene an Open Data Advisory Group made up of agency and community
           representatives, which will:

               i. Manage and guide the development of open data policy in the state of
                  Washington.

               ii. Share and establish best practices for government open data.

      e. Consult with agencies at no additional cost to prioritize datasets for publication
         and improve open data publication practices.

      f. Include Agency Open Data Advisors on all open data communications with their
         respective agencies, except where otherwise agreed.

      g. Provide a collaboration and document-sharing space where all Agency Open
         Data Advisors can share knowledge and best practices.

      h. Provide open data news and resources via the collaboration space.

      i. Facilitate engaging and valuable Open Data Advisory Group meetings.

      j. Partner with the geospatial data community, advise that spatial open data be
         published on https://geo.wa.gov and fiscal open data be published on
         https://fiscal.wa.gov, and will work to make data on all of those sites findable on
         https://data.wa.gov.

2. Agencies, as defined in POL-01, will make all data that is already published online and
   that the public would reasonably expect to find on the state open data portal
   discoverable through the state open data portal at https://data.wa.gov by, at a
   minimum, cataloging it as external links.

      a. This policy does not require agencies to make any additional data publicly
         available.

      b. Agencies will use the Public Data Discoverability Guidance to identify what data
         must be discoverable, and for recommendations on how to meet the
         discoverability deadline.

      c. Agencies with already published data that is not discoverable through
         https://data.wa.gov will make progress by October 1 of each year beginning
         October 1, 2027, as follows:

            i. By October 1, 2027, publish a list of all already published open data that is
               not discoverable at https://data.wa.gov, https://geo.wa.gov, or
               https://fiscal.wa.gov.

            ii. By October 1 of each following year, make at least five (5) additional items
                on the list discoverable through https://data.wa.gov until all items on the
                list are discoverable, with a goal of achieving completion no later than
                October 1, 2033.

                                               2

      d. Agencies may consult with the Open Data Program about what data to make
         discoverable on https://data.wa.gov and how to do so most efficiently with
         limited resources.

3. Agencies own and will maintain their content on https://data.wa.gov.

      a. Agencies will annually review their open data content for quality, such as
         accuracy, currency, and completeness.

      b. Agencies will follow the Data Removal Procedure for any content they remove
         from https://data.wa.gov.

4. Agencies will designate one or more employees with agency enterprise level
   responsibility as the agency’s Open Data Advisor(s), and will:

      a. Use the Agency Open Data Advisor Selection and Support Guidance as needed.

      b. Include their agency’s Open Data Advisor(s) in processes and discussions about
         open data.

      c. Support their agency’s Open Data Advisor(s) in advancing open data publication
         across the agency.

      d. Make, reconfirm, or update their Agency Open Data Advisor designation
         annually using the annual policy compliance survey starting October 1, 2026.

5. Agency Open Data Advisors will:

      a. Participate in the Open Data Advisory Group as described in the charter.

      b. Improve and document their agency’s open data publication process.

      c. Advise the Open Data Program on how to support their agency.

      d. Authorize and audit their agency’s open data portal publication accounts.

      e. Serve as the Open Data Program’s primary contact for questions about the
         agency’s open data and potential open data.

      f. Inform agency staff and leadership about open data policies, standards, and
         guidelines.
