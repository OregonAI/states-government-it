1.0   PURPOSE
      This document contains the current list of technologies, including hardware, software, vendors,
      and services, that are banned from use in the State of Nevada due to security concerns.

2.0   SCOPE
      This Banned Technology list applies to all state agencies and authorized users meeting the
      criteria identified in the State Information Security Program Policy, Section 1.2, Scope and
      Applicability.

3.0   EFFECTIVE DATES
      This standard becomes effective at the time of approval by the State Information Security
      Committee (SISC).

4.0   RESPONSIBILITIES
      The agency head and appointed Information Security Officer (ISO) have the responsibility to
      ensure the implementation of and compliance with this list and its associated standard.

5.0   RELATED DOCUMENTS
      State Information Security Program Policy, 100
      State Security Standard S.6.02.07 – Technology Bans

      6.0.2     BANNED BY FEDERAL GOVERNMENT

                All vendors, manufacturers and products banned for use by US Federal Government
                agencies will be included by default on this list.
      6.0.3     BANNED BY STATE POLICY

                Products from vendors or manufactures owned in whole or part by foreign governments
                of concern, or in which a foreign government of concern has significant control, will be
                included on this list by default.

7.0   DEFINITIONS
      Foreign Government of Concern – a foreign government listed by the US Department of State as
      a Country of Particular Concern or a Special Watch List Country

8.0   RESOURCES

9.0   EXCEPTIONS
      Requests for exception to the requirements of this list must be documented, provided to the OIS,
      and approved by the State CISO.
