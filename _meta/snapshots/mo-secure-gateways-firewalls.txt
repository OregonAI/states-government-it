Name                       Secure Gateways and Firewalls
                           Secure gateways and firewalls are devices or systems that control the flow of
                           traffic between networks employing differing security postures. There are
                           several types of secure gateways and firewalls, ranging from boundary routers
Description
                           that can provide access control, to even more powerful systems that can filter
                           on the content of the traffic.

                           Secure gateways and firewalls are standard equipment for Internet and
                           boundary connections, and protect sites from exploitation of inherent
                           vulnerabilities. Additionally, they help mitigate problems associated with
Rationale
                           unsecured systems and the problems inherent in providing robust security for
                           large numbers of systems.

                           •   Secure gateway and firewall technologies can integrate with other security
                               and intrusion detection systems.
                           •   Secure gateways can scan for viruses and malicious code in electronic mail
Benefits                       and web pages.
                           •   Secured gateways and firewalls are used to restrict connectivity to and
                               from internal networks servicing more sensitive functions.
