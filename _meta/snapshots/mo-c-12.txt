I.     General Statement

       Access to the Internet is provided to Missouri state employees as a research and
       communication tool for conducting official state business. The Office of
       Administration acquires access to the Internet through the Missouri Research
       and Education Network (MOREnet) and is therefore required to abide by the
       MOREnet Acceptable Use Policy.

II.    Guidelines

       A.   Office of Administration employees may use the Internet for state
            government related business.

       B.   Internet services are to be considered a state resource and should be
            treated as such. Employees are expected to exercise good judgement in
            the use of Internet services and to comply with Executive Order No. 92-04,
            the Office of Administration Policy C-5, the provisions of Chapter 105,
            RSMo, and any other provisions of state or federal law relating to ethics in
            government service.

       C.   Office of Administration employees are to abide by the MOREnet
            Acceptable Use Policy.

III.   MOREnet Acceptable Use Policy

       A.   The primary mission of MOREnet is to provide collaborative networked
            information services for educational, research, public service, economic
            development and government purposes to its members and those they
            serve.

       B.   All network use by MOREnet members, project participants and those
            connected via MOREnet members or project participants shall be for, or in
            support of, research, education, local, state, or national government affairs,
            economic development, or public service.

       C.   It is not acceptable to use MOREnet for purposes which violate any federal
            law or state law.

       D.   It is not acceptable to use MOREnet for any purpose which violates
            Copyright.

      E.   It is not acceptable to use MOREnet in a manner that is harmful or
           harassing to others.

      F.   It is not acceptable to use MOREnet in a manner that intentionally or
           negligently disrupts normal network use and service. Such disruption
           would include the intentional or negligent propagation of computer viruses,
           the violation of personal privacy, and the unauthorized access to protected
           and private network resources.

      G.   It is not acceptable to use MOREnet for commercial activities, that are not
           in support of education, research, public service, economic development or
           government purposes. Further, it is not acceptable to distribute unsolicited
           advertising. Additional information regarding unacceptable commercial
           uses from MOREnet is available.

      H.   Each MOREnet member or project participant must make reasonable
           efforts to publicize the Internet Acceptable Use Policy of MOREnet and to
           ensure compliance of those connected through them. Reported and
           perceived violations of the Acceptable Use Policy will be reviewed by the
           MOREnet executive Director. Violations that are not promptly remedied by
           the member institution or project participant may result in action including
           the termination of MOREnet service or the forfeiture of MOREnet
           membership.
