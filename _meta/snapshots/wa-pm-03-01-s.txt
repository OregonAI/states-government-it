Project Quality Assurance Policy

1. WaTech categorizes all major projects under oversight as Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier
   3 projects based on complexity and risk assessment. The agencies are
   recommended to evaluate project complexity, overall project risk and define
   minimum experience and qualification criteria for the QA vendor, such as:

         a. Added project management and/or quality assurance experience based on
            overall project risk level. Higher risk projects would benefit from seasoned,
            experienced resources.

         b. Previous public sector project management experience on multiple large-
            scale projects.

         c. Subject matter expertise related to the project.

         d. Prior experience with the development methodology proposed by the
            project.

2. For Tier 3 projects, the designated lead QA provider must have:
      a. Minimum seven years of recent (within the past 10 years) experience
         successfully performing the QA activities outlined in the Minimum Project
         Quality Assurance Activities Standard on a project with $10M or more total
         project planned spend, OR

         b. A combination of prior QA or PM experience totaling 10 or more years in
            the past 15 years that include:
                i. Minimum three years of recent (within the past five years) experience
                   successfully performing the QA activities outlined in the Minimum
                   Project Quality Assurance Activities Standard on a project with $10M
                   or more total project planned spend.

                  ii. Any experience as a lead or deputy project manager providing
                      successful project management services on information technology

                (IT) projects of similar or greater size, risk, and complexity when
                compared to the project being undertaken.

3. For Tier 2 projects, the designated lead QA provider must have:
      a. Minimum six years of recent (within the past 10 years) experience
         successfully performing the QA activities outlined in the Minimum Project
         Quality Assurance Activities Standard on a project with $2M or more total
         project planned spend, OR
      b. A combination of prior QA or PM experience totaling six or more years in
         the past 10 years that includes:
              i. Experience successfully performing the QA activities outlined in the
                 Minimum Project Quality Assurance Activities Standard on a project
                 with $2M or more total project planned spend, AND
             ii. Experience as a lead or deputy project manager providing successful
                 project management services on information technology (IT) projects
                 of similar or greater size, risk, and complexity when compared to the
                 project being undertaken.

4. For Tier 1 projects, the designated lead QA provider must have:
       a. Minimum five years of recent (within the past 10 years) experience
          successfully performing the QA activities outlined in the Minimum Project
          Quality Assurance Activities Standard on a project with up to $2M total
          project planned spend, OR
       b. Minimum five years of recent (within the past ten years) experience as a
          lead or deputy project manager providing the successful project
          management services on an information technology (IT) project of similar or
          greater size, risk, and complexity when compared to the project being
          undertaken.

       c. A combination of prior QA or PM experience totaling five or more years in
          the past 10 years.
