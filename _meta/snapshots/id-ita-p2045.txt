I. AUTHORITY

   Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

   This policy requires state agencies to adopt a risk management program strategy to
   manage risks to the State of Idaho that result from threats to the confidentiality,
   integrity and availability of agency data and information systems.

III. DEFINITIONS

   See ITA Guideline G105 (Glossary of Terms) for any definitions.

IV. POLICY

   All agencies shall establish and maintain a Risk Management Program that
   ensures:
        1. Completion of risk and security assessments for all information systems and
           infrastructure
        2. Implementation of ongoing processes to evaluate and monitor the current
           security posture of the environment

   Assessment Requirements

   Risk assessments must be conducted for the following:
       1. Cybersecurity threats
       2. Planned agency projects, at key stages:
             a. Concept phase
             b. Design phase
             c. Pre-launch (Go-live)
       3. Emerging risks or areas of concerns identified by the agency
       4. Audit findings requiring risk evaluation

   Risk Response and Documentation

   For each identified risk:
       1. The risk must be formally acknowledged by the designated responsible parties
          or accountable stakeholders
       2. It must be documented in the agency’s risk register or equivalent system
       3. A risk response strategy must be selected and recorded (e.g., mitigate,
          transfer, accept, or avoid)

   The date of each assessment must be included in the agency’s Annual IT Security
   Plan.

V. EXEMPTION PROCESS

   Refer to ITA Policy P1010 (Information Technology Policies, Standards, and
