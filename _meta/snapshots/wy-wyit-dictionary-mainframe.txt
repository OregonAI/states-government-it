                       ‭Mainframe Specific Definitions‬

‭ URPOSE:‬‭This document provides a list of words and‬‭definitions to clarify the‬
P
‭mainframe-specific terminology contained within the State of Wyoming Policies and‬
 ‭Standards.‬

 ‭I.‬ ‭APPLICABILITY‬

      ‭ his policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff‬
      T
      ‭(collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,‬
       ‭affiliates, and temporary employees.‬

 ‭II.‬ ‭MAINFRAME SPECIFIC DEFINITIONS‬

‭A‬
      ‭●‬ ‭Agency‬‭Liaison‬‭:‬‭Designated‬‭mainframe‬‭security‬‭liaisons‬‭who‬‭manage‬‭mainframe‬
           ‭access for personnel accessing their respective agencies’ data.‬

‭E‬
      ‭●‬ ‭ETS‬‭Liaison‬‭:‬‭The‬‭designated‬‭mainframe‬‭security‬‭liaison‬‭at‬‭ETS.‬‭This‬‭person‬‭also‬
           ‭manages access for agency liaisons.‬

‭F‬
      ‭●‬ ‭F541‬‭:‬ ‭Mainframe‬ ‭forms‬ ‭used‬ ‭to‬ ‭create,‬ ‭change,‬ ‭delete,‬ ‭and‬ ‭acknowledge‬
           ‭mainframe user accounts.‬

      ‭●‬ ‭Invalid‬‭login‬‭actions/repercussions:‬ ‭See‬‭sections‬‭IV.I.1,‬‭Login‬‭Banner,‬‭and‬‭IV.L.3,‬
           ‭Reviews for Detection of Unauthorized Access.‬

      ‭●‬ ‭ISV‬ ‭Software‬‭:‬ ‭Independent‬ ‭Software‬ ‭Vendor‬ ‭Software.‬ ‭Non-IBM‬ ‭systems‬
           ‭software that supports mainframe operations.‬
                                                                       ‭Mainframe Specific Definitions      Page‬‭1‬

‭M‬
      ‭●‬ ‭Mainframe‬ ‭security‬ ‭liaison:‬ ‭A‬ ‭person‬ ‭appointed‬ ‭by‬ ‭an‬ ‭agency‬ ‭director‬ ‭to‬
           ‭perform‬‭mainframe‬‭security‬‭access‬‭duties,‬‭i.e.,‬‭submit‬‭F541‬‭forms‬‭for‬‭adding‬‭and‬
            ‭user‬ ‭access‬ ‭and‬ ‭resetting‬ ‭passwords.‬ ‭In‬ ‭this‬ ‭document,‬ ‭mainframe‬ ‭security‬
             ‭liaisons may also be referred to as security liaisons or liaisons.‬

      ‭●‬ ‭Message‬ ‭of‬ ‭the‬ ‭Day:‬ ‭This‬ ‭term‬ ‭is‬‭not‬‭used‬‭by‬‭this‬‭document.‬‭See‬‭section‬‭IV.I.1,‬
           ‭Login Banner.‬

‭P‬
      ‭●‬ ‭Password‬‭requirements‬‭:‬ ‭Standards‬‭for‬‭password‬‭creation.‬‭(‬‭WyIT‬‭-‬‭3024,‬‭Section‬
           ‭XI‬‭)‬

‭R‬
      ‭●‬ ‭Resource Access Control Facility (RACF)‬‭: IBM’s mainframe‬‭security software.‬

      ‭●‬ ‭Remote‬ ‭access‬ ‭allowances:‬ ‭Who‬ ‭is‬ ‭allowed‬ ‭to‬ ‭access‬ ‭the‬‭mainframe‬‭remotely.‬
           ‭(‭W
              ‬ yIT - 3024, Section VII‬‭)‬

‭S‬
      ‭●‬ ‭Session‬ ‭Lock‬ ‭requirements:‬ ‭Inactivity‬ ‭that‬ ‭locks‬ ‭a‬ ‭user’s‬ ‭session.‬ ‭See‬ ‭section‬
           ‭IV.G.2.‬

‭T‬

      ‭●‬ ‭Technical‬‭authentication‬‭process:‬ ‭How‬‭access‬‭permissions‬‭are‬‭determined.‬‭See‬
           ‭sections IV.F.3, IV.G.1.a, IV.H.1‬

‭U‬
      ‭●‬ ‭Usage restrictions:‬‭See Session Lock requirements,‬‭section IV.G.2.‬
