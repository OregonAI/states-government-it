1.       PURPOSE
         The purpose of this policy is to ensure that New Jersey Executive Branch Agencies’ Web or
         online services contain appropriate disclaimer statements.

2.       SCOPE
         This policy applies to all public Websites of State agencies in the Executive branch of
         Government other than independent State agencies that operate Internet Web sites as a public
         service.

3.       DEFINITIONS
             Independent State Agencies are authorities, broads, commissions and other agencies of the
              State of New Jersey, which are not subject to the policy supervision and control of the
              Governor. An example of an independent State agency would be the New Jersey Transit
              Authority.

             An Intrusion detection system gathers and analyzes information from various areas within
              a computer or a network to identify possible security breaches, which include both
              intrusions (attacks from outside the organizations) and misuse (attacks from within the
              organizations.)

             Online is the condition of being connected to a computer or a telecommunications system.
              For the purposes of this policy, the term is used to describe a connection to the Internet.

             A Web site is a collection of HTML (Hypertext Markup Language) files on a particular subject
              that includes a beginning file called a home page. For example, most companies,
              organizations, or individuals that have Web sites publish a single address. This is their home
              page address. From the home page, you can get to all the other pages on their site.

4.         POLICY AND PROCEDURES
           All New Jersey Executive Branch Agencies that engage in online activities or electronic
           commerce should provide user with statements disclaiming legal liability from damages
           resulting from the access of agency Web site, statements disclaiming liability pertaining to links
           to other sites, statements disclaiming liability for erroneous or outdated information and
           disclosure statements regarding intrusion detection systems.

           In addition, the disclaimer statement should make clear to users that the information provided
           is on an “as is” basis and the user assumes the risk when relying on such information.

           The statements shall be developed in consultation with agency legal counsel. Links to an
           agency’s disclaimer statements shall be displayed on the home page of all agencies subject to
           this policy.

5.         ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
           5.1       Agency Senior Management

                     5.1.1     Designate a person or persons to ensure compliance with this policy.

           5.2       Compliance

                     5.2.1     Agencies shall comply with this policy within 90 days of its effective date.
