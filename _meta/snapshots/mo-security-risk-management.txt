                                                       DEFINITION
Name                     Security Risk Management
                         Security Risk Management is a four-step process of framing, assessing, responding to
                         and monitoring risk. This allows agency managers to balance the operational and
                         economic costs of protective measures and achieve gains in mission capability by
                         protecting the agency systems and data that support their mission.
                         Risk framing establishes the context and provides a common perspective on how
                         organizations manage risk.
                         Risk assessment includes identification and evaluation of risks, risk impacts, and
                         recommendations for risk-reducing measures.
Description
                         Risk response provides a consistent, agency-wide, response to risk in accordance with
                         their risk framework.
                         Risk monitoring is maintaining ongoing awareness of the agencies’ risk environment, risk
                         management program, and associated activities to support risk decisions.
                         Risk management is ongoing and evolving, which emphasizes the need for continuing risk
                         evaluations and assessments. The system owner determines whether the remaining risk
                         is at an acceptable level or whether additional security controls should be implemented.
                         Security Risk Management ensures that appropriate, cost-effective safeguards are
Rationale                incorporated into existing and new installations of agency systems.
                         Security Risk Management helps to ensure the balance of risks, vulnerabilities, threats,
Benefits                 countermeasures, and available resources to achieve an acceptable risk level based on
                         the sensitivity or criticality of the individual systems.
