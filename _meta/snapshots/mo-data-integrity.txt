                                                        DEFINITION
Name                     Data Integrity
                         Data Integrity is the assurance that information is unchanged from its source, and has not
Description              been accidentally or maliciously modified, altered or destroyed.
                         Data integrity controls prevent, limit and deter authorized and unauthorized users from
Rationale                deliberately or accidentally making erroneous modifications to or the destruction of
                         sensitive and critical information.
                              •   Data integrity controls provide assurance to the user the information meets
Benefits                          expectations about its quality and integrity.
