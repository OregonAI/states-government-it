       COMPUTER SECURITY THREAT RESPONSE
                                       WyIT - 3009

 PURPOSE: This policy defines the CIOs and affiliate designated security liaisons'
 responsibility in responding to security threats affecting the confidentiality, integrity,
 and/or availability of IT resources.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by the WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

 IV.    COMPUTER EMERGENCY RESPONSE

        ● A Cyber Computer Emergency Response Team (CCERT) shall be established.
          The CCERT shall be led by the CIO or their equivalent if not available.

        ● The CCERT shall consist of representatives from all departments.

        ● The CCERT shall communicate security information and guidelines for
          notification processes, identify potential security risks, and coordinate
          responses to thwart, mitigate, or eliminate security threats to IT resources.

● Upon the activation of CCERT by the CIO, all applicable affiliate security
  liaisons and other CCERT representatives shall report directly to the CIO for
  the duration of the CCERT activation.

● Each affiliate shall establish and implement Affiliate Cyber Computer
  Emergency Response Procedures that consist of the following, at minimum:
  ○ Creating an incident response policy and plan.
  ○ Developing procedures for performing incident handling and reporting.
  ○ Setting guidelines for communicating with outside parties regarding
     incidents.
  ○ Selecting a team structure and staffing mode.
  ○ Establishing relationships and lines of communication between the incident
     response team and other groups, both internal (e.g., legal department) and
     external (e.g., law enforcement agencies).
  ○ Determining what services the incident response team shall provide.
  ○ Staffing and training the incident response team.

● The CCERT shall develop a notification process to ensure appropriate
  notification within the State in response to IT security incidents.

● The CCERT responsible for taking the necessary corrective action to
  remediate IT security incidents. Such action shall include all necessary steps to
  preserve evidence in order to facilitate the discovery, investigation, and
  prosecution of crimes.

● Each affiliate shall provide the CIO with contact information for its primary and
  secondary security liaisons, including, without limitation, after-hours, and
  immediately notify the CIO of any changes to that information.

● Each affiliate shall maintain current contact information for all personnel who
  are important for the response to security threats to IT resources and/or the
  remediation of security incidents.

● Each affiliate shall provide its primary and secondary security liaisons with
  adequate portable communication devices (e.g., cell phones and pagers).
