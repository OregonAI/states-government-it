Executive Order 01.01.2007.26
Information Technology
A. Chief information officers of all principal departments, agencies, and units shall cooperate with the Chief of Information Technology to look for opportunities to streamline information technology projects across State government, achieve cost-savings through economies of scale, consolidate enterprise-wide services where appropriate, and coordinate on security and disaster recovery planning.
B. The Chief of Information Technology shall participate directly in meetings of the Governor's cabinet and all subcabinet meetings involving technology matters.
C. The Chief of Information Technology shall participate directly in the StateStat process.
Administrative History
Effective date: November 30, 2007 (34:26 Md. R. 2249)
