I. AUTHORITY

   Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

    All agency IT projects are subject to review by the Office of Information Technology
   Services (ITS) in accordance with applicable statutes, budget processes, and statewide
   policies.

   In support of the Idaho Technology Authority’s statutory responsibility under Idaho Code
   § 67-833 to review Large-Scale information technology projects, this policy establishes
   that certain projects with significant statewide financial, operational, security, or public
   service impact require additional review and approval by the ITA prior to proceeding.

   Enterprise impact thresholds and screening criteria used to identify such projects will be
   defined and maintained within ITS policies and standards to ensure consistent,
   objective, and statewide application. Projects meeting these established thresholds will
   be referred to the ITA for formal review.

   For these selected information technology and telecommunications projects, State
   agencies will be required to submit a detailed project plan and make a formal
   presentation to ITA for review and approval. Upon approval, a periodic project status
   report will be required. Any changes in scope, technologies, funding, legislative
   mandates, and other factors will be submitted to ITA for review as they occur.

   This policy establishes a structured framework for reviewing and approving these
   projects to ensure significant IT investments align with statutory requirements, strategic
   goals, and recognized project management best practices. Agencies must submit a
   detailed project plan, risk assessment, and justification demonstrating alignment with
   agency and statewide priorities.

   The goal of this policy is to promote transparency, accountability, and the successful
   delivery of IT projects that support mission-critical operations and public service
   outcomes.

III. DEFINITIONS

   See ITA Guideline G105 (Glossary of Terms) for any definitions.

IV. POLICY

   1. Project Profile Submission
      Upon request by the ITA, agencies must submit project profiles for specified IT
      projects. These profiles will be evaluated for compatibility with statewide IT and
      telecommunications plans, as well as ITA policies and standards.
          a. Project profiles must be submitted to: ITS Approvals Form
          b. Refer to ITA Guideline G210 (IT Project Profile) for submission requirements.
   2. Project Selection for Comprehensive Review
      Based on the submitted profiles, the ITA may select certain projects for a formal,
      comprehensive review. Agencies will be formally notified if their project is selected.
   3. Comprehensive Project Plan and Presentation
      Agencies with selected projects must submit a detailed project plan that includes:
          a. Full project scope and objectives
          b. Risk assessment
          c. Cost-benefit analysis
          d. Total cost of ownership
      Agencies will be scheduled to present the project plan to the ITA for review and
      approval. Presentations must demonstrate sound project management practices,
      demonstrate agency due diligence and address questions outlined in ITA Guideline
      G230 (IT Project Best Practices Checklist).

      Agencies must account for the full lifecycle costs associated with the proposed IT
      initiative. This includes both the initial acquisition and implementation expenses, as
      well as all ongoing costs incurred throughout the operational lifespan of the solution –
      including maintenance, support, licensing, upgrades, staffing, and decommissioning.
      The total cost of ownership should reflect a realistic projection of financial
      commitments necessary to sustain the solution and achieve its intended outcomes.
   4. Ongoing Project Reporting
      Agency directors must submit periodic status reports at intervals established by the
      ITA. Reports must include:
           a. Progress against the original plan

V. EXEMPTION PROCESS

   Refer to ITA Policy P1010 (Information Technology Policies, Standards, and Guidelines).
