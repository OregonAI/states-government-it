8-104. Policy exception process.
    This policy establishes the controls and activities necessary to appropriately protect
information and information technology resources. While every exception to a policy or standard
weakens the protection for state IT resources and underlying data, it is recognized that at times
business requirements dictate a need for temporary policy exceptions. In the event an agency
believes it needs an exception to this policy, the agency may request an exemption by following
the procedure outlined in section 1-103.
