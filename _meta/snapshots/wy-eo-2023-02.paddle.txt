EXECUTIVE ORDER
Order 2023-02
ESTABLISHMENT OF THE WYOMING CYBER ASSISTANCE RESPONSE
EFFORT TEAM
WHEREAS, due to the increasing involvement of technology in daily life, cybersecurity
threats have become increasingly common and sophisticated, posing a significant risk to
the security and well-being of Wyoming citizens and critical infrastructure; and
WHEREAS, cyber-attacks on Wyoming's government agencies, businesses, critical
infrastructure, and people can result in the theft of sensitive information, the disruption of
essential services, and financial losses; and
WHEREAS, these cyber-attacks can have severe consequences for Wyoming's economy
and public safety, as well as potentially compromising national security; and
WHEREAS, there are sixteen Critical Infrastructure Sectors, as defined by Presidential
Policy Directive/PPD-21, whose systems, networks, and other assets are vital to the State
of Wyoming's economy, safety, and well-being; and
WHEREAS, any impairment or destruction of these assets, would have severe
consequences for Wyoming's security, economic stability, public health, and safety; and
WHEREAS, it is essential that Wyoming take proactive measures to prevent and
respond to cybersecurity incidents; and
WHEREAS, maintaining cybersecurity is a shared responsibility in which everyone has
a critical role to play; and
WHEREAS, effective prevention and responsiveness necessitates that state agencies,
local governments, tribal governments, the private sector, academic institutions, and other

WHEREAS, it is vital to marshal the resources and expertise needed to combat these

NOW, THEREFORE, pursuant to the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
laws of the State of Wyoming, I , Governor Mark Gordon, do hereby order and direct:
1. The Wyoming Cyber Assistance Response Effort (CARE) Team is hereby
established.
2. The mission of the CARE Team is to protect Wyoming and its institutions by
reducing the impacts of cyber-related events, including incidents and disruptions,
through prevention, response, and recovery. This Executive Order provides a
framework that enables the state emergency management and information
technology to work seamlessly with state, local, tribal, private sector, and other
relevant partners to rapidly respond to and minimize the impact of cyber events in
Wyoming.
3. Members of the CARE Team shall include, but are not limited to, at least one
representative from each of the following entities:
a. The Wyoming Office of the Chief Information Officer and Enterprise
Technology Services (ETS);
b. The Wyoming Information Analysis Team;
c. The Wyoming Division of Criminal Investigation's Computer Crime Team;
d. The Wyoming Office of Homeland Security; and
e. The Wyoming National Guard.
4. The Wyoming Office of Homeland Security (WOHS) shall lead the CARE Team
and shall determine the frequency of its meetings.

5. The CARE Team shall review cybersecurity and other technology-based threat
information and assess vulnerabilities, and present their findings and
recommendations for the protection of State resources to the Governor's Office.
6. The CARE Team shall continuously review, update, and implement the Wyoming
Cyber Disruption Response Plan. As updates to the plan are made, the CARE
Team shall submit the plan to the Governor's Office for review and approval. This
plan provides a common framework for identifying and responding to
technological threats that mirrors the federal government model, with
corresponding responses to address threats of increasing scope and severity.
7. All Wyoming state agencies shall report known or suspected cybersecurity
incidents to ETS immediately upon detection. ETS shall carry out the following
functions:
a. Pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Ann. §§ 9-2-2901 through -2906, ETS is
responsible for working with state agencies managing security risks. This
includes managing information security policies and standards, working
with agencies on technical matters, and ensuring enterprise projects meet
security requirements.
b. ETS, in cooperation with CARE Team members and other subject matter
experts, shall investigate all state agency reports of cybersecurity incidents.
c. ETS will decide on behalf of state agencies in determining the appropriate
reactive measures to a cyber event and shall regularly and promptly notify
the CARE Team members of such events, event details, and response
efforts.
8. The CARE Team will create partnerships that help protect Wyoming Critical
Infrastructure and Key Resources, as that term is defined by the United States
Department of Homeland Security. This includes the following actions:
a. The CARE Team shall seek to work and partner with relevant federal
agencies including the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), the
Cybersecurity Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), the Federal Bureau
