Enterprise Technology Strategy and Services Policy 09-01
Cell Phone and Mobile Broadband Devices
Last Revision: 4/21/2025                                                                    doa.entsec@doit.ri.gov

1. Purpose

Identify the process and procedures for the procurement and support of cellular telephones and mobile
broadband devices (commonly referred to as air cards or hotspots).
2. Applicability

This policy is applicable to all State of Rhode Island Executive Branch Departments1 (including agencies,
boards, and commissions), and their employees (including permanent, non-permanent, full-time, and part-
time) and interns, consultants, contractors, vendors, contracted individuals, and any entity having access
to state information systems and data, whether operated or maintained by the state or on behalf of the
state. For this policy, the term "agency" is used to refer to any department, agency, division, or unit of the
Executive branch of the State of Rhode Island.
    a. Unless otherwise specifically provided, the Telecommunications Section of the Division
       of Enterprise Technology Strategy and Services (ETSS) is responsible for the procurement,
       deployment, installation, synchronization, trouble resolution, and repair of all cellular
       telephones and mobile broadband devices as well as the training necessary to utilize the
       device.
    b. Only state procured and issued devices will be supported by ETSS.
    c. State issued cellular phones are not to be used as a replacement for a personal cellular
       phone. Further guidance is provided in this policy regarding reimbursement procedures.
       In accordance with Accounts and Control Policy A-69 (State-Issued Cellular Phones and
       other Mobile Devices), incidental and infrequent use for personal reasons is allowed
       provided specific requirements are met (see section 6, Personal Use, below).
3. Assignment
    a. The assignment of cellular telephones and mobile broadband devices will be based upon
       the functional requirements of an individual's position and shall be limited to the
       following areas as determined and authorized by the funding agency:
            i.    Crisis Management
           ii.    Emergency Management
          iii.    Productivity Requirements
          iv.     Communications

   b. All requests for the assignment of mobile broadband devices must be submitted in
      writing. The request form can be found at ETSS Telecommunications Request Form.
      The user should state reasons why each assignment is necessary and must be approved by
      both the requesting agency or department designee and agency Chief Financial Officer
      (CFO) and submitted to the ETSS service desk for processing.
   c. No individual shall be issued more than one (1) cellular telephone without a written
      request for exception submitted to the ETSS Chief Digital Officer (CDO) by the agency
      leadership designee and agency CFO.
4. Service and Support
   a. All requests for procurement, installation, synchronization, trouble resolution, repair or
      training for cellular phones must be submitted to the ETSS Service Desk. The service
      desk can be reached via the ETSS IT Service Desk portal: Service Portal - Enterprise
      Technology Strategy & Services.
   b. In accordance with the Master Price Agreement (MPA) for Cellular Telephone Services
      that has been approved by the Division of Purchases, only ETSS Telecommunications
      staff (Telecom staff) are authorized to deal directly with the vendors.
   c. Individuals are not authorized to bring their cellular telephone to any vendor service
      center for any reason without the prior approval of Telecomm staff.
   d. All cellular telephones and mobile broadband devices issued to employees are state
      property and as such must be returned to the ETSS Telecommunications team when no
      longer needed by the individual to whom it was assigned.
   e. Transfer of state issued cell phone numbers to a private phone is not authorized and will
      not be approved in any instance.
   f. The downloading and installation of third-party applications, e.g., Instant Messaging,
      Facebook, X (Formerly Twitter), Weather Bug etc., is not authorized and may be remotely
      removed upon detection. The setup of third party or personal accounts on state issued
      devices is not authorized. These will be removed when detected.
5. Security
   a. According to the rules and policies established by ETSS, applicable security settings will
      be configured at the time of issuance. Maintaining the security settings of all cellular
      telephones and mobile broadband devices is mandatory.
   b. All users are required to password protect their devices with a password consisting of at
      least 6 alphanumeric characters. Users are not authorized to turn off the password.
   c. All state issued mobile broadband devices, commonly referred to as hotspot devices, will
      be configured with WPA2 security at a minimum with an appropriate password to prevent
      unauthorized access.
6. Personal Use
   a. State issued cellular telephones are issued to individuals to permit them to perform the
      assigned duties in the most efficient manner.
   b. State issued cellular phones are not to be used as a replacement for a personal cellular
      phone. However, in accordance with Accounts and Control Policy A-69 (State-Issued Cellular
      Phones and other Mobile Devices) , incidental and infrequent use for personal reasons is
      allowed if the use is:

           i.   Infrequent, appropriate and short in duration
         ii.    Such that it does not involve added cost to the agency
         iii.   Such that it does not interfere with work
         iv.    In compliance with State Network Device Security Policy10-04

7. ETSS Procedure
   a. When the Telecomm Request Form is submitted to ETSS, the Telecomm staff will begin
      the procurement process.
   b. When the device is received, Telecom staff will contact the user to either pick up the
      device or plan to meet with the individual to perform the setup and/or configuration
      needed.
   c. All requests for repair or other types of support must also be submitted to the ETSS
      Service Desk.
   d. No one other than Telecom staff is authorized to provide any support for devices without the
      expressed approval of Telecom staff.
   e. Damaged or broken devices must be returned to the ETSS Telecommunications team, who will
      determine if the device can be repaired or will require replacement.
   f.   Prior to disposal of any device, all memory will be erased by the Telecom staff to ensure that any
        sensitive information is removed.
   g. For device upgrade requests, the user must enter a ticket into ETSS Service Desk Portal to request
      the upgrade. Telecomm verifies whether the device is eligible for upgrade and, if yes, sends the
      user a cost approval form which must be signed off by the agency CFO. The user then signs and
      sends the form back to Telecomm for processing.
   h. If a device is lost or stolen, the user should immediately contact Telecomm via email to the
      Service Desk (ent.servicedesk@ri.gov) to open the ticket to investigate.
   i. For device deactivation, a ticket should be opened with the service desk at
      ent.servicedesk@ri.gov requesting deactivation.
   j. For overseas travel requests, the user must open a Service Desk Ticket, search for
      “International” in the search box, select “ETSS Overseas / Out of Country Travel Request
      Form” from the search results, then complete and submit the required forms no less than 2
      weeks prior to traveling to ensure equipment configurations or requisition requirements
      can be met.
