Scope:
​
This policy establishes requirements for protecting state electronic data resources through proper data
destruction and sanitization procedures. It provides agencies with guidance on how to properly handle
data destruction and sanitization within the State of Wyoming, ensuring compliance with data privacy
and security regulations.

This policy addresses the secure disposal of electronic media containing state data to prevent
unauthorized access, data breaches, and compliance violations.

Authority
This policy is promulgated under the authority of Wyoming State Statute 9-2-2906 and its subsections,
the Department of Enterprise Technology Services is required to adopt and enforce information
technology standards, and promote efficient use of information technology throughout state
government.

Specifically, this policy is further promulgated under the authority of the below listed Wyoming State
Statute subsections.

   ●​ Implements and maintains an information technology governance program for state agencies
      (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (ii));
   ●​ Establishes and enforces data security and privacy policies and standards for the state data
      infrastructure Establishes review criteria for the agencies information technology projects,
      procurements and purchases. (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (iii)); and

                                                       ​
Adopts information technology policies and standards and ensures agency compliance with those
policies and standards (W.S. 9-2-2906,(b), (vi)).

Audience
This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff (collectively
referred to as "agencies"). This policy is also applicable to consultants, affiliates, third party vendors
and temporary employees that use or store state data.

Policy Statements:
 1.​   The agency may create agency-specific policies that meet the outlined scope, purpose, and
       requirements as stated in this policy. In lieu of agency-specific policies that meet the outlined
       scope, purpose, and requirements as stated in this policy, this policy shall serve as the
       governing policy.
       [1], [2], [3]

 2.​   Agencies may implement supplemental policies to augment this governing policy as needed.
       This policy does not supersede any State or Federal regulation and does not override, waive, or
       provide exemption from the requirements of the Wyoming Public Records Act (Wyoming Statute
       16-4-201 through 205). Agencies remain fully obligated to comply with all legal requirements
       while implementing appropriate privacy safeguards for exempt information.
       [1], [2], [3]

 3.​   The State CIO recognizes three levels of media sanitization:​

       3.1.​     Clear: Perform a full manufacturer's reset, reinstall or a format of the storage to either ​
                 restore the equipment to its factory default settings or simply format over the storage.​

       3.2.​     Purge: Use crypto shredding, wiping, or degaussing in an organizationally approved
                 degausser rated at a minimum for the media.​

       3.3.​     Destroy: Shred, disintegrate, pulverize, or incinerate the device in a licensed incinerator.
       [4], [2], [5], [6], [3]

 4.​   Agencies shall classify their data according to WyIT 2001 Information Classification levels and
       those levels shall determine disposal methods.
       [2], [8], [3]

 5.​   Public Data:​
       ​
       Media remaining within your agency (internal reuse, storage): The agency may choose to
       perform a clear sanitization (factory reset/format). Media transferred to another agency must be
       cleared.

                                                       2

                                                      ​
       5.1.​     Media remaining within your agency (internal reuse, storage): The agency may choose
                 to perform a clear (factory reset/format).Media transferred to another agency must
                 perform a clear.
       5.2.​     If the media leaves the State’s control, it must be purged. This can be done by the
                 agency, contracted services, ETS, or Surplus Property.
       [4], [2], [3]

 6.​   Internal Data:

       6.1.​     Media remaining within your agency (internal reuse, storage): The agency may choose
                 to perform a clear sanitization (factory reset/format).Media transferred to another agency
                 must perform a clear sanitization.

       6.2.​     If the media leaves the State’s control, it must be purged. This can be done by the
                 agency, contracted services, or ETS.

       6.3.​     If the media is not to be reused, it must be destroyed in a manner that renders the
                 electronic media unreadable and unusable.
       [4], [2], [5], [3]

 7.​   Confidential Data:

       7.1.​     If the media is not to be reused or is leaving the agency's control, it must be destroyed in
                 a manner that renders the electronic media unreadable and unusable.

       7.2.​     If the media is to be reused within the agency, it must be purged.
       [4], [2], [5], [6], [3]

 8.​   Agencies shall identify and comply with federal and state regulations where applicable as
       determined by the agency data owner.​
       [2], [7], [8], [3]

 9.​   When verification of destruction is required, agencies shall contract with qualified vendors to
       provide documented proof of media destruction completion. Such verification shall include
       certificates of destruction that detail the destruction method, date, location, and witness
       information.​
       [4], [2], [9], [3]

Compliance & Exceptions:
Compliance
Employees who violate this policy may be subject to appropriate disciplinary action up to and including
discharge as well as both civil and criminal penalties. Non-employees, including, without limitation,
contractors, may be subject to termination of contractual agreements, denial of access to IT resources,
and other actions as well as both civil and criminal penalties.

Exceptions
Requests for exceptions to this policy shall be reviewed by the Chief Information Security Officer
(CISO) and the Chief Information Officer (CIO). Departments requesting exceptions shall provide such
requests to the CIO in accordance with WyIT.3002 Policy Exception Request.

The request should specifically state the scope of the exception along with justification for granting the
exception, the potential impact or risk attendant upon granting the exception, risk mitigation measures
to be undertaken by the IT Department, initiatives, actions and a time-frame for achieving the minimum
compliance level with the policies set forth herein. The CIO shall review such requests; confer with the
requesting department.

Any exception to established policy shall be defined and tracked as a risk to the state and established
mitigating controls monitored by the agency.
