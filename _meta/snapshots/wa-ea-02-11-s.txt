1. PURPOSE
This standard establishes the statewide service capability, Mainframe, as an enterprise service
under EA-02 – Establishing an Enterprise Service Policy. WaTech’s Mainframe as a Service
(MFaaS) brokered model standardizes mainframe hosting and operational support, providing
state agencies with access to shared enterprise mainframe computing resources. This service
allows state agencies to take advantage of economies of scale and expertise in the
mainframe space.
In support of the Mainframe service, WaTech provides 24/7 operations and technical
support, high-availability workload processing, data storage/backup, disaster recovery,
capacity planning, reporting and performance management, software management, and
integration support.
A Washington state agency must not adopt, build, expand, or renew a service with
capabilities similar to those in Section 4 unless it has an approved waiver or exemption (see
Section 5).

2. SCOPE
In Scope
The Mainframe service must be used by agencies that require mainframe computing for
large-scale data processing. WaTech offers infrastructure-based system administration of the
Mainframe service.
Out of Scope
Responsibilities associated with applications hosted by agencies are not in scope of this
service, including:
     •   Development and maintenance of agency applications hosted on the Mainframe
     •   Optimization or performance tuning for agency applications hosted on the Mainframe
     •   Administration of agency applications hosted on the Mainframe

   •   Support of agency applications hosted on the Mainframe

3. APPLICABILITY
The scope of this standard applies to all state agencies (as defined in RCW 43.105.020).
This standard does not apply to the K-20 network, institutions of higher education,
legislature, judiciary, or local governments.

4. ENTERPRISE SERVICE CAPABILITIES
The Mainframe enterprise service designation encompasses the following capabilities:
   •   Mainframe infrastructure system administration
   •   Mainframe operations

5. WAIVERS AND EXEMPTIONS TO THIS STANDARD
In accordance with POL-01-02-S Technology Policy and Standard Waiver Request Standard,
agencies may qualify for a waiver from compliance with this standard. No categorical
exemptions apply.

6. AUTHORITY
This standard is issued under the authority of RCW 43.105.054, which grants Washington
Technology Solutions authority to establish statewide technology standards and enterprise
services. RCW 43.105.385 establishes Washington Technology Solutions as a central service
provider for state agencies, supporting the delivery of common enterprise technology
services. RCW 43.105.450 requires state agencies to comply with statewide information
technology security policies and standards.
