 Subject:

                              Strategic Direction for Information Technology Investments

 By Direction Of:                                                                           Date:

                                                                                                February 8, 2006
                         Joseph S. Martz, Secretary of Administration

                                   The Office of Administration, Office for Infor-
                                   mation Technology (OA/OIT) is responsible for
                                   the policy, guidelines, and standards govern-
                                   ing Information Technology (IT) investments by
                                   all agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction
                                   (enterprise), based on direct input and advice
                                   from Commonwealth agencies. This amend-
                                   ment recognizes "IT Bulletins" as having the
                                   same force and effect as a Management Direc-
                                   tive.

1. PURPOSE. To ensure that guidelines pertaining to the planning, management, and acquisition of IT
resources are governed by policy and performance.

2. SCOPE.           This directive applies to all agencies, boards, commissions, and councils under the Governor's
jurisdiction.

3. OBJECTIVES. To ensure the Governor's program priorities are achieved through an effective and proac-
tive approach, the OA/OIT has established the following objectives to:

    a. Leverage and maximize IT investments and the buying power of the Commonwealth to benefit the
enterprise and, where possible, Pennsylvania's schools, libraries, communities, businesses, and citizens.

    b. Develop and issue enterprise-wide "Open Systems" standards and interoperability policies.

    c. Develop acquisition priorities that support strategic, enterprise-wide investments in IT as a shared
resource, developed through a shared vision and shared usage.

    d. Facilitate, in partnership with agencies, the research, evaluation, and piloting of new technologies for
enterprise-wide strategic policies and applications, including opportunities for business process redesign.

4. POLICY. IT resources will be managed, planned, and acquired in accordance with the direction set forth
in Executive Order 2004-8 As Amended, Enterprise Information Technology Governance Board.                 •

                                                       (Office for Information Technology, OA, 717/787-5440) Page 1 of 2

5. RESPONSIBILITIES.

    a. The Secretary of Administration, in conjunction with the Deputy Secretary for Information Tech-
nology, shall develop, promulgate, and be the sole interpreter of all enterprise IT policy governing the
planning, management, and acquisition of IT resources.

    b. The Office of Administration, Office for Information Technology, shall be responsible for devel-
oping guidelines and standards for the planning, management, and acquisition of IT resources for all agen-
cies under the Governor's jurisdiction.

    c. Heads of agencies shall be responsible for effectively managing and maintaining their agency's IT
investments and to ensure procurements and contracts are executed in accordance with IT policy, guidelines,
and standards established by the OA/OlT through "IT Bulletins," which shall have the same force and effect as
a Management Directive.

     d. The Secretary of the Budget, through the Deputy Secretary for Comptroller Operations, shall
notify the Deputy Secretary for Information Technology of any IT related findings identified in agency and
enterprise audits.

     e. The Secretary of General Services, through the Deputy Secretary for Procurement, shall procure
IT related commodities and services based on Commonwealth IT policy, standards, and guidelines estab-
lished by the OA/OIT.

                              This directive replaces, in its entirety, Manage-
                              ment Directive 245.13 dated February 2, 1999.
