            SENDING ENCRYPTED EMAIL STANDARD
                                          WyIT -3001-S

 PURPOSE: This standard is to ensure the proper encryption standards are utilized
 when sending sensitive data via the State authorized email solution.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This standard applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards and commissions
       staff (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to
       consultants, affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       There are no exceptions to this standard.

IV.    REQUIREMENTS

            ● The OCIO has provided the ability to send encrypted emails outside of the
              wyo[.]gov domain. It’s required that emails and associated attachments
              containing, included but not limited to protected, sensitive, personally
              identifiable information (PII) and protected health information (PHI) utilize
              the encryption capable enterprise solution. The following is an example
              based on a new email creation in the State authorized solution:

policies, federal or state statutes.
