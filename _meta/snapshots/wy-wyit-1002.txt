       ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION MANAGEMENT
                                           WyIT - 1002

 PURPOSE: To ensure agencies managing internal IT infrastructure maintain an IT
 change management practice, minimize risk to agency business, and maintain
 compliance with statewide security policies, standards, and procedures.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

IV.    ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION CONTROLS

       A. Monitoring and Access

          ● The State of Wyoming:
            o Owns all electronic communication data and reserves the right to specify
               and control its use.
            o Reserves the right to inspect all email-related data at any time as
               authorized by statute or policy.
          ● Requests to access or monitor an employee’s email account:

                                                                               WyIT 1002          Page 1

           o Must have the approval of the Director or designee, and
           o Authorization of the Attorney General’s Office.

     B. Account Delegation

        ● Upon employee termination or an inter-agency transfer, delegated access to
          the employee’s email account may be granted to the employee’s successor
          or appropriate management as determined by the originating agency.
        ● If the originating agency denies a request to transfer an account, a new
          account will be created for the employee at the new agency following the
          naming convention standard.

     C. Official Records

        ● All documents created in, sent by, or attached to electronic mail may
          constitute official records of the State of Wyoming and may be subject to
          public information requests.
        ● To the extent that email constitutes a “record” for records management
          purposes, there may be State statutes or other policies affecting its use and
          maintenance.
        ● Email that has been identified in any court or regulatory proceedings as
          having a high likelihood of imminent litigation shall remain available for
          discovery until the legal hold has been removed. In all situations where this
          section applies, an email system administrator must be notified immediately.

     D. Retention of Public Records

        ● Email content created or received in the course of conducting State of
          Wyoming business will be retained and maintained in an alternative format as
          prescribed by state and agency records retention schedules.

     E. Revoking Access

        ● Access to the email system may be denied by:
          o The owning agency, or
          o ETS if determined a violation of policy or abuse has occurred.

V.   ACCEPTABLE USE

     A. Authorized Access

        Agencies may grant employees access to enterprise email and instant

                                                                    WyIT 1002             Page 2

         messaging to carry out assigned duties. Access for non-state employees shall
         be granted only with the approval of the agency director or designee.

      B. Personal Use

         ● The State of Wyoming’s email system is to be used primarily for legitimate
           state business purposes.
         ● Incidental personal use is acceptable if it does not unreasonably impact the
           employee’s work performance or the ability to conduct State of Wyoming
           business activities and does not compromise system security.

VI.   UNACCEPTABLE USE

      A. The following uses are prohibited:

         ●   Illegal Activities - Any illegal or wrongful conduct is prohibited. Any
             information or knowledge regarding illegal actions will be provided to the
             Attorney General, Division of Criminal Investigation, or appropriate law
             enforcement agency.
         ●   Intellectual Property Infringement - Inclusion of copyrighted material in email
             that violates copyright laws is prohibited.
         ●   Discrimination and Harassment - The use of the email and instant messaging
             system to transmit data that is disparaging or harassing to individuals or
             groups will not be tolerated. This includes but is not limited to, writings,
             drawings, jokes, or any other form of data that is degrading or harassing to
             others based on an individual’s race, religion, color, sex, national origin, age,
             or disability.
         ●   Insensitive or Profane Language - Users must not send messages containing
             offensive, derogatory, profane, or abusive language.
         ●   Objectionable Material - Users must not use the system to distribute
             pornography, malicious code, or illegal software.
         ●   Interference with System Operation - Any use that seriously and unduly
             affects system functionality is prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to,
             intentional misuse of group addresses, forwarding chain letters, sending
             SPAM messages, email bombs, initiating denial of service attacks, or other
             forms of cyber terrorism.
         ●   Use of Other Accounts - The use of another user’s account or intentionally
             falsifying an identity to send or receive communications (identity theft) is
             prohibited.
         ●   Personal Gain - The system may not be used for personal, commercial
             ventures, or other personal gains.

           political causes or endorsement of candidates is prohibited
