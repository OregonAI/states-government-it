                                   Compliance Component

                                                     DEFINITION
Name                   Risk Monitoring
                       Risk monitoring provides organizations with the means to:
                           (i) Verify compliance
Description                (ii) Determine the ongoing effectiveness of risk response measures
                           (iii) Identify risk-impacting changes to organizational information systems and
                                     environments of operation.
                       The agency employs risk monitoring tools, techniques, and procedures to increase risk
Rationale              awareness, helping senior leaders/executives develop a better understanding of the
                       ongoing risk to the state’s operations and assets.
                       Analyzing monitoring results gives organizations the capability to maintain awareness of the
Benefits                   risk being incurred, highlight the need to revisit other steps in the risk management
                           process, and initiate process improvement activities as needed.

                                                COMPLIANCE DETAIL
                                    1. Risk Monitoring Strategy - Develop a risk monitoring strategy for the
                                       agency that includes the purpose, type, and frequency of monitoring
                                       activities. Organizations implement risk monitoring programs by:
                                             a. Compliance Monitoring - Verify that required risk response
                                                measures are implemented and that information security
                                                requirements derived from and traceable to organizational
                                                missions/business functions, federal legislation, directives,
                                                regulations, policies, and standards/guidelines are satisfied.
State the Guideline, Standard                b. Effectiveness Monitoring - Determine the ongoing effectiveness
or Legislation                                  of risk response measures after the measures have been
                                                implemented.
                                             c. Change Monitoring - Identify changes to agency information
                                                systems and the environments in which the systems operate that
                                                may affect risk including changes in the feasibility of the ongoing
                                                implementation of risk response measures.
                                                      i. Information System - Changes can occur in agency
                                                         information systems (including hardware, software, and
                                                         firmware) that can introduce new risk or change existing

                                                        risk.
                                                    ii. Environments of Operation - The environments in which
                                                        information systems operate can also change in ways that
                                                        introduce new risk or change existing risk. Environmental
                                                        and operational considerations include, but are not limited
                                                        to, missions/business functions, threats, vulnerabilities,
                                                        mission/business processes, facilities, policies, legislation,
                                                        and technologies.
                                  2. Risk Monitoring - Monitor agency information systems and environments
                                     of operation on an ongoing basis to verify compliance, determine
                                     effectiveness of risk response measures, and identify changes. The
                                     particular aspects of monitoring that are performed are dictated largely by
                                     the assumptions, constraints, risk tolerance, and priorities/trade-offs
                                     established by organizations during the Risk Framing step (see CC – Risk
                                     Framework).
