Increase to Delegated Cost Thresholds

BACKGROUND

The California Technology Agency (Technology Agency) assigns each agency1 a delegated cost
threshold for project review and approval purposes. The Technology Agency delegates to the agency
the resource approval authority for any IT proposal with an estimated total development cost equal to or
less than the agency's assigned cost threshold, provided that the proposal does not meet any other
reporting criteria established by Technology Agency, as defined in the State Administrative Manual
(SAM) Section 4819.37.

PURPOSE AND DESCRIPTION

The purpose of this Technology Letter (TL) is to announce an increase in existing delegated cost
thresholds.
In light of the current IT environment and state economy, the Technology Agency has reassessed and
increased specific delegated cost threshold assignments. The increase applies to projects whose
feasibility study reports are submitted on or after January 1, 2012.

PROCESS

Agencies and departments should refer to the listing of designated cost thresholds for reporting IT
expenditures/proposals to the Technology Agency, located at:
http://www.cio.ca.gov/Contact_Us/staff_assignments.html.

QUESTIONS

Questions should be directed to your Technology Agency Program Management Office (PMO) Principal
or Manager. A listing of PMO Managers and Principals, their departmental assignments, and the
department’s delegated cost threshold amount can be found on the Technology Agency Web site
located at: Technology_Agency_PMO_Assignments.
