I.      AUTHORITY

        Authority:    Idaho Code § 67-833

II.     ABSTRACT

        The purpose of this standard is to ensure logical and consistent presentation of web-
        based materials and services while conforming to federal standards for accessibility
        by users with disabilities. The requirements of this standard apply to all public-facing
        web pages registered under the idaho.gov domain.

III.    DEFINITIONS

        See ITA Enterprise Guideline G105 (Glossary of Terms) for any definitions.

IV.     STANDARDS

        A. Branding

           The State of Idaho is dedicated to a cohesive approach for its online presence,
           ensuring consistent domain names, a unified look, and a seamless navigation
           experience.

      B. Accessibility

          The standards in this section fall under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities
          Act that sets enforceable technical standards for website and mobile app
          accessibility. State websites and web applications must comply with the success
          criteria of Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG):

          1. WCAG 2.1 Level AA
          2. WCAG 2.1 Level AAA Guideline 3.2.5 - Change on Request - Users have full
             control of major content changes. For example: Do not disable the Back button
             or system function of browsers.

      C. Content

          1. Use the clearest and simplest language appropriate for a website's content.
          2. All pages must be thoroughly checked for the appropriate use of any
             materials. All possible copyrighted, patented, and/or trademarked materials
             must be properly authorized and cited.
          3. Information published on State of Idaho Internet web servers should not
             include any content that may promote harmful activities. Agencies should
             review all content published on applicable Internet web servers to identify
             potentially sensitive information that could assist in the development and/or
             execution of harmful acts. Suggested information that should be restricted
             includes, but is not limited to, the following:

             a) bio-terrorism response plans;
             b) chemical accident risk management plans;
             c) computer network diagrams;
             d) computer network risk/vulnerability assessment reports;
             e) detailed floor plans to government facilities;
             f) facility risk/vulnerability assessment reports;
             g) physical security plans and procedures;
             h) preparedness or status reports on the ability to respond to terrorist acts;
                and
             i) water supply maps.

      D. Privacy

          See ITA Policy P1020 (Idaho.gov Portal Privacy Notice).

      E. Metatags

          See ITA Standard S4221 (Metatags).
