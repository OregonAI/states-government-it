Name                       Logical Access Controls
                           Logical access controls are protection mechanisms that limit users' access to
                           information and restrict their forms of access on the system to only what is
                           appropriate for them. Logical access controls are typically a system of
Description
                           measures and procedures, both within an organization and in the software
                           products used, aimed at protecting computer resources (data, programs and
                           terminals) against unauthorized access attempts.
                           Logical Access Control policies and procedures provide assurance that access
Rationale                  to operating systems, programs, and data is limited to properly authorized
                           individuals.
