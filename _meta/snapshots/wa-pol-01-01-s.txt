 RCW 43.105.054 WaTech Governance 2a(iv) and 2(b)
 RCW 43.105.025 WaTech Responsibilities
 POL-01 Technology Policies and Standards

     1. The first two to four letters of the policy number will represent chapter
        headings.

          a. Chapter headings and abbreviations are as follows:

              1. Policies on Policies – POL

              2. IT Management & Governance – MGMT

              3. Enterprise Architecture – EA

              4. IT Security – SEC

              5. Project Management – PM

              6. Data Management – DATA

              7. End User – USER

     2. The two digits following the first dash after the chapter abbreviation
        represent a specific policy number.

              a. Example 1: POL-01 is the first policy in the Policy on Policies chapter.

              b. Example 2: EA-01 is the first policy in the Enterprise Architecture
                 chapter.

     3. The two digits after the dash following the policy number will represent a
        standard, procedure, or guideline.

              a. Standards, Guidelines and Procedures are designated under the policy
                 they connect to with a “-S”, “-G” or “-PR” suffix, followed by two digits.

                                                                                              1

         b. Example 2: The third standard in the fourth IT Security Management
            policy would be named as: SEC-04-03-S.

         c. Example 1: POL-01-01-PR for the first procedure for the first policy in the
            first chapter.

         d. Example 3: The second guideline on the fifth End User policy would be
            named as: USER-05-02-G.

   4. Additional chapter headings may be added to this standard by WaTech as
      emerging technology needs are identified. Chapter heading revisions to
      this standard constitutes an administrative revision.
