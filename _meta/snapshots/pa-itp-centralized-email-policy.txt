1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This policy replaces, in its entirety, the Centralized Email Policy, dated January 6, 2025.

3. Purpose
The purpose of this Information Technology Policy (ITP) is to establish that the Office of
Administration, Office for Information Technology (OA/IT) maintains a centralized
enterprise email system.

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

Agencies shall use the centralized email system for email communications.

Agencies utilizing the Commonwealth Metropolitan Area Network shall use the email filtering
solution provided by the Commonwealth's Telecommunication Contract. This includes
Agencies that have obtained an IT policy exception and are maintaining email servers that are
not part of the Commonwealth active directory forest.

The centralized email system operations shall be managed by the Office of Administration,
Office for Information Technology, Enterprise Technology Services Office (OA/OIT/ETSO).

There is no requirement for a Commonwealth email account to be assigned if an employee or

   5.1 Exchange Configurations and Basic Settings
   IT Administrators shall follow guidance on usage limits, detailed at:
   https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/exchange- online-
   service-description/exchange-online-limits

   5.2 Email Message Size
   IT Administrators across the Commonwealth shall configure the email message
   maximum size to thirty-five megabytes (35 MB). Agencies needing to share files larger
   than 35 MB are encouraged to use an alternate file sharing service (i.e. OneDrive).
   Exemptions may be requested where a business need exists. Agencies needing an
   exception to the maximum message size shall obtain an approved policy exception as
   detailed in section 7.

   5.3 Personal Storage Table (PST) Requirement
   IT Administrators across the Commonwealth shall deploy configurations that do not
   allow users to create nor open .PST files. Agency personnel who are required to utilize
   .PST files, due to business requirements, must obtain an approved IT policy exception.
   Exception requests shall include the Commonwealth personnel’s CWOPA user ID. A
   business justification for allowing the creation of .PST files shall also be provided. Refer
   to Section 7 for guidance on the exception process.

   5.4 Bulk Email
   The use of the centralized email solution for bulk mail can negatively impact service, including
   the risk of being blacklisted as a spammer, which would also impact business email. Therefore,
   the centralized email system shall not be used to send bulk email. All bulk email must be
   distributed through an approved Commonwealth bulk email service. The following requirements
   shall also apply:

        5.4.1 Subscribing and Opting Out
        • All external mailing lists must use a double opt-in process to confirm recipient consent
            to receive such emails.
        • All non-mandatory bulk communications shall include a clear and functional
            unsubscribe mechanism.
        • Distribution lists shall be regularly reviewed and maintained to remove inactive,
            bounced, or unengaged addresses.

        5.4.2 Content Standards
        • Subject lines must be clear, accurate, and free of spam-associated terms, excessive
            capitalization, or repeated punctuation.
        • Emails shall comply with industry standards for formatting and coding, including the
            provision of both HTML and plain-text versions.
        • Attachments, Flash, JavaScript, and other non-standard code are prohibited in bulk
            email.
        • All bulk emails shall include a valid physical address to support sender authentication
            and compliance with applicable regulations.

6. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

7. Exception from Policy
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this policy, a request for a policy
exception shall be submitted via the IT policy exception process. Refer to IT Policy
Governance for guidance.
