Introduction:
This standard establishes the structural and organizational requirements and characteristics that data
domains must meet to support Data Owner management activities as defined in WyIT 2001-S1, Data
Owner Standard.

This standard applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff (collectively
referred to as "agencies"). This standard is also applicable to consultants, affiliates, third party vendors
and temporary employees that use or store data.

Authority
This standard is promulgated under the authority of Wyoming State Statute 9-2-2906 and its
subsections, the Department of Enterprise Technology Services is required to adopt and enforce
information technology standards, and promote efficient use of information technology throughout state

Primary Duties:
 1.​   DUTY DESCRIPTION
       1.1.​ Data domains shall have clearly defined boundaries that logically group related data
             types, systems, or business functions. Domains may be organized around agency
             programs, business services, application systems, or data types, and shall encompass
             both structured data (such as databases, spreadsheets, and organized electronic files)
             and unstructured data (such as documents, emails, multimedia files, and social media
             content). Domain boundaries shall minimize governance gaps and overlapping authority
             while supporting Data Owner management responsibilities.​

       1.2.​   Data domains shall be formally documented within agency operational scope, including
               domain boundaries, included data types, responsible systems, Data Owner assignment,
               regulatory considerations, and interdependencies with other domains. Domain
               documentation shall be maintained in a format accessible to Data Owners, Data
               Stewards, and audit functions.​

       1.3.​   Data domains shall maintain classification levels that reflect the highest sensitivity of
               component data within the domain. Domain classification shall provide the framework for
               Data Steward classification activities and regulatory compliance requirements.​

       1.4.​   Data domains shall have assigned Data Owners with documented authority as specified
               in WyIT 2001-S1. Domain governance structure shall support clear accountability and
               decision-making processes.​

       1.5.​   Data domains that share data or have operational dependencies shall maintain
               documented interfaces and coordination mechanisms. Cross-domain activities shall
               preserve individual Data Owner authority while ensuring consistent governance
               application.

       1.6.​   Data domains containing regulated information shall be structured to support specific
               regulatory requirements including HIPAA, FERPA, CJIS, PCI DSS, and IRS 1075.
               Regulatory domains shall implement enhanced documentation and control requirements.

Audit Controls and NIST Framework Mappings:
These are the standard controls that are from prior audits.

    1.​ (REQ-001) Domain Structure and Boundary Definition

                                                         4

                                                ​
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall have clearly defined boundaries that logically group
           related data types, systems, or business functions to minimize governance gaps and
           support Data Owner management responsibilities.
       b.​ Validation. Verify domain boundaries are documented, logical, and minimize overlapping
           authority through boundary definition audits and governance coverage assessments.​

2.​ (REQ-002) Domain Documentation Compliance
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall maintain standardized documentation including
           domain scope, data types, systems, Data Owner assignments, regulatory
           considerations, and interdependencies.
       b.​ Validation. Audit domain documentation for completeness and standardization, verifying
           all required elements are current and accessible to authorized personnel.​

3.​ (REQ-003) Domain Classification Standards
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall maintain classification levels reflecting the highest
           sensitivity of component data and provide framework for Data Steward classification
           activities within the domain.
       b.​ Validation. Review domain classification assignments and verify they properly reflect
           component data sensitivity and support operational classification activities.​

4.​ (REQ-004) Domain Governance Structure Verification
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall have assigned Data Owners with documented
           authority as specified in WyIT 2001-S1 and clear accountability structures for domain
           governance decisions.
       b.​ Validation. Verify Data Owner assignments are current, authority is documented, and
           governance structures support effective domain management and decision-making.​

5.​ (REQ-005) Cross-Domain Interface Management
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains with shared data or operational dependencies shall
           maintain documented interfaces and coordination mechanisms that preserve individual
           Data Owner authority.
       b.​ Validation. Audit cross-domain interfaces and coordination mechanisms, verifying they
           facilitate necessary collaboration while maintaining clear authority boundaries.​
           ​

                                               ​
6.​ (REQ-006) Regulatory Domain Structure Compliance
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains containing regulated information shall implement
           enhanced structure requirements to support specific regulatory compliance including
           HIPAA, FERPA, CJIS, PCI DSS, and IRS 1075
       b.​ Validation. Verify regulatory domains meet enhanced structural requirements and
           support regulatory compliance through specialized audits and compliance assessments.​

7.​ (REQ-007) Domain Lifecycle Management Standards
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall follow standardized lifecycle processes for domain
           creation, modification, merging, splitting, and retirement with appropriate Data Owner
           approval and documentation
       b.​ Validation. Review domain lifecycle documentation and verify changes follow established
           processes with proper approvals and impact assessments.​

8.​ (REQ-008) Domain Integration Standards
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall align with enterprise governance initiatives while
           maintaining support for Data Owner management authority and agency-specific
           operational requirements.
       b.​ Validation. Assess domain alignment with enterprise standards and verify integration
           supports rather than conflicts with Data Owner domain management responsibilities.​

9.​ (REQ-009) Domain Security and Access Standards
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall implement appropriate security measures and
           access controls based on domain classification levels and regulatory requirements.
       b.​ Validation. Verify domain security implementations align with classification levels and
           regulatory requirements through security control assessments.​

10.​(REQ-010) Domain Incident Response Standards
       a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall have documented incident response procedures
           that integrate with agency and enterprise incident response processes while supporting
           Data Owner incident management responsibilities.
       b.​ Validation. Review domain incident response procedures and verify they integrate
           appropriately with broader incident response frameworks.​

   11.​(REQ-011) Domain Reporting and Monitoring Standards
          a.​ Implementation. Data domains shall support governance reporting requirements and
              monitoring activities that provide visibility into domain health and compliance status.
           b.​ Validation. Verify domain reporting and monitoring capabilities provide appropriate
              visibility and support governance oversight requirements.
