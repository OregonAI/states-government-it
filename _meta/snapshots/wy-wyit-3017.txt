                               REMOTE ACCESS
                                       WyIT - 3017

 PURPOSE: This policy defines requirements for connecting to the state’s internal
 network(s) from external hosts via remote access technology and/or for utilizing the
 Internet for business purposes via affiliate ISP devices.

 To protect state resources from unauthorized use and/or malicious attack, all remote
 access privileges to enterprise resources must employ state-approved methods. Any
 applicable affiliate utilizing remote access privileges is responsible for ensuring remote
 access connections remain as secure as the network access within a state government
 facility. It is imperative that any remote access connection used to conduct state business
 be utilized appropriately, responsibly, and ethically.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS
       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by the WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request.

IV.    ACCESSING AND SECURING STATE-OWNED EQUIPMENT

       All state-owned equipment must be:

                                                                                WyIT - 3017       Page 1

      ● Accessed only by state employees for remote and/or telework access on the
        state network.
      ● Connected to the state network for asset inventory management, security
        vulnerability scanning, patch management, and maintenance, Monday –
        Saturday, 6 am – 6 pm.
      ● Physically secured from all physical and environmental threats.
      ● Regularly updated with the CIO approved endpoint and enterprise security
        solution(s).
      ● Reported immediately to a manager and the CIO Service Desk if damaged, lost,
        or stolen.

V.    AUTHORIZED AFFILIATE & CONTRACTOR EQUIPMENT

      All authorized affiliate and contractor-owned equipment and devices must:

      ● Be physically secured from any unauthorized use and access. Verification and
        validation of physical security controls will be provided to the CIO annually
        and/or when a significant change to the environment has occurred.
      ● Have CIO approved endpoint and enterprise security solution(s).

VI.   REMOTE CONNECTIONS

      ● Employees, contractors, affiliates, and temporary staff may be permitted to
        remotely access the state network while offsite. Users must use the approved
        Virtual Private Network (VPN) service(s). Users will be required to authenticate
        using multifactor authentication (MFA). Only authorized users are permitted to
        access the network through a VPN. Users attempting to connect to the State
        domain using an unapproved access solution will be denied access.
      ● Remote state employees using a public ISP from an authorized telework location
        must employ the state-provided and approved method of connecting, which
        must be made through managed points of entry and reviewed by the security
        liaison. When approved solutions are not appropriate due to connectivity
        limitations, a secondary solution can be authorized.
      ● Remote connections (i.e., hotspots, etc.) configured to access state resources
        must adhere to all applicable State policies. Prior approval for any remote access
        connection must be provided by the CIO. In addition, all hardware security
        configurations must be approved by the CIO. An assessment must be
        performed and documented to determine the scope and method of access, the

           technical and business risks involved, and the contractual, process, and
           technical controls required for such connection to take place.
       ●   Working from a remote location must be authorized by management and
           practices that ensure the appropriate protection of data in remote environments
           must be shared with the individual prior to the individual being granted remote
           access.
       ●   Employees, contractors, affiliates, and temporary staff will make no modifications
           to the remote access connection without express approval by the CIO. This
           includes but is not limited to, split tunneling, dual homing, non-standard
           hardware, or security configurations.
       ●   Employees, contractors, affiliates, and temporary staff with remote access
           privileges must ensure that their computers are not connected to any other
           network while connected to the state network, with the exception of internet
           connectivity.
       ●   No employee may use internet access through company networks via remote
           connection for the purpose of illegal transactions, harassment, competitor
           interests, or obscene behavior in accordance with other existing employee
           policies.
       ●   Unless coordinated, remote access sessions timeout after 45 minutes of
           inactivity and will terminate after 9 hours of continuous connection. Both
           timeouts will require the user to reconnect and re-authenticate in order to
           re-enter the state network.

VII.   USER AGREEMENTS

       Remote access affiliates agree:

       ● To immediately report to their manager and the CIO any incident or suspected
         incident of unauthorized access and/or disclosure of State resources, databases,
         networks, etc.
       ● To accept that access and/or connection to the state’s network(s) may be
         monitored to record dates, times, and duration of access to identify unusual
         usage patterns or other activity. As with computers physically residing in a state
         government facility, this is done to identify accounts/computers that may have
         been compromised by external parties.

  REFERENCES: This policy has no supporting references to websites, other policies,
  or federal or state statutes.
