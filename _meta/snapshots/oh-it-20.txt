IT-20 Vulnerability Management

State of Ohio Administrative Policy
No: IT-20 - Information Technology
Effective: November 20, 2024
Issued By: Kathleen C. Madden, Director

I. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to provide statewide Vulnerability management requirements that will allow for the consistent identification, prioritization, and Remediation of known security Vulnerabilities across all Agency-Managed Information Systems and Agency-Managed System Assets. This policy also establishes a statewide security Vulnerability severity rating methodology.
All defined terms are capitalized and a link to the glossary of definitions is found in section IV. In addition, references to National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Special Publication 800-53, “Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations,” Risk Assessment (RA) and System and Information Integrity (SI) family identifiers and control numbers are provided in parentheticals next to requirements, where applicable.

II. Scope
This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

III. Policy
The Department of Administrative Services (DAS) Office of Information Security and Privacy (OISP) is designated as the central authority responsible for statewide identification and communication of security Vulnerabilities to the Agencies. Agencies must maintain a Vulnerability management program. As part of the program the Agency must define, document, implement, and maintain the processes and procedures required to meet the requirements of this policy as well as Ohio IT Standard ITS-SEC-03, “Vulnerability Management.”

A. Vulnerability Identification (RA-5)
The DAS OISP, in collaboration with Agencies, shall routinely monitor and scan all Agency-Managed Information Systems and Agency-Managed System Assets for Vulnerabilities.

B. Vulnerability Severity Ratings Methodology
DAS OISP and Agencies must prioritize and address security Vulnerabilities in accordance with the severity ratings published in the NIST National Vulnerability Database (NVD), utilizing the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS).

C. Vulnerability Remediation (RA-5 and SI-2)
Security Vulnerabilities, as identified by DAS OISP and Agencies, must be Remediated in a timely manner by Agencies through the application of patches, configuration changes, replacing or removing software, or coding changes in accordance with the requirements outlined in Ohio IT Standard ITS-SEC-03, “Vulnerability Management.”

IV. Definitions
All defined terms are capitalized within the policy and the online glossary of definitions can be found at IT Policy Glossary - Department of Administrative Services.
