I. DEFINITION

   See G105 (ITA Glossary of Terms) for related definitions.

II. RATIONALE

   The primary role of information technology is to support the agency’s business goals and
   objectives, ensuring efficient and effective services to citizens. The Agency IT Plan
   serves as a strategic document integrated with the Agency’s Business Plan, outlining the
   necessary IT projects and resources to achieve these goals.

   These guidelines assist agencies in evaluating and preparing IT Plans in alignment with
   ITA Policy P2010 (Information Technology Planning Process). They provide a flexible
   framework that ensures IT planning remains closely harmonized with the business
   objectives, while allowing agencies to tailor the format to their needs.

III. GUIDELINE

   Agencies should structure their IT Plans using the following categories including details
   as appropriate to ensure alignment with business objectives, compliance requirements,
   and statewide IT strategies.

   1. Executive Summary

       •   An overview of the agency’s enterprise business strategy including its mission,
           goals, accomplishments, and IT-related objectives

       •   A list of IT projects and initiatives

   2. Methodology

       •   A concise summary of the methods used in developing the IT Plan, including
           interviews, external resources, and research sources.

   3. IT Projects and Initiatives

       •   A comprehensive list of IT projects and initiatives supporting the agency’s
           business goals.

           ♦ Must include project title, description (purpose and benefits), estimated costs,
             funding sources (e.g., federal grants, state general fund), IT resources, and
             projected timeframe.

       •   Reference applicable ITA policies, including:
           ITA Policy P2010 (Information Technology Planning Process) and
           P2030 (Information Technology Large-Scale Project Review).

   4. Policies and Standards Compliance

       •   Explanation of how the Agency IT Plan aligns with the State IT Plan and Policies
           and Standards. (See https://ita.idaho.gov/resources/ )

   5. Business Recovery Plan

       •   Agencies must include their current Business Recovery Plan as part of the IT Plan
           submission for the annual ITA review. (See ITA Policy P2020 (Business Recovery
           Planning))

   6. Supporting Appendices – Consider including the following details in an appendix:

       •   IT staffing details

       •   Hardware inventory and requirements

       •   Software inventory and requirements

       •   Accessibility Compliance Report

       •   Geographic Information Systems (GIS)

       •   Additional relevant information

IV. PROCEDURE REFERENCE

    Policies for agency IT plan are detailed in ITA Policies P2010 (Information Technology
    Planning Process), P2020 (Business Recovery Planning), and P2030 (Information
    Technology Large-Scale Project Review).
