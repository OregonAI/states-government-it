IT-01 Authority of the State Chief Information Officer to Establish Ohio IT Policy

State of Ohio Administrative Policy
No: IT-01 - Information Technology
Effective: October 15, 2025
Issued By: Kathleen C. Madden, Director

I. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to communicate the information technology (IT) policy-making authority of the state chief information officer (state CIO), who is also the assistant director for the Ohio Department of Administrative Services (DAS). All defined terms are capitalized and a link to the glossary of definitions is found in section IV.

II. Scope
This state policy is applicable to every organized body, office, or agency established by the laws of the state for the exercise of any function of state government except for those specifically exempted.

III. Policy
Under the authority and within the limits set forth in the Ohio Revised Code (ORC), the state CIO, under the direction of the director of DAS, has been granted policy authority as it relates to the acquisition and use of telecommunications and information technology, including, but not limited to, hardware, software, technology services, information security and privacy (including the protection of Confidential Data and Personally Identifiable Information), and the extension of the service life of information technology systems. This policy authority is promulgated in four distinct forms - administrative rules, policies, standards, and procedures (see Attachment 1 - Table of Responsibility). Each has its own enumeration scheme (see Attachment 2 - Table of Enumeration).

A. Ohio IT Administrative Rules
Administrative rules are written statements of law adopted by an administrative agency pursuant to authority granted under the ORC to carry out the policies and intent of a statute enacted by the General Assembly.
Administrative rules are codified in the Ohio Administrative Code once they are adopted. The state CIO establishes IT-related administrative rules. The DAS Office of Information Technology (OIT) administrator for Investment and Governance, the chief information security officer (CISO), and/or the chief privacy officer (CPO) administer their drafting and promulgation. The Secretary of State and the Ohio Legislative Service Commission maintain official registers of Ohio administrative rules. Established rules are available at the Ohio General Assembly Website at Laws - Ohio Legislature. Pending or newly adopted rules are published in the Register of Ohio.

B. Ohio IT Policies
Policies are statements of specific management direction, established by the state CIO, to ensure that investment in and use of information technology by state agencies produces the best solutions for the state as a whole and its respective agencies and aligns with applicable, IT-related federal and state regulations. Such policies are written in a standardized format and contain an effective date after which the policy is operational. Policies do not have an expiration date. They are updated or rescinded as needed. The state CIO establishes IT-related state policies. The DAS OIT administrator for Investment and Governance, the CISO, and/or the CPO administer their drafting. The DAS OIT administrator for Investment and Governance is responsible for promulgating and maintaining the official register of Ohio IT Policy, which is published at Policy Finder - Department of Administrative Services.

C. Ohio IT Standards
Standards are statements of specifically prescribed use and acquisition, established by the state CIO, to produce the best IT solutions for the state as a whole and its respective agencies and to ensure alignment with applicable IT-related federal and state regulations. Ohio IT standards are written in a standardized format and contain an effective date for compliance. Ohio IT standards do not have an expiration date. They are updated or rescinded as needed. The state CIO establishes state IT-related standards. The DAS OIT administrator for Investment and Governance, the CISO, and/or the CPO administer their drafting. The DAS OIT administrator for Investment and Governance is responsible for promulgating and maintaining the official register of State of Ohio IT Standards, which is published at Policy Finder - Department of Administrative Services.

D. Enterprise IT Procedures
Enterprise IT procedures are statements of the specific steps or a course of action that state agencies and other entities external to DAS OIT must follow:
1. to obtain a DAS OIT-provided service.
2. in interacting with DAS OIT in its efforts to carry out its coordination and oversight role as mandated by the ORC.
3. to comply with an Ohio IT administrative rule, policy, or standard.
Enterprise IT procedures are written in a standardized format and contain an effective date for compliance. Enterprise IT procedures do not have an expiration date. They are updated or rescinded as needed. The state CIO, CISO, CPO, administrator for Investment and Governance or any DAS OIT business unit manager or service owner shall direct the drafting and promulgation of procedures assigned to their respective areas. The DAS OIT administrator for Investment and Governance administers a publication process, and maintains the official register of Enterprise IT Procedures, which is published at Policy Finder - Department of Administrative Services.

IV. Definitions
All defined terms are capitalized within the policy and the online glossary of definitions can be found at IT Policy Glossary - Department of Administrative Services.
