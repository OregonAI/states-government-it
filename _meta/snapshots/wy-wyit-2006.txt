Scope:
This policy establishes the requirements for designating and empowering an Agency Official for Privacy
(AOP) role within each Executive Branch agency to ensure appropriate governance, compliance, and
risk management regarding data privacy operations. The AOP serves as the central privacy authority
responsible for developing, implementing, and maintaining comprehensive privacy programs that
protect sensitive data and information, regardless of format, structure, or formal asset designation and
ensure compliance with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

This policy addresses the critical need for dedicated privacy leadership within state agencies to
manage privacy risks, ensure regulatory compliance, and provide accountability for privacy-related
decisions and incidents.

Authority
This policy is promulgated under the authority of Wyoming State Statute 9-2-2906 and its subsections,
the Department of Enterprise Technology Services is required to adopt and enforce information
technology standards, and promote efficient use of information technology throughout state
government.

Specifically, this policy is further promulgated under the authority of the below listed Wyoming State
Statute subsections.

   ●​ Implements and maintains an information technology governance program for state agencies
      (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (ii));
   ●​ Establishes and enforces data security and privacy policies and standards for the state data
      infrastructure Establishes review criteria for the agencies information technology projects,
      procurements and purchases. (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (iii)); and

                                                         ​
Adopts information technology policies and standards and ensures agency compliance with those
policies and standards (W.S. 9-2-2906,(b), (vi)).

Audience
This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff (collectively
referred to as "agencies"). This policy is also applicable to consultants, affiliates, third party vendors
and temporary employees that use or store state data

Policy Statements:
 1.​   AGENCY OFFICIAL FOR PRIVACY (AOP) DESIGNATION REQUIREMENTS
       1.1.​ The Agency Official for Privacy (AOP) shall:

               1.2.​       Be an official serving in a central leadership position at the agency;​

               1.3.​       Have visibility into relevant agency operations; and ​

               1.4.​       Is positioned highly enough within the agency to regularly engage with other
                           agency leadership.​

               1.5.​       Classification must occur upon data creation, acquisition, or when existing data
                           undergoes significant modification.
                [1], [2]

       1.2.​   Personnel identified by the agency as the AOP(s) shall have the necessary skills,
               knowledge, and expertise to lead and direct privacy programs.
                1.2.1.​ The agency AOP(s) shall receive training consistent with the ability to carry
                        out the privacy-related functions dictated by the needs of the State and
                        agency as well as those described in law and policies.
               [1], [3], [4]

       1.3.​   The AOP shall have the necessary authority at the agency to lead and direct the
               agency's privacy program and carry out the privacy-related functions described in law
               and policies.​
               [1], [3], [5]​

 2.​   RESPONSIBILITIES
       2.1.​ The Agency Head is ultimately responsible for ensuring privacy interests are protected,
             and sensitive data and information, regardless of format, structure, or formal asset designation
             is managed responsibly within the agency. To ensure agencies effectively carry out the
             privacy-related functions described in law and policy, the agency head shall designate
             an AOP with the following responsibilities:

                2.1.1.​        The agency shall develop, implement, document, maintain, and oversee an
                               agency-wide privacy program that supports state data governance

                                                 ​
                       requirements and ensures protection of sensitive data and information,
                       regardless of format, structure, or formal asset designation in accordance with
                       applicable state and federal law.​

         2.1.2.​       The privacy program shall be led by an AOP responsible for ensuring
                       compliance with applicable privacy requirements, developing and evaluating
                       privacy policy, and managing privacy risks consistent with the agency's
                       mission.
                        [1], [3], [5], [6], [7]​

2.2.​   At the discretion of the AOP or agency head, other qualified agency personnel may
        perform specific privacy functions under AOP oversight, excluding final decision-making
        authority for privacy risk acceptance and incident response. The AOP retains ultimate
        accountability for all delegated privacy activities and must ensure proper training and
        supervision of delegated personnel.
        [1],[2],[3]​

2.3.​   The AOP shall ensure that the agency considers and addresses the privacy implications
        of all agency regulations and policies and shall lead the agency's evaluation of the
        privacy implications of legislative proposals.​
        [1], [3]​

2.4.​   The AOP shall have a central role in overseeing, coordinating, and facilitating the
        agency's privacy compliance efforts. The AOP shall ensure that the agency complies
        with applicable privacy requirements in law, regulation, and policy.​
        [1], [3], [6], [7]

2.5.​   The AOP shall manage privacy risks throughout the complete lifecycle of sensitive data
        and information, regardless of format, structure, or formal asset designation, from
        collection through disposal. The AOP's review of privacy risks shall begin at the earliest
        planning and development stages of agency actions and policies that involve sensitive
        data and information, and continue throughout the lifecycle of the programs or
        information systems. Appropriately managing privacy risks may require agencies to take
        steps beyond those required in law, regulation, and policy.​
        [1], [3]

2.6.​   The AOP shall implement a procedure in their agency to report suspected privacy
        incidents and/or breaches for agency staff to the AOP.​
        [1], [3]​

2.7.​   The AOP shall represent their agency on any subcommittee, committee, or council that
        governs privacy in the state.​
        [1], [3]​

       2.8.​   ETS shall provide training to the AOP on privacy concepts. This training shall not be
               construed as a substitute for any training required for agencies that have federal
               requirements. Agencies shall remain subject to federal and regulatory training
               requirements.​
               [1], [3]​

 3.​   IMPLEMENTATION
       3.1.​ The head of each agency shall assess the extent, management, structure, and operation
             of the agency's privacy program and designate an official(s) to serve as the Agency
             Official for Privacy (AOP).​
             [1], [3]​

       3.2.​   Agencies shall conduct privacy risk assessments of existing information systems that
               process, store, or transmit sensitive information, regardless of format, structure, or
               formal asset designation, prioritizing systems based on risk level.​
               [1], [3]

Compliance & Exceptions:
Compliance
Employees who violate this policy may be subject to appropriate disciplinary action up to and including
discharge as well as both civil and criminal penalties. Non-employees, including, without limitation,
contractors, may be subject to termination of contractual agreements, denial of access to IT resources,
and other actions as well as both civil and criminal penalties.

Exceptions
Requests for exceptions to this policy shall be reviewed by the Chief Information Security Officer
(CISO) and the Chief Information Officer (CIO). Departments requesting exceptions shall provide such
requests to the CIO in accordance with WyIT.3002 Policy Exception Request.

The request should specifically state the scope of the exception along with justification for granting the
exception, the potential impact or risk attendant upon granting the exception, risk mitigation measures
to be undertaken by the IT Department, initiatives, actions and a time-frame for achieving the minimum
compliance level with the policies set forth herein. The CIO shall review such requests; confer with the
requesting department.

Any exception to established policy shall be defined and tracked as a risk to the state and established
mitigating controls monitored by the agency.
