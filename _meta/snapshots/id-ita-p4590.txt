I. AUTHORITY
Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT
The State of Idaho considers an incident or breach that occurs within an Agency a
serious matter. This policy ensures that an Agency/Agency IT Security Coordinator
establishes incident response guidelines.

III. DEFINITIONS
See ITA Guideline G105 (ITA Glossary of Terms) for definitions.

IV. POLICY

   1. State agencies must document incident response guidelines that
      define their agencies roles and responsibilities if an incident or breach
      does occur.

   2. State agencies must investigate and report their findings on incidents and
      breaches in accordance with policies and standards for reporting.
