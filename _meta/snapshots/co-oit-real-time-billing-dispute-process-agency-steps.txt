These are the key steps agencies must follow to dispute a Real-time Billing (RtB) charge or to
request a change. For unique disputes involving lack of detail, service quality, multi-agency
impacts, or division coding changes, consult the Real-time Billing Dispute Procedure for more
information.

Step 1: Identify and Notify
If an agency identifies a needed correction, it must first notify its assigned IT Director to
determine if an official dispute should be submitted.

Step 2: Access the Dispute Form
If a dispute is deemed necessary, the agency Subject Matter Expert (SME) must log in to the
OIT Real-time Billing (RtB) website and click the “Dispute Charges” button located on any of
the Detailed Dashboards to access the dispute form.

Step 3: Submit the Dispute Form
The agency SME must complete the official dispute form, clearly detailing the specific
services that need changes and providing the rationale for the correction.

Step 4: Adhere to Submission Timelines
Agencies must ensure disputes are submitted timely — closed previous fiscal years cannot be
corrected. The dispute submission timelines are as follows:

Step 5: Track Dispute Status
Once the dispute form is submitted, the OIT Budget team will process the request and notify
the appropriate service owner for review. Agencies can track the status of their submitted
dispute on the Billing Adjustments dashboard on the RtB website.
