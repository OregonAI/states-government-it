8-606. Staff use of cloud storage websites.
    Accessing online cloud storage websites (such as Dropbox, Google Drive, etc.) is a security
risk that will be restricted based on an employee’s job functions. Use of these systems for any
state purposes is prohibited unless approved by the employee’s supervisor or manager. Even if
approved, it is prohibited to process or store any HIGH IMPACT or MODERATE IMPACT
information with these services, unless the storage is encrypted with approved technology, and
has been approved in advance by the state information security officer.
