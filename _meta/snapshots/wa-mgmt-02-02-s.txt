Standard 113.30 – TBM Taxonomy Standard

STANDARD STATEMENT
Agencies within the program must follow the published TBM taxonomy for IT Services,
Technology Towers and Cost Sources made available by the TBM Council Board
Committee on Standards. Link to published taxonomy.

   •   During the first quarter of each fiscal year, the program will review published
       standards and establish a timeline to incorporate any changes made to the TBM
       taxonomy. All efforts will be made to incorporate taxonomy changes at beginning
       of the fiscal year.
