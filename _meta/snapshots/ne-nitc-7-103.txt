                                                                                Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                           Technical Standards and Guidelines

7-103. SMTP routing standard.
   All inbound and outbound SMTP traffic must be routed through the anti-spam and anti-virus
appliance managed by the Office of the CIO.
