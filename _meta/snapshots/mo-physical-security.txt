Name                       Physical Security
                           Physical Security is the application of physical barriers and control procedures
Description                as preventive measures or countermeasures against threats to resources and
                           sensitive information.
                           Physical Security reduces the exposure of assets to physical perils. They are
                           implemented to protect the system resources themselves, the facility housing
Rationale                  the system resources, and the facilities used to support their operation (such
                           as wiring, backup media, and electric power).
                           Prevents:
                           • Physical damage to assets
                           • Interruptions in information technology services
Benefits                   • Unauthorized disclosure of information
                           • Corruption of system integrity
                           • Theft
