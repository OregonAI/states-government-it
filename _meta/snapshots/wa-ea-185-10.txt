Enterprise Service Designation

Enterprise Business Processes for Internal Identity Management

• Manage the State's internal identity management solution, the Enterprise Active Directory, to accomplish single sign-on.
• Manage the State Forest within the Enterprise Active Directory.
• Manage the federation to the Enterprise Active Directory.
• Manage the delegations within shared/central services, such as email.
• Maintain security boundaries between agencies.
• Administer the enterprise tenant account within Office 365.

These enterprise business processes are centrally managed by WaTech.

This enterprise service designation does not apply to institutions of higher education.

For questions about this enterprise service or to request a waiver, please contact the OCIO Policy and Waiver Mailbox.
