BACKGROUND
Government Code Section 11545 states that the California Department of Technology (Department of
Technology) must develop an annual California Information Technology (IT) Strategic Plan which
guides the acquisition, management, and use of IT. To achieve the required integration of multi-agency
business and strategic technology planning, each Agency/state entity1 is required to develop a five-year
IT Capital Plan (ITCP) that must be submitted to the Department of Technology. This planning process
was implemented in 2008 and is updated annually.

The Department of Technology uses the information submitted by Agencies/state entities to build the
annual Statewide ITCP, thus achieving the required integration of multi-agency business and strategic
technology planning. The Statewide ITCP represents the state's plan for strategic IT investments and
supports the Governor's Budget and the California IT Strategic Plan.

In September 2013, the Department of Technology released Technology Letter (TL) 13-02 which
announced the restructuring of the IT project approval process and introduced a Stage/Gate model and
new SIMM Sections 19 and 19A.

SIMM Section 19A contains the Stage 1 Business Analysis, which is the first stage of the Project
Approval Lifecycle process and is used to generate the annual Statewide ITCP. The Stage 1 Business
Analysis provides a basis for project management, program management, executive management, and
state-level control agencies to understand and agree on business problems or opportunities, and the
objectives to address them. Agencies/state entities must receive approval of their Stage 1 Business
Analysis by the Department of Technology prior to starting work on their feasibility study.

In June 2014, the Department of Technology released TL 14-02 which replaces Section 3 of the
Feasibility Study Report with the Stage 1 Business Analysis, placing more emphasis on building a
strong business case including the business drivers, business problems or opportunities, business
objectives, business owners, key stakeholders, and executive sponsors.

ITCP requirements are defined in SAM Section 4904. SIMM Section 57 provides instructions to
Agencies/state entities on the specifics of how to develop and submit annual ITCPs.

1
 State entity: Includes every state office, officer, department, division, bureau, board, and commission, including

PURPOSE
 The purpose of this TL is to announce:

       This TL supersedes TL 13-05.
       Calendar year 2014 ITCP and Stage 1 Business Analysis submission processes will continue
        to follow the 2013 processes and will be unchanged.
       For calendar year 2014 only, the ITCP and Stage 1 Business Analysis submission deadline to
        the Department of Technology is extended to October 31, 2014.
       ITCPs are to be submitted electronically to: CIOPMOSubmission@state.ca.gov with a cc to
        the assigned Department of Technology IT Project Oversight and Consulting (ITPOC)
        Manager.
       ITCP and Stage 1 Business Analysis submissions to the Department of Technology must be
        completed per the instructions in SIMM Sections 57 and 19A or they will be returned.
        The link to SIMM is as follows: http://www.cio.ca.gov/Government/IT_Policy/SIMM.html.
