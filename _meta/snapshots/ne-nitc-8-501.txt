                                                          Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                     Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-501. System security; approved hardware and software; documentation.
    (1) Only Office of the CIO approved hardware or software is permitted within the state’s
information technology infrastructure.
    (2) All authorized hardware and software shall be inventoried and documented. Results shall
be secured in an auditable fashion.
