                                                                                         Technical Standards and Guidelines

5-301. Internet fax standard for state agencies.
   All state government agencies, except higher education entities, shall use the OCIO Internet
Fax System provided by the Office of the CIO for computer-based fax services, including
desktop and application-based faxing.
    This standard does not apply to the use of stand-alone fax machines connected directly to a
telephone line.
