Name                   Date/Time Controls
                       Restrictions based on time and day bolsters the control environment. The intent
Description            is to require more than simple access controls, normally based on user-IDs and
                       passwords.
                       Hackers are most active at night, just when systems are sparsely staffed, if
Rationale              staffed at all. If users stay logged on, hackers can attack their network assets
                       and use them to attack other systems.
Benefits               •   Reduces the amount of time the account is open to unauthorized access.
