# Recommended PC Specifications (ETS Doc No. 306.01)

## At a glance

Attachment A to Doc No. 306: the recommended specification for each user type and the justification required for non-standard types. It prints example device prices and, as an illustration of a justification, sample rows with example user names; they are mirrored as printed. Library row: effective 2018/06/22, revised 2024/07/02. OWNER DECISION whether an attachment of example specifications is an instrument (see left_out).
