# Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) Examples, with the list of Prohibited GenAI Tools (DTI Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) Examples)

## At a glance

One page that DTI issues through the Technology and Architecture Standards Committee under its standards header: examples of public and enterprise generative AI products, and the 'Prohibited GenAI Tools' list (RedNote, DeepSeek, Webull, WeChat AI, Qwen, WormGPT, FraudGPT), 'an initial list that meets the criteria in the policy'. The policy OT-AI-001 points users to it. Owner decision: seeded as doc_type standard (its header is the standards header; it prints no document number or revision), and as an instrument because it names the tools users may not use; its first list says it is examples only.

## Full text

                                                                  DELAWARE STATE-WIDE INFORMATION
                                                    TECHNOLOGY AND ARCHITECTURE STANDARDS

                Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) Examples*

Public GenAI
    •    ChatGPT
    •    DALL•E 2
    •    Gemini

Enterprise GenAI
    •    Adobe Firefly
    •    Microsoft Copilot
    •    Amazon Bedrock
    •    Azure AI
*these are only examples. They do not represent an endorsement of these companies or their products.
Please note that this list is not intended to serve as confirmation that the State has purchased a product
listed here.

                               Prohibited GenAI Tools Examples*

Prohibited GenAI Tools
    •    RedNote
    •    DeepSeek
    •    Webull
    •    WeChat AI
    •    Qwen
    •    WormGPT
    •    FraudGPT
*this is an initial list that meets the criteria in the policy.

These examples are provided by the Department of Technology and Information (DTI), through the Technology and Architecture
Standards Committee (TASC), and are applicable to all Information Technology use throughout the State of Delaware. Any
questions or comments should be directed to dti_tasc@delaware.gov.
