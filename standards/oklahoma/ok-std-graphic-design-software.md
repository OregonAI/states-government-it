# Graphic Design Software Standard (OMES Graphic Design Software Standard)

## At a glance

A CIO standard in the 'Applications and Data' group of the OMES statewide IT policy and standards library, binding on state agencies under the Information Technology Consolidation and Coordination Act. Its Purpose or Introduction, as printed: The purpose of this document is to establish a standard for graphic design software and its expected usage.

## Full text

                              Graphic Design Software Standard

Introduction
Graphic design is an important part of state services and when it is done well, can enhance the
user experience for our citizens as they engage with OMES and the agencies we support.

Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish a standard for graphic design software and its
expected usage.

Standard
The standard tools to be used for graphic design are the tools of the Adobe Creative Cloud. This
includes, but is not limited to:
   •   Adobe Photoshop.
   •   Adobe Illustrator.
   •   Adobe InDesign.
   •   Adobe Acrobat.
   •   Adobe XD.
   •   Adobe Lightroom.
It is expected that these tools should be used for creating designs and digital assets to support
the goals of state agencies, state initiatives and to create assets for the State of Oklahoma.
Requesting Access.
A request for a license to the Adobe Creative Suite is requested through the OMES service
desk or service catalog.
Exceptions to the standard.
It is expected that anyone creating digital assets for the state should first try to acquire a license
to the Adobe Creative Suite. If access cannot be granted, then other approved tools include:
   •   Canva.
   •   Figma.
   •   Paint.Net.
   •   G.I.M.P.
   •   Inkscape.
   •   Photopea.
   •   Sketch.
Any other software not included in the above list that is required to be used by the graphic
designer should be requested for approval and addition to this standard before use.

Compliance
This standard shall take effect upon publication and is made pursuant to Title 62 O.S. §§
34.11.1 and 34.12 and Title 62 O.S. § 35.8. OMES IS may amend and publish the amended
standards policies and standards at any time. Compliance is expected with all published policies
and standards, and any published amendments thereof. Employees found in violation of this
standard may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

Rationale
To coordinate and require central approval of state agency information technology purchases
and projects to enable the chief information officer to assess the needs and capabilities of state
agencies as well as streamline and consolidate systems to ensure that the state delivers
essential public services to its citizens in the most efficient manner at the lowest possible cost to
taxpayers.

Revision history
This standard is subject to periodic review to ensure relevancy.
