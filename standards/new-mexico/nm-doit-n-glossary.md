# Glossary of Terms (N-DEF001)

## At a glance

Statewide architectural configuration requirement of the Department of Information Technology (the page's ACR table): Glossary of Terms. The file prints its own effective date; it is one of the DoIT standards, guidelines and policies issued under the Department of Information Technology Act and 1.12.6 NMAC. A one-page glossary of the terms the requirements use, printed as an ACR document (N-DEF001.001); a reference table of external glossary links.
