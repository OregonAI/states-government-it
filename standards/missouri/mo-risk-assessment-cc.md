                                                 COMPLIANCE DETAIL
                                A Risk Assessment encompasses the following steps
                                • Step 1 – Prepare for the Assessment
                                           o Identify Purpose
                                                    Response to an event or breach.
                                                    Regulatory compliance
                                                    Agency policy
                                           o Identify Scope
State the Guideline, Standard                       Systems or data based on regulation, compliance or
or Legislation                                           agency needs.
                                           o Identify specific assumptions, constraints, risk tolerance, and
                                               priorities/trade-offs used within agencies to make operational
                                               decisions.
                                           o Identify information sources of descriptive, threat, vulnerability,
                                               and impact information to be used in the risk assessment.
                                           o Identify Risk Model and Analytic Approach
                                                    Three types of assessments:
                                                             • Quantitative

                                                          • Qualitative
                                                          • Semi-quantitative
                              • Step 2 – Conduct the Assessment
                                         o Identify threat sources of concern, including capability, intent and
                                            targeting characteristics for different kinds of threats, such as
                                            adversarial threats, internally and externally.
                                         o Identify potential threat events, and any sources that could initiate
                                            events, such as a fire or natural disaster.
                                         o Identify vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions that affect the
                                            likelihood that threat events of concern result in adverse impacts.
                                         o Determine the likelihood that a threat event would occur given the
                                            agency’s potential vulnerabilities.
                                         o Determine the adverse impacts from threat events of concern
                                            considering the characteristics of the threat sources that could
                                            initiate the events, the vulnerabilities/predisposing conditions
                                            identified, and the susceptibility reflecting the
                                            safeguards/countermeasures planned or implemented to impede
                                            such events.
                                         o Determine the risk to the agency from potential threat events,
                                            considering the impact that would result from the events and the
                                            likelihood of the events occurring.

                              • Step 3 – Communicate and Share Risk Assessment Results
                                          o   The agency can communicate risk assessment results in a variety
                                              of ways (e.g., executive briefings, risk assessment reports,
                                              dashboards).
                                          o   Share risk-related information produced during the risk
                                              assessment with appropriate organizational personnel.
                              • Step 4 – Maintaining the Risk Assessment
                                          o   Agencies monitor important risk factors on an ongoing basis to
                                              ensure that the information needed to make credible, risk-based
                                              decisions continues to be available over time. Monitoring risk
                                              factors can provide critical information on changing conditions that
                                              could potentially affect the ability of agencies to conduct core
                                              missions and business functions.
                                          o   Update existing risk assessment using the results from ongoing
                                              monitoring of risk factors.

                              The Risk Assessment Report is used in the Risk Mitigation phase of the Security
                              Risk Management process.
                              This document will be reviewed annually.
