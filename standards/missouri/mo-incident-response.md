                                                       DEFINITION
 Name                  Incident Response
                       Incident Response is an organized approach to addressing and managing the aftermath of

                       recovery time and cost.
 Rationale             Provides a consistent approach to handling security incidents.
                           •   More rapid detection of incidents
                           •   Minimizes loss and destruction
