                                   COMPLIANCE COMPONENT

                                                      DEFINITION
Name                  Incident Response Policies, Procedures and Training
                      Incident Response Policies and Procedures help communicate to the State's IT community
                      and interested parties (external agencies and the public) about discovered security threats
                      and breaches. Policies and Procedures should help agencies record and document the
                      following:
                           • Contact information for the recipient of the incident response report;
                           • Incident description;
                                   o Date, Time, and location where the incident occurred
                                   o How the incident was discovered
                                   o IP Address/Host Name/Physical location of the breach
                                   o What data was compromised
Description                                  Type or types of data compromised
                                             Sensitivity of data/parties affected
                                   o Actions taken to mitigate the damages
                                   o Other parties contacted regarding the incident
                           • Contact information for the person reporting the incident
                                   o Name, title and signature of the reporting official
                           • Time and Date incident was resolved (if applicable)
                           • Method of incident resolution
                      Incident Response Training teaches state employees about their responsibilities in the
                      event of a security incident.

                      Agencies handling confidential information need to have policies and procedures in place to
                      assist them when responding to security incidents. Incident response training is associated
Rationale
                      with the assigned roles and responsibilities of organizational personnel to ensure that the
                      appropriate content and level of detail are included in such training.
                          • Provides responders with a comprehensive and structured response to a security
                               incident.
Benefits
                          • Helps to minimize the damage from security incidents
                          • Builds knowledge base
