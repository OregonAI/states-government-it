TS-INF-001: End User Computer Equipment

  Title                             End User Computer Equipment
  Executive Sponsor                 Deskside Support Services, Enterprise Deskside Support Services

  Issuing Authority                 Office of Information Technology (OIT)
  Applies to                        Any end-user desktop, laptop, tablet, or computer hardware
                                    purchased or supported by OIT and OIT-supported agencies,
                                    intended for use as the end user's Primary Workstation or Tablet
                                    PC.
  Tier                              Standard
  Version                           4.0
  Effective Date                    08/20/2015
  Last Updated                      04/16/2026

1. Purpose
Review, evaluate, and recommend end-user desktop, laptop, and tablet computer hardware
standards for employees of the Governor's Office of Information Technology (OIT), and agencies
supported by OIT, to assist with standardization and in making the support and buying of end-user
computer technologies more consistent and efficient (per state statute Article 37.5, Title 24).

2. Scope
These standards apply to any end-user desktop, laptop, tablet, or computer hardware either
purchased or supported by OIT and OIT-supported agencies, intended for use as the end user's
Primary Workstation or Tablet PC. Secondary or companion devices (such as mobile phones, ARM-
based tablets, etc.) are not within the scope of this standard (see the upcoming Mobile Device
Standard).

3. Roles and Responsibilities
Enterprise Architecture - Chief Enterprise Architect
   ●      Chairs and conducts business of the Architecture Review Board (ARB).
   ●      Updates effective date and/or last review date.
   ●      Publishes the ARB-approved Standard to the OIT public-facing website.
Architecture Review Board (ARB)
   ●      Engages stakeholders and subject matter experts to determine the standard.

   ●   Reviews and approves the Standard (this document).
Deskside Support Services - Enterprise Deskside Support Services Manager
   ●   Owner of Standard; engages stakeholders and subject matter experts to determine the
       standard.
   ●   Updates document standards and version number.
   ●   Presents to the Architecture Review Board for approval and adoption.
Colorado Information Security Office (CISO)
   ●   Reviews and approves requests for the use of devices used internationally that would connect
       into the state network.
All OIT staff are responsible for being aware of the existence of this (and all) standard(s) and its
purposes of authority and governance in support of OIT's statutorily mandated mission in service of
Colorado State Government affairs.

4. Standards
Based on the following technical specifications, each Deskside Support Manager will create and keep
updated, as changes occur, the computer make, model, and configuration used by the supported
agency.
Desktop Computer — User Level I and II: Processor: Intel Ultra 5, i5 11th generation or newer, or AMD
Ryzen 5 5th generation and newer. RAM: 16 GB DDR5 through 32 GB DDR5. Monitor: 24"–27" LED,
1280x1024 through 3840x2160. Hard Drive: 256 GB or higher SSD or 2.5" NVMe (preferred). Network
Adapter: Ethernet 1 GB, DMI; Wake on LAN. Wireless: Wifi 6–7 (optional). Ports: USB 3.0 or higher
required. Energy Compliance: EPEAT Silver. Tech Support: 3 or 4 year on-site next business day.
Laptop Computer — User Level I and II: Processor: Intel Ultra 5, i5 11th gen or newer, or AMD Ryzen 5
5th gen and newer. RAM: 16 GB DDR5 or greater. Hard Drive: 256 GB or greater SSD or NVMe.
Wireless: Wifi 6–7. USB: v3.0 or above (2+). Energy Compliance: EPEAT Silver. Tech Support: 3 or 4
year parts and labor.
Tablet PC (Convertible) — User Level I and II: Must run Windows 11+ as a primary device. Same
processor, RAM, and drive requirements as laptop. Wireless: Wifi 6–7. Energy Compliance: EPEAT
Silver. DMI Software required.
Tablet Devices — User Level I, II, and III: Must run Windows 11+ as a primary device. Processor: Intel
Ultra 5, i5 15th gen or newer, or AMD Ryzen 5 5th gen and newer. RAM: 16 GB or greater. Hard Drive:
256 GB or more SSD. Network: Vendor Ethernet 1GB peripheral. Wireless: Wifi 6–7. Energy
Compliance: EPEAT Silver.
Mac, Apple, Android, and Chrome laptops or tablets must be exception-requested items (see Section
5). Approved devices will be supported to the best of OIT's ability but may have limited support.

5. Variance and Exception Process
Any requests that are variances to the standards in Section 4 will need to be requested using the
Security Exception Request Form. For all devices, they must not utilize Snapdragon or ARM64
architecture.
State equipment should not be taken out of the country for any purpose without approval granted
through the current CISP-001 policy. Doing so will result in the network quarantining of the device
and the user account being disabled. Upon returning to Colorado, the device will be wiped before it
can be returned, and accounts must be re-enabled by the IAM team.

   ●   End User Levels — Table I (Level I: 3.77%, Level II: 90.04%, Level III: 6.19%)

7. Definitions
Desktop Computer: A computer with a separate CPU and monitor with an external keyboard and
mouse; may be a Tower, Small Form Factor (SFF), or Mini PC.
End User: A state employee or any individual provided direct IT services, software, hardware, or
support by OIT.
Laptop Computer: A computer with an optional DVD/CD internal drive, built-in keyboard and mouse,
which folds for travel.
Primary Workstation: The main computer used by a state employee, providing all computer
functions necessary for the job duties of the position.
SSD: Solid State Drive — a solid-state storage device that uses integrated circuit assemblies as
memory to store data persistently.
Tablet: A smaller-profile computer without a built-in keyboard, with a touch screen, running
Windows OS; can be used as a primary device.
Tablet PC: A smaller-profile laptop capable of running Windows OS with a screen that converts or
swivels into a tablet computer.
Kiosk: Any system designated for customer-facing input only (desktop, Chromebook, thin client,
tablet, or other state-owned device). Not a point-of-sale device.
