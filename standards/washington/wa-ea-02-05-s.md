PURPOSE
This standard establishes the statewide service capability, Network Connectivity, as an
enterprise service under EA-02 – Establishing an Enterprise Service Policy. A Washington
state agency must not adopt, build, expand, or renew a service with similar capabilities unless
it has an approved waiver or exemption (see Section 5).

SCOPE
The Network Connectivity capabilities listed in Section 4 must be used to ensure secure,
reliable, centralized, and standardized access to enterprise network resources, systems, and
data.

APPLICABILITY
This standard applies to all state agencies (as defined in RCW 43.105.020) and their
organizational users (including employees, contractors, partners, and vendors) to access
internal state resources.
This standard does not apply to the K-20 network, institutions of higher education,
legislature, judiciary, or local governments.

ENTERPRISE SERVICE CAPABILITIES
The Network Connectivity enterprise service designation encompasses the following
capabilities:
     1. Domain Name Services (DNS) – available to all agencies connected to the State
        Government Network (SGN) for name resolution and authoritative hosting for the
        wa.gov and wa.us domains.
     2. Dedicated Cloud Connectivity – delivers secure, high-speed connectivity to mission-
        critical cloud services to ensure reliable performance and availability.

                                                                                                     1

   3. Network Core Services – the networking infrastructure and technology that provides
      state agencies and other authorized customers connectivity to the state's wide area
      network (WAN) and SGN hosted systems and data.
   4. Transport & Connectivity – provides end to end design, provisioning, implementation,
      and operations support for an agency’s physical and virtual networks.
   5. Wireless – provides secure, centrally managed statewide wireless connectivity that
      enables mobility and productivity using a common, supported infrastructure for your
      agency's network.
   6. Network Time Protocol (NTP) – provides SGN connected agencies with network-
      synchronized time.
   7. Internet Protocol (IP) version 4 and version 6 – network identification and location
      address allocations

WAIVERS AND EXEMPTIONS TO THIS STANDARD
In accordance with POL-01-02-S Technology Policy and Standard Waiver Request Standard,
agencies may qualify for a waiver or exemption from compliance with this standard. Agencies
may request an exemption for situations that meet one of the following criteria.
   1. Exemptions Apply to Transport and Connectivity Capabilities
          a. Fiber optic network connectivity owned by a state agency is exempt.
          b. Isolated or segmented, non-SGN networks used for specialized purposes (e.g.,
             federally regulated, public safety, transportation systems).
   2. Exemptions Apply to Wireless Capability
          a. Wireless networks that do not connect to the SGN.
          b. Wireless networks that require end-to-end encryption (e.g., FIPS).
          c. Wireless networks that require network micro-segregation (e.g., TrustSec).
          d. Wireless networks that require RFID or Bluetooth tracking.

AUTHORITY
This standard is issued under the authority of RCW 43.105.054, which grants Washington
Technology Solutions authority to establish statewide technology standards and enterprise
services, including wireless communications systems. RCW 43.105.006 establishes
Washington Technology Solutions as the centralized provider of broadly used and uniform
technology services. RCW 43.105.450 requires state agencies to comply with statewide
information technology security policies and standards.
