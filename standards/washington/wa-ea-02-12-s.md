1. PURPOSE
This foundational standard establishes the enterprise service framework through which
WaTech develops, delivers, and governs enterprise capabilities supporting implementation
of WA.gov.
WA.gov will leverage the Customer Identity & Access Management service (Enterprise
Standard EA-02-07-S) to provide a single point of access to Washington State government
services and an improved user experience for residents, visitors, businesses and state
agencies.

2. SCOPE
In Scope
This standard applies to all executive agency websites and digital applications that provide
public access to state services.

3. APPLICABILITY
This standard applies to all state agencies (as defined in RCW 43.105.020). This standard
does not apply to the K-20 network, institutions of higher education, legislature, judiciary, or
local governments.

4. ENTERPRISE SERVICE CAPABILITIES
The WA.gov enterprise service broadly encompasses the following capabilities:
    1. Display of the standardized WA.gov navigational component on all agency websites
       and digital applications.
    2. List agency services in the statewide service and agency directory.
    3. Connect agency applications with WA.gov

    Enterprise Implementation Roadmap
    WA.gov Enterprise Service capabilities will be implemented incrementally. Requirements
    will be established through service-specific standards, technical requirements,
    procedures, implementation guidance, service catalogs, and related governance
    documents as those services become operational.

5. WAIVERS AND EXEMPTIONS TO THIS STANDARD
Requests for temporary waivers resulting from phased implementation of WA.gov
capabilities shall be evaluated according to the Enterprise Waiver Process (POL-01-02-S
Technology Policy and Standard Waiver Request Standard).
No categorical exemptions currently apply.

6. AUTHORITY
This standard is issued under the authority of RCW 43.105.054, which grants WaTech
authority to establish statewide technology standards and enterprise services. RCW
43.105.385 establishes WaTech as the central service provider for state agencies, supporting
the delivery of common enterprise technology services. RCW 43.105.450 requires state
agencies to comply with statewide information technology security policies and standards.
Executive Order 25-06 establishes statewide direction for improving customer experience
across Washington state government. This standard establishes the governance framework
for WaTech enterprise services that support agencies in implementing an improved digital
experience for those accessing Washington State’s online services. This standard does not
replace or supersede requirements established by the Executive Order or by the Governor's
Office.
