1. PURPOSE
This standard establishes the statewide service capability, Productivity Suite, as an enterprise
service under EA-02 – Establishing an Enterprise Service Policy. The Productivity Suite
provides a centrally governed, enterprise-level capability for communication, collaboration,
and productivity across Washington state government. It enables consistent, secure, and
efficient interaction among agencies, employees, partners, and external parties.
The Productivity Suite is designated as an enterprise service to reduce duplication, improve
interoperability, strengthen security, and enable statewide collaboration at scale.
 A Washington state agency must not adopt, build, expand, or renew a service with similar
capabilities unless it has an approved waiver or exemption (see Section 5).

2. SCOPE
In Scope
The Productivity Suite must be used for the communication, collaboration, and productivity
capabilities listed in Section 4.
Out of Scope
The following capabilities are not in scope of this standard:
    1. Specialized applications that include embedded collaboration features not intended
         for general productivity use (e.g., law enforcement records management, electronic
         health records management).
    2. Public-facing communication and document sharing platforms used exclusively for
         engagement with external users (e.g., Box, broadcast-only systems, reservation
         systems).
    3. The use of Zoom when necessary for collaboration with external organizations or
         individuals, or when required to accommodate accessibility needs. Such use may

    4. One-time or ad hoc communication methods that do not establish an ongoing

       emergency response, corrections management system, RightFax as part of the DES
       Managed Print Service).
    8. Capabilities related to digital visualization, automation, and development.

3. APPLICABILITY
This standard applies to all state agencies (as defined in RCW 43.105.020) and their
organizational users (including employees, contractors, partners, and vendors).
This standard does not apply to the K-20 network, institutions of higher education,
legislature, judiciary, or local governments.

4. ENTERPRISE SERVICE CAPABILITIES
The Productivity Suite enterprise service designation encompasses the capabilities listed
below. Note that the vendors and products listed represent current examples and are subject
to change.
    1. Email and calendar-based communication (Microsoft Exchange).
    2. Real-time communication using chat, voice, video, or virtual meetings (Microsoft
       Teams).
    3. Document storage and collaboration (Microsoft OneDrive).
    4. Team-based or cross-agency collaboration (Microsoft SharePoint).
    5. Digital workplace tools for word-processing, spreadsheets, and presentations as the
       primary toolset for an agency (Microsoft Office Word, Excel, and PowerPoint).
    6. Electronic faxing functionality, allowing users to send and receive faxes via email,
       computer, tablet, or smartphone (eFax).

5. WAIVERS AND EXEMPTIONS TO THIS STANDARD
In accordance with POL-01-02-S Technology Policy and Standard Waiver Request Standard,
agencies may qualify for a waiver from compliance with this standard. Agencies may request
an exemption for situations that meet one of the following criteria.
The following exemptions apply to electronic faxing capabilities:
    1. High volume fax servers
    2. Fax servers that have document management systems

6. AUTHORITY
This standard is issued under the authority of RCW 43.105.054, which grants WaTech
authority to establish statewide technology standards and enterprise services. RCW
43.105.385 establishes WaTech as the central service provider for state agencies, supporting
the delivery of common enterprise technology services. RCW 43.105.450 requires state
agencies to comply with statewide information technology security policies and standards.
