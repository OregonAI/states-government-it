# 3-101. GIS software. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 3-101)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 3-101, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                          Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                     Technical Standards and Guidelines

3-101. GIS software.
    State agencies shall coordinate all purchases of GIS software and software maintenance
through the Office of the CIO. The Office of the CIO will provide guidance to agencies on GIS
software that is compatible with the state’s enterprise GIS environment.
