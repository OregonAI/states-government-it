# 3-203. Lidar standard. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 3-203)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 3-203, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                                           Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                      Technical Standards and Guidelines

3-203. Lidar standard.
    The commission adopts by reference the most recent version of the Lidar Base Specification
(LBS) standards released by the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) [https://www.usgs.gov/ngp-
standards-and-specifications/lidar-base-specification-online] for elevation acquisition using lidar.
--
