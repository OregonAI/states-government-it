# 7-103. SMTP routing standard. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 7-103)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 7-103, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                                                Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                           Technical Standards and Guidelines

7-103. SMTP routing standard.
   All inbound and outbound SMTP traffic must be routed through the anti-spam and anti-virus
appliance managed by the Office of the CIO.
--
     History: Adopted on June 27, 2007. Amended on March 4, 2008, and July 12, 2018.
