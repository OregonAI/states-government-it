# 3-102. NebraskaMAP portal. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 3-102)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 3-102, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                          Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                     Technical Standards and Guidelines

3-102. NebraskaMAP portal.
   All agency geospatial data and GIS web applications that are available to the public shall be
made accessible through the NebraskaMAP portal.
--
     History: Adopted on November 8, 2018.
