# 5-201. Email standard for state agencies. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 5-201)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 5-201, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                                               Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                          Technical Standards and Guidelines

5-201. Email standard for state agencies.
   All state government agencies, except higher education entities, shall use the email service
provided by the Office of the CIO for their workers.
