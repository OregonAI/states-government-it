# 7-102. DNS forwarding standard. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 7-102)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 7-102, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                                                Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                           Technical Standards and Guidelines

7-102. DNS forwarding standard.
    All outbound internet DNS traffic must be forwarded through the state’s internal DNS
servers.
