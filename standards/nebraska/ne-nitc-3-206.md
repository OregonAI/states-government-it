# 3-206. Address points. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 3-206)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 3-206, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102).

## Full text

                                                                              Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                         Technical Standards and Guidelines

3-206. Address points.
   The commission adopts by reference the most recent version of sections 2, 3, and 3.2 of the
NENA Standard for NG9-1-1 GIS Data Model released by the National Emergency Number
Association [https://www.nena.org/page/ng911gisdatamodel] for GIS data that consists of
address points.
--
     History: Adopted on March 27, 2015. Amended on July 25, 2019 and November 10, 2022.
