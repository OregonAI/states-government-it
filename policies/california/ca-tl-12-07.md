# Suspend Enterprise Architecture Reporting (TL 12-07)

BACKGROUND
Information Technology Policy Letter (ITPL) 09-03 and ITPL 10-15 announced a framework for a
statewide enterprise architecture (EA). The EA framework required departments to provide information
needed for a Business Reference Model (BRM), Service Component Reference Model (SRM), and
Technical Reference Model (TRM). Departments and agencies are required to annually submit updated
EA information to the California Technology Agency by the last Friday of June.

PURPOSE AND DESCRIPTION
The purpose of this Technology Letter (TL) is to suspend the requirement for agencies and
departments to report BRM, TRM and SRM-related information as the Technology Agency reviews the
EA program to optimize its value for agencies, departments, and the state as a whole. Effective with the
release of this Technology Letter, agencies and departments are not required to complete or submit the
Business Template, Services Template or the Technology Template. References to the reporting
requirement will be updated in the Statewide Information Management Manual (SIMM) Section 05A to
reflect the suspension. Revised SIMM Section 05A is included as Attachment A.

PROCESS
Agencies and departments are not required to prepare or provide updated EA reports until further
notice. The Technology Agency will update the statewide EA program in collaboration with agencies,
departments, and the state’s EA community and will provide further instructions in advance of any
required reports.

The Technology Agency delegates to agency or department CIO’s the authorization to retain or create
agency or departmental policies or processes associated with EA reporting.

QUESTIONS
Questions should be directed to your Technology Agency PMO Principal or Manager. A listing of PMO
Managers and Principals and their departmental assignments can be found at the Technology Agency
Web site located at: Technology_Agency_PMO_Assignments.
