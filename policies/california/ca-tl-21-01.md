BACKGROUND

Pursuant to Government Code (GC), Sections 11545 and 11546, the California Department of Technology
(CDT) has the authority and responsibility to oversee and assign an IT Project Cost Delegation to
Agencies/state entities. State Administrative Manual (SAM) Section 4819.39 describes and outlines the
authority that the CDT has to assign project cost thresholds for oversight and reporting purposes.

In an effort to increase autonomy and promote high quality and successful IT projects, the CDT has defined
processes and procedures to determine the Project Cost Delegation, previously known as the Delegated
Cost Threshold. Statewide Information Management Manual (SIMM) Section 15 has been updated to
include a new process whereby Agency/state entities can complete the Project Cost Delegation assessment
if they would like to request a change to their current Project Cost Delegation. Each Agency/state entity is
designated by the CDT a minimum total project development cost based on the completion of the Project
Cost Delegation Accreditation. The Project Cost Delegation Accreditation template should be completed
from the perspective of the Agency/state entity that is requesting the change.

Pursuant to Public Contract Code (PCC) Section 12100 (g) and 12102.2 (e), IT project acquisition (aka
“procurement”) authority is dependent upon Project Cost Delegation. Acquisitions related to delegated IT
projects are conducted under the Department of General Services’ (DGS) procurement authority. SAM
Sections 5200.5 and 5210 describe the acquisition authority for IT goods and services related to IT projects.
Increases to Project Cost Delegations may increase autonomy for project oversight; however, the
acquisitions related to delegated IT projects are subject to DGS oversight and procurement authority. The
process in SIMM Section 15, specifies that CDT will consult with the DGS Procurement Division before
changing the Project Cost Delegation for an Agency/state entity.

PURPOSE

The purpose of this Technology Letter (TL) is to announce:
   •   Updated SIMM Section 15, to include the materials for requesting a change to an Agency’s/state
       entity’s Project Cost Delegation.
   •   New SIMM Section 15A, Project Cost Delegation Accreditation Instructions.
   •   New SIMM Section 15B, Project Cost Delegation Accreditation Template.
   •   New SIMM Section 15C, Project Cost Delegation Historical Projects Worksheet.
   •   New SIMM Section 15D, Project Cost Delegation Executive Transmittal.
   •   Updated SAM Sections 4819.2, 4819.34, 4819.37, 4819.39, 4819.41 and 4942 documenting the
       name change from Delegated Cost Threshold to Project Cost Delegation and updated the
       definition of development cost.

QUESTIONS

Questions regarding this Technology Letter should be directed to your CDT IT Project Oversight
Manager. Assignments can be found at: https://cdt.ca.gov/project-approvals-and-oversight-contact/ or
California Department of Technology, Office of Statewide Project Delivery, Project Approval and
Oversight at ProjectOversight@state.ca.gov.
