BACKGROUND
In 2010, the Department of Technology, in partnership with the Department of Rehabilitation,
issued the IT Accessibility Resource Guide (SIMM Section 25) which includes accessibility
standards that Agencies/state entities must use to comply with IT policy. These standards were
based on the World Wide Web Consortium’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 1.0.
Although SIMM 25 was effective in providing tools to assist web developers, designers, and
content providers to implement accessibility standards, the guide did not align with updated
standards that the World Wide Web Consortium released with WCAG 2.0. Additionally, the
California State Auditor’s Office issued a report on compliance with the Rehabilitation Act of
1973(Report No. 2014-131) which recommended the Department of Technology specify methods
by which state departments should conduct web accessibility testing. As a result of these findings
the Department of Technology, in partnership with the Department of Rehabilitation, performed a
comprehensive review and update of the IT Accessibility Resource Guide.
State law (GC § 11135) directs all Agencies/state entities (in developing, procuring, maintaining, or
using electronic or information technology, either indirectly or through the use of state funds by
other entities) to comply with the accessibility requirements of Section 508 of the federal
Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended (29 U.S.C. Sec. 794d), and regulations implementing that
act as set forth in Part 1194 of Title 36 of the Code of Federal Regulations. In requiring
compliance with Section 508, GC § 11135 mandates that Electronic and Information Technology 1
(EIT) be accessible to individuals with disabilities. GC § 11135 applies the same requirements to
state agencies and employees that Section 508 applies to federal agencies or employees.
Agencies/state entities and contractors working on their behalf are responsible for ensuring that
their Agency/state entity public websites are accessible to the general public, and that their internal
information technology systems are accessible by state employees with disabilities. SAM Section
4833 includes the IT policy in support of regulations on accessibility in state government IT.
PURPOSE
The purpose of this Technology Letter (TL) is to announce:
   •      Revised SIMM Section 25 IT Accessibility Resource Guide to align with new WCAG 2.0.
   •      Revised SIMM Section 25 to include standardized web accessibility testing guidelines and
          methods for conducting accessibility testing on various operating systems, browsers and includes
          information for assistive technologies.
   •      Revised SAM Section 4819.2 to include definitions referenced in the resource guide including: (1)

       1 For the purposes of this policy, “EIT” is limited to IT that is within the scope of authority of the California Department

       of Technology.

       Accessibility/Accessible; (2) Assistive Technology; (3) Computer Accessibility; and (4) Public
       Facing Applications.
