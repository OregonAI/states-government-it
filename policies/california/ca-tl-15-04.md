BACKGROUND

Since 2008, the California Department of Technology (Department of Technology) required each
Agency/state entity1 to develop and submit a five-year IT Capital Plan (ITCP) annually. The annual
ITCPs, along with the corresponding Stage 1 Business Analysis proposals, were used to achieve multi-
agency business and strategic technology planning. Supported proposals were integrated into the
Statewide ITCP to represent the state's plan for strategic IT investments and support the Governor's
Budget. This information was also used to develop an annual California IT Strategic Plan, which guides
the acquisition, management, and use of IT as required by Government Code Section 11545.

SIMM Section 19A contains the Stage 1 Business Analysis which is the first stage of the Project
Approval Lifecycle process and is used to generate the annual Statewide ITCP. The Stage 1 Business
Analysis provides a basis for project management, program management, executive management, and
state-level control agencies to understand and agree on business problems or opportunities, and the
objectives to address them.

The improvements to the Project Approval Lifecycle have resulted in greater emphasis on project
planning and organizational readiness. These enhancements have incorporated much of the
investment prioritization, business planning and strategic integration that the annual ITCP was intended
to achieve at the Agency/state entity level. Changes to the ITCP policies are necessary to better align
this process with the new Project Approval Lifecycle.

PURPOSE
    The purpose of this TL is to announce that:

          Agency/state entities are no longer required to submit an ITCP annually to the Department of
           Technology.
          The Department of Technology will publish, quarterly, an updated Conceptually Approved IT
           Project Proposals report which will incorporate all approved Stage 1 Business Analyses as of
           the quarter end.
          Stage 1 Business Analyses may be submitted any time of the year.
          Stage 1 Business Analysis submissions to the Department of Technology must be completed
           per the instructions in SIMM Section 19A or they will be returned. The link to SIMM is as
           follows:https://cdt.ca.gov/policy/simm/
