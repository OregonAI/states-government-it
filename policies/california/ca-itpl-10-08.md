Executive Order S-03-10

DISTRIBUTION              Agency Secretaries
                          Department Directors
                          Agency Chief Information Officers
                          Department Chief Information Officers

PURPOSE                   Given the current economic circumstances, the state has a compelling
                          need to consolidate information technology (IT) services and operations
                          to minimize overlap, redundancy, and cost in state operations. In
                          addition, there is an increasing demand for broader transparency and
                          accountability in reporting government activities. Accordingly, the
                          purpose of this Information Technology Policy Letter (ITPL) is to:
                               • Establish the Progress Report Scorecard process for reporting
                                 Agency 1 and department progress towards meeting the goals
                                 identified in Executive Order (EO) S-03-10.
                               • Advise Agency Chief Information Officers (Agency CIOS) and
                                 Department Chief Information Officers (CIOs) of their reporting
                                 responsibilities.

BACKGROUND                Within the authority of Government Code Section 11545, the Office of the
                          Chief Information Officer (OCIO) has responsibility and authority for the
                          establishment and enforcement of state IT in California State
                          Government. This includes establishing and enforcing the state’s IT
                          strategic plans, policies, standards and enterprise architecture. Within
                          this responsibility is implementation of EO S-03-10 issued February 9,
                          2010.
                          A major focus of EO S-03-10 concerns the consolidation of IT
                          infrastructure and services. Working with the state’s IT leadership the

1
  When capitalized, the term “Agency” refers to one of the state’s super Agencies such as the State and Consumer
Services Agency or the Health and Human Services Agency. When used in lower case, the term “agency” refers to
any office department, board, bureau, commission or other organizational entity within state government. Within
this ITPL, “agency” and “department” are used interchangeably.

                   OCIO has established the Infrastructure Consolidation Program (ICP),
                   and developed the governance structure and the reporting process to
                   ensure the consolidation goals, timeframes and objectives outlined in the
                   EO are met by agencies within the Executive Branch.
                   Six consolidation areas were identified, and workgroups, chaired by
                   Agency CIOs, were established to implement the consolidation goals. To
                   measure progress towards achieving the EO goals and objectives,
                   Progress Report Scorecards (Scorecards) for each consolidation area
                   were developed.

POLICY AND         Scorecards will be published quarterly but may occur more frequently
PROCESS            when significant progress towards achieving an EO objective is realized.
                   In addition, departments will be identified individually to help focus efforts
                   towards organizations needing additional attention. The OCIO will notify
                   the Governor’s Office, Cabinet Secretaries and the Legislature, and
                   provide copies of the Scorecards.
                   Scorecards are published on the OCIO’s ICP Web site at:
                   http://www.cio.ca.gov/Business/consolidation/default.asp
                   While the Scorecards share a common “look and feel,” the metrics used
                   to prepare them are different for each consolidation area. Accordingly,
                   the Executive Sponsor or OCIO Lead within each consolidation area will
                   work with Agency CIOs and CIOs regarding the process used for
                   collecting and reporting Scorecard metrics. The ICP Web site identifies
                   the Executive Sponsors and OCIO Leads, and also includes associated
                   overview descriptions and governance documents for each consolidation
                   area.
ROLES AND
RESPONSIBILITIES   Agency CIOs shall ensure departments are in compliance with the EO
                   through their department CIOs. Agency-affiliated department CIOs shall
                   implement IT consolidation activities and provide their Agency CIOs with
                   the Scorecard metrics needed to measure compliance with the EO.
                   CIOs who are not affiliated with an Agency have the same responsibilities
                   as Agency CIOs and Agency-affiliated department CIOs.

APPLICABLILITY     This ITPL applies to all state agencies, departments, boards,
                   commissions, offices or other organizational entity under the direct
                   executive authority of the Governor’s Office.

CONTACT            Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Department of
                   Technology, Office of Government Affairs at ITPolicy @state.ca.gov
