Real-time Billing Dispute Policy
  Document ID                      POL-FIN-001
  Title                            Real-time Billing Dispute Policy
  Executive Sponsor                David Edinger, Chief Information Officer & Executive Director
  Issuing Authority                Governor’s Office of Information Technology (OIT)
  Applies to                       All Colorado state agencies utilizing OIT common policy services
                                   and participating in the Real-time Billing (RtB) process.
  Instrument Type                  Policy
  Version                          1.0
  Effective Date                   05/05/2026
  Last Updated                     05/05/2026

1. Purpose
To establish a policy for agencies to dispute Real-time Billing (RtB) charges and request
changes during the billing process, and to ensure open and regular communication between
the Office of Information Technology (OIT) and agencies to identify, track, and resolve RtB
billing and utilization corrections quickly and accurately.

2. Policy
It is the policy of OIT to provide agencies with monthly RtB invoices and data using the RtB
website. Agencies are recommended to confirm that the items billed are being consumed and
utilized by the agency. If corrections are needed, agencies must submit a request to OIT
through the formal dispute form on the RtB website. OIT and agencies must comply with the
responsibilities, processes, and timelines described in the Real-time Billing Dispute
Procedure.

3. Applicability
This policy applies to all Colorado state agencies utilizing OIT common policy services and
participating in the RtB process.
