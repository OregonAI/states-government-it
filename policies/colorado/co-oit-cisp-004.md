CISP-004: Data Security Policy
  Document ID                     CISP-004
  Title                           Data Security Policy
  Executive Sponsor               Jill Fraser, Chief Information Security Officer
  Issuing Authority               Governor’s Office of Information Technology (OIT)
  Applies to                      All public agencies as defined in C.R.S. 24-37.5-102(26). All
                                  contracted parties must comply with these requirements.
  Instrument Type                 Policy
  Version                         3.0
  Effective Date                  07/01/2026
  Last Updated                    03/09/2026

1. Purpose
This policy supports the State of Colorado Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) in
achieving the requirements of the Colorado Information Security Act (C.R.S. 24-37.5-401, et
seq.). It governs the management and protection of State of Colorado data by public agencies
and users.

2. Applicability
This policy applies to all public agencies as defined in C.R.S. 24-37.5-102(26). All contracted
parties must comply with these requirements.

3. Defined Terms
All CISP-specific terms are defined in the CISP Glossary unless otherwise defined in this policy.

4. Policy
All state IT resources, information and data are the sole property of the State of Colorado,
and applicable statutes, policies, and technical standards govern their use. All users must
manage and protect state data in accordance with this policy.

5. Safeguards
Data Protection, Recovery and Sanitization
The technology provider, in coordination with the agency, must establish, maintain and
document data management procedures. At a minimum, data management must address:
   ●      Data Ownership

   ●   Governance
   ●   Management
   ●   Training
   ●   Sharing
   ●   Retention
   ●   Categorization
   ●   Access
   ●   Protection
   ●   Disposal Requirements

IT Environmental Protection and Physical Security
Data centers where a technology provider stores and manages state data must abide by
environmental protection requirements. An agency's physical premises are the responsibility
of the agency. Both must establish, maintain and document IT Environmental Protection and
Physical Security procedures. At a minimum, they must incorporate these physical security
practices:
   ●   Access Control
   ●   Resource Monitoring and Control
   ●   Emergency Infrastructure
   ●   Infrastructure Protection
   ●   Environmental Monitoring
   ●   Damage Protection
   ●   Entry and Exit Management and Logging
   ●   Physical Security
   ●   Protection of Power Equipment and Cabling
   ●   Disaster Emergency Preparation

6. Compliance
Failure of users to comply with this policy may result in, as applicable:
   ●   Loss of federal grant funding, fines, or penalties.
   ●   Agency-level sanctions regarding state IT resources: restriction, deactivation, or
       suspension of the user's account, project, or system, or termination of employment.
   ● Termination of contract or assignment for temporary workers, interns, volunteers,
       contractors, or vendors who violate this policy.
Non-consolidated agencies must have appropriate policies and/or procedures to ensure
compliance with this policy and all other Colorado Information Security Policies.

7. Exceptions
Any exceptions to this policy must be documented, justified, and approved in advance of
implementation. Requestors must submit a Secure Configuration Exception Request.
