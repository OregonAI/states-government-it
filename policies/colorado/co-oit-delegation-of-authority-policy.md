# Delegation of Authority Policy (POL-ENT-001)

## At a glance

OIT's policy for delegation of authority for certain information technology functions from OIT to another state agency (the purpose the PDF states). Listed by OIT under Current Statewide Policies, Enterprise Governance; the procedure that carries it out is PROC-ENT-001.

1. Purpose
To establish the Governor's Office of Information Technology (OIT) policy for delegation of
authority for certain information technology functions from OIT to another state agency.

2. Policy
It is the policy of OIT, within the sole discretion and judgment of the State Chief Information
Officer, to delegate information technology functions from OIT to another state agency in
accordance with C.R.S. 24-37.5-105.4. For an information technology function to receive
delegated authority, it must have its own delegation of authority request approved, and a
corresponding delegation of authority interagency agreement must be fully executed by both
the State Chief Information Officer and the agency's Executive Director.

3. Applicability
This policy applies to state agencies as defined in C.R.S. 24-37.5-102(28) as “all of the
departments, divisions, commissions, boards, bureaus, and institutions in the executive
branch of the state government” — not including the legislative or judicial departments, the
department of education, the department of law, the department of state, the department of
the treasury, or state-supported institutions of higher education.

5. Definitions
Reference the Delegation of Authority Procedure for definitions.

6. Responsibilities
Reference the Delegation of Authority Procedure for responsibilities.

7. Compliance
An agency does not have authority to assume responsibility for, or exercise authority over,
any information technology function until the associated delegation of authority interagency
agreement is fully executed. Failure to comply with this policy and follow the associated
Delegation of Authority Procedure may risk placing the requesting agency, OIT and/or the
State out of statutory and/or legal compliance.

8. Expiration
This policy remains in effect until superseded or rescinded by the State Chief Information
Officer & Executive Director of OIT.
