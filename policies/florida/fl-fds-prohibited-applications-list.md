# Prohibited Applications on Government Devices List (Prohibited Applications on Government Devices List (April 30, 2026))

## At a glance

The list the Department of Management Services, through the Florida Digital Service, determined under s. 112.22(1)(f), F.S., and rule 60GG-2.008, F.A.C.: 41 applications, among them AI applications (DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax Agent, Z.AI AI Agent App, StepFun AI Assistant and others), each with the date it was added. Public employers must keep them off government devices and evaluate applications not listed. Linked from the Prohibited Applications List page as 'Prohibited Applications List 04/30/2026'; the Florida Digital Service publishes it quarterly.
