# Geospatial Information Systems Policy (Commonwealth of Pennsylvania IT Policy: Geospatial Information Systems Policy)

## At a glance

Information Technology Policy of the Office of Administration for agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction. Printed purpose: This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the policy and standards for Geospatial Information Systems (GIS) to increase interoperability and reduce duplication of capabilities and investments across agencies. Computer Aided Design (CAD) software and Global Positioning System (GPS) equipment are not covered by this policy

## Full text

1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This document replaces, in its entirety, the Geospatial Information Systems Policy, dated January 6,
2025.

3. Purpose
This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the policy and standards for Geospatial
Information Systems (GIS) to increase interoperability and reduce duplication of capabilities
and investments across agencies. Computer Aided Design (CAD) software and Global
Positioning System (GPS) equipment are not covered by this policy

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

Agencies requiring geospatial software shall only procure software that meets the current product
standards as defined within Geospatial Information Systems Standard.

Prior to procuring new servers, databases, data storage, or software for geospatial applications, an
agency should contact its OA/IT Service Coordinator to determine if the agency’s needs can be
accommodated through the Geospatial Enterprise Server Architecture (GESA) in order to
save costs and leverage enterprise geospatial server resources.

An approved IT policy exception is required prior to any new procurement for geospatial data

6. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

7. Exception from Policy
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this policy, a request for a policy
exception shall be submitted via the enterprise IT policy exception process. Refer to IT Policy
Governance Policy for guidance.
