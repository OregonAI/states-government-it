# Minimum Contractor Background Checks Policy (Commonwealth of Pennsylvania IT Policy: Minimum Contractor Background Checks Policy)

## At a glance

Information Technology Policy of the Office of Administration for agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction. Printed purpose: This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the enterprise-wide policy of minimum background check requirements for contractors.

## Full text

             Minimum Contractor Background Checks Policy

       Effective Date:                                        Category:
       June 10, 2026                                          Security

       Scheduled Review:
       June 2027

1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This document replaces, in its entirety, the Minimum Contractor Background Checks Policy,
dated January 6, 2025.

3. Purpose
This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the enterprise-wide policy of minimum
background check requirements for contractors.

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

Service Organizations, as a condition preceding award of a contract or purchase order, shall
conduct a pre-employment background check on every contractor including those within its
Subservice Organizations, who will:

 •   Perform services onsite at Commonwealth owned or leased facilities or
 •   Have access to Commonwealth infrastructure, including facilities (owned or leased) or
     information systems via onsite or remote access or
 • Have access to Commonwealth data.
For contractors who continue to meet any of the conditions listed above for more than
one year, the Service Organization shall conduct such background checks on an annual
basis.

Pre-employment background checks of prospective contractors shall include, at a
minimum, verification of identity, employment history, and criminal record review. The
criminal record review shall include checks that screen against state and local systems
containing information on criminal arrests, convictions, warrants, and incarcerations.

The Service Organization, or its Subservice Organizations, shall have a criminal
background check conducted in the state where the contractor currently resides. For
Contracted Resources who reside within the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, requests
for criminal record checks shall be made to the Pennsylvania State Police.

For contractors who have or who will have access to the Pennsylvania Commonwealth
Law Enforcement Assistance Network , Criminal Justice Information, Federal Tax
Information, or any Criminal History Record Information data, by either onsite or remote
computer access, a pre-employment background check shall also include a fingerprint
database search. The type of fingerprint search required shall be determined by the
applicable governing policy or regulation (e.g., FBI CJIS Security Policy, IRS
Publication 1075) and shall be performed in compliance with those requirements.
Recertification shall be maintained in accordance with the applicable policy or regulation.

For more information on background checks, refer to:

 •   IRS Publication 1075
 •   CJIS Security Policy
 •   Request for Criminal Record Check
 •   Out of State Background Check

Service Organizations and Subservice Organizations with contractors providing services
from a location outside of the United States, as permitted by an approved exception to the
Information Security Policy, shall provide background checks performed by an
internationally recognized service per the terms of the applicable contract or purchase
order. The Commonwealth retains the right to reject background checks that are deemed
insufficient or incomplete.

Service Organizations shall provide copies of pre-employment background checks to the
Contract Manager or appropriate agency personnel.

Subject to the terms of the applicable contract and all applicable laws and regulations,
agencies may determine whether the results of a background check preclude a contractor
from being granted access to agency facilities or data.

The Service Organization, or their Subservice Organization(s), shall be responsible for the
payment of all fees associated with background checks and fingerprinting of their respective
contractors and subcontracted employees.

In addition to the requirements above, the following language, or substantially similar, shall
be included in all Commonwealth procurement vehicles and documents, including, but not
limited to: Requests for Proposal, Invitation for Bids, Invitations to Qualify, Requests for
Quote, sole-source contracts, renewals, amendments for services to be performed by Service
Organization’s personnel or subcontractors onsite at Commonwealth owned or leased
facilities, via onsite or remote computer access to Commonwealth infrastructure, or who
have access to Commonwealth data:

   The Service Organization shall, at its expense, arrange for a background check for each
   of its employees, as well as for the employees of its subcontractors, who will have access
   to Commonwealth infrastructure, to include facilities (owned or leased) and information
   systems, either through onsite or remote access or who will have access to Commonwealth
   data.

   Background checks shall be conducted via the Request for Criminal Record Check for
   in- state contractors or via a criminal background check through the appropriate State
   Agency for the employees residing out of state. The background check shall be completed
   before the employee is granted access and annually thereafter.

In addition, if any additional background check requirements are required by law (e.g., IRS
Publication 1075 background checks), those background check requirements must also be
included in the governing terms and conditions.

   Before the Commonwealth grants a contractor access to Commonwealth owned or leased
   facilities or Commonwealth data and annually thereafter, the Service Organization shall
   provide copies of background checks to the appropriate contract manager or office
   designated by the agency that the required background check(s). If, at any time, it is
   discovered that a contractor has a criminal record that includes a felony or misdemeanor
   involving terrorist threats, violence, use of a lethal weapon, or breach of trust/fiduciary
   responsibility; or which raises concerns about building, system, or personal security, or
   is otherwise job-related, the Service Organization shall not:

     •    assign that employee to any Commonwealth facilities,

     •   provide that employee any access privileges and remove any access privileges
         already given to the employee, and

     •   permit that employee remote access to Commonwealth facilities or systems or
         access to any Commonwealth data, unless the agency consents, in writing, prior to
         the access being provided.

   The agency may withhold its consent at its sole discretion. Failure of the Service
   Organization to comply with the terms of this paragraph may result in default of the
   Service Organization under its contract with the Commonwealth.

6. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

7. Exception from Policy
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this policy, a request for a policy
exception shall be submitted via the IT policy exception process. Refer to IT Policy
Governance Policy for guidance.
