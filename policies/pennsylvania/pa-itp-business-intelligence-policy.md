# Business Intelligence Policy (Commonwealth of Pennsylvania IT Policy: Business Intelligence Policy)

## At a glance

Information Technology Policy of the Office of Administration for agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction. Printed purpose: This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the enterprise-wide policy and standards for Business Intelligence (BI) infrastructure, reporting, and dashboards.

## Full text

                           Business Intelligence Policy

       Effective Date:                                         Category:
       November 18, 2025                                       Information

       Scheduled Review:
       November 2026

1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This document replaces, in its entirety, the Business Intelligence Policy, dated January 6, 2025.

3. Purpose
This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the enterprise-wide policy and standards
for Business Intelligence (BI) infrastructure, reporting, and dashboards.

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

This policy treats BI separately from data warehousing. (For Data warehousing see the Data
Management Policy).

Reports or Dashboards that are part of out-of-the-box application functionality and are not
leveraging separate BI analytics solutions are not in the scope of this policy.

Agencies shall coordinate with the Office of Administration, Office for Information Technology
(OA/IT) to ensure that all Business Intelligence (BI) solutions, reports, and dashboards are
implemented with comprehensive security and process controls that uphold the confidentiality,
integrity, and availability of data in accordance with the data owner’s requirements. This
includes conducting thorough security requirement analyses, applying appropriate security
controls, and ensuring that all users are appropriately authenticated and authorized.

Access to BI reports, dashboards, and data sources must be restricted to only those users who are
entitled to view them. In instances where authentication is required for BI implementation, Agencies
shall leverage the enterprise access management solutions as outlined in the Access Management
Policy.

Agencies shall coordinate with their Agency Chief Counsel and OA/IT to ensure all BI
solution, report, and dashboard implementations comply with all applicable statutes, laws,
regulations, privacy requirements, and policies.

Agencies requiring BI solutions, reports, or dashboards shall use one of the current standards as
defined in Business Intelligence Standards.

Agencies shall not produce BI reports or dashboards for public consumption that contain
personally identifiable information or other closed records as documented in the Data
Classification Policy.

Agencies shall ensure only the data required for their business process is collected and retained
per the agency record retention plan.

Agencies shall review and update their Agency records retention plan with every
implementation of BI Reporting in accordance with Management Directive 210.5 Amended,
The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania State Records Management Program.

In the implementation of a reporting solution, application performance and operational
database server utilization shall be analyzed for current and anticipated future levels to
determine if the reporting solution requires a dedicated data store.

For BI solutions that have AI integrations or capabilities, agencies shall refer to the Artificial
Intelligence Policy.

Additionally, Agencies shall ensure:

   •   Applications leveraging a BI Dashboard provide consistent data and metadata for
       KPIs or Metrics.
   •   Data that supports the KPIs or Metrics displayed in BI Dashboards are synchronized
       with the underlying detail data that is used for drill-down analysis.
   •   BI Dashboard implementations comply with any applicable data
       suppression requirements.

6. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

7. Exception from Policy
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this Standard, a request for an
exception shall be submitted via the enterprise IT Policy Exception process. Refer to IT Policy
Governance Policy for guidance.
