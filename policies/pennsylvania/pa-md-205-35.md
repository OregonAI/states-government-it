# Information Reporting from the Commonwealth's Enterprise Business Information Warehouse System (Management Directive 205.35)

## At a glance

Policy for planning, managing and using the enterprise Business Warehouse (BW) reporting system, including how agencies request new reports.

## Full text

                          Management Directive
                          Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
                          Governor’s Office

Management Directive 205.35 Amended – Information Reporting
  from the Commonwealth's Enterprise Business Information
                     Warehouse System

Date:                 June 12, 2024

By Direction of:
                      Neil R. Weaver, Secretary of Administration

Contact Agency:       Office of Administration
                      Office for Information Technology
                      Telephone: 717.787.5440

This directive establishes policy, responsibilities, and procedures for users of the
Commonwealth's Integrated Enterprise System (IES). This amendment updates
definitions, responsibilities, and procedures, and revises the policy to ensure the
proper and cost-effective use of existing technology and to streamline the process
for requesting new reports or changes to existing reports.

1.      PURPOSE.

        To establish policy, responsibilities, and procedures for the planning, management,
        and utilization of the Commonwealth's Business Warehouse (BW) system.

2.      SCOPE.

        This directive applies to all departments, offices, boards, commissions, and councils
        under the Governor’s jurisdiction and to independent agencies with access to or
        which utilize the Commonwealth's BW system (hereinafter referred to as “agencies”).

3.      OBJECTIVE.

        To ensure that IT Resources made available through the Commonwealth's BW
        system are utilized in an effective and efficient manner.

4.    DEFINITIONS.

      a.     Agency Business Information Users. Users who benefit from having
             insight into the data that is captured in IES, including but not limited to Data
             Analysts, Executives, Business Users, and users in the Integrated Enterprise
             System Office (IESO).

      b.     Business Information Warehouse or Business Warehouse (BW). The
             data repository for the Commonwealth’s ERP system.

      c.     Business Process Owner (BPO)– Designated individual(s) within an agency
             that make decisions and assign priorities for work efforts within IES business
             modules.

      d.     Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) – A system of integrated software
             applications that is used to integrate core business processes (i.e., finance,
             human resources, payroll, procurement, plant maintenance, real estate, sales
             and distribution, etc.).

      e.     Help Desk Ticket - A request submitted by a user or a request submitted by
             a Help Desk employee on behalf of a caller to receive assistance for IT related
             questions or problems.

      f.     Integrated Enterprise System (IES). The Commonwealth's enterprise
             transaction processing system, which serves as the processing system for all
             business transactions in support of enterprise business processes. With
             respect to this directive, the IES System is the source for reports of a
             transactional, non-strategic nature.

      g.     Information Technology (IT) Resources. Equipment or interconnected
             systems or subsystems of equipment, networks, or services used to receive,
             input, store, process, manipulate, control, manage, transmit, display and/or
             output information, including, but not limited to: computers, mobile devices,
             servers, telephones, fax machines, copiers, printers, Internet, Intranet, email,
             ancillary equipment, software, firmware, cloud-based services, systems,
             networks, platforms, plans and data, training materials and documentation
             and social media websites.

5.    POLICY.

      It is the policy of the Commonwealth to ensure that users of information from the
      Commonwealth's BW system are made aware of and comply with the following
      requirements for the efficient and effective utilization of IT Resources.

      a.     In order to minimize duplication in the delivery of information, agencies shall
             leverage existing IT Resources instead of creating or procuring separate
             reporting systems.

      b.    Agency Business Information Users, in collaboration with the appropriate
            BPO, shall work with the IES team, to identify business information reporting
            needs and ensure the solution meets the needs.

6.    RESPONSIBILITIES.

      a.    Secretary of Administration shall, in conjunction with the Deputy Secretary
            for Information Technology, issue and be the sole interpreter of all enterprise
            information reporting policy and procedures governing the planning,
            management, and utilization of IT Resources supported by the
            Commonwealth's BW system.

      b.    Office of Administration, IESO shall develop procedures, guidelines, and
            standards for all reporting from the Commonwealth's BW system.

      c.    Agency Business Information User shall, in collaboration with the
            appropriate BPO, work with the IES team, to identify business information
            reporting needs and ensure the solution meets the needs.

7.    PROCEDURES.

      a.    Agency Business Information Users.

            (1)    Determine that a report is needed to perform their job more efficiently
                   and effectively, and that no existing report, in IES or BW, will fulfill
                   their need.

            (2)    Input the formal request document, as identified by the IES team
                   member, utilizing the:

                   (a)    Agency Help Desk Ticket for changes to existing reports; or

                   (b)    Project Intake Form (PIF) for new report requests.

      b.    IES, BW Team.

            (1)    Receives and reviews the request for the reporting requirement.

            (2)    Ensures the BPO is aware of the request.

            (3)    Contacts the requestor to discuss requirements and determine if a
                   change to an existing report or development of a new report is
                   required.

            (4)    Reviews the request with the appropriate BPO for prioritization.

            (5)   If the reporting requirement can be met with existing reports, informs
                  the Agency Business Information User of the appropriate report. If a
                  change to an existing report or new report is required, initiates the
                  development process.

            (6)   Notifies the Agency Business Information User when the report is
                  available for use.

This directive replaces, in its entirety, Management Directive 205.35 Amended,
