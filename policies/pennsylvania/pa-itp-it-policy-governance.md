# IT Policy Governance (Commonwealth of Pennsylvania IT Policy: IT Policy Governance)

## At a glance

Information Technology Policy of the Office of Administration for agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction. Printed purpose: This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the governance structure and lifecycle for IT Policies. It also establishes the policy for exceptions to published ITPs.

1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This policy replaces, in its entirety, IT Policy Governance, dated April 7, 2025.

3. Purpose
This Information Technology Policy (ITP) establishes the governance structure and lifecycle for
IT Policies. It also establishes the policy for exceptions to published ITPs.

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance, assigns the Office
of Administration, Information Technology (OA/IT) enterprise-wide governance responsibility
for the planning, acquisition, and management of Information Technology (IT) resources,
including the development and publication of IT policies, standards, procedures, and guidelines.

Management Directive 245.13, Strategic Direction for Information Technology Investments,
establishes Information Technology Policies as the formal means by which OA/IT defines,
communicates, and enforces enterprise IT requirements and expectations.

Agencies may establish their own policies that are more restrictive than an Enterprise IT policy
but may not circumvent the controls or requirements described in Enterprise IT policies.

   5.1 IT Policy Lifecycle
   All IT policies shall be reviewed every year by the Subject Matter Experts (SMEs). The
   review shall occur within 12 months from the last IT policy publication date. It is the
   responsibility of the IT Policy Coordinator and SMEs to conduct the annual review of the IT
   policy. Reviews of individual policies may be performed more frequently if a need arises.
   All reviews are to be documented in the IT policy’s revision table including reviews that
   result in no revisions to the policy.

   5.2 IT Policy Governance Framework

        5.2.1 Governance, Risk, and Compliance Team
        The OA/IT Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Team is responsible for the
        governance of IT Policies and related documents. This includes the creation and
        revision of IT policies as well as ownership of the review and approval process.

        5.2.2 Policy Review Committee
        The Policy Review Committee (PRC) provides oversight and guidance regarding IT
        policies in support of IT supplies and services within the Commonwealth. The Policy
        Review Committee establishes formal structures, membership, decision rights, roles, and
        responsibilities. This Committee is established and maintained by OA/IT for the purpose
        of conducting business related to IT policies.

        5.2.3 OA/IT Senior Leadership
        The Commonwealth CIO, Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), GRC Head or a
        designee will review and provide final approval or disapproval of IT policies.

   5.3 IT Policy Exceptions
   While, adherence to ITPs is required in order to protect the Commonwealth’s IT Resources and
   mitigate business and IT risks, there may be circumstances where an IT policy exception is
   needed for a specific period. In these instances, OA/IT has an IT Policy Exception process in
   place, which allows agencies to request an exception from the requirements of a specific ITP.

   Agencies shall submit an IT Policy Exception request when they are not able to comply with the
   requirements of an ITP, or when advised as necessary by OA/IT Senior Leadership. Agencies
   requiring an IT Policy Exception shall review and adhere to the process detailed in IT Policy
   Exception Procedure.
