# Information Security Policy (Commonwealth of Pennsylvania IT Policy: Information Security Policy)

## At a glance

Information Technology Policy of the Office of Administration for agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction. Printed purpose: This policy establishes the Commonwealth’s enterprise information security program to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Commonwealth IT resources and data in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and recognized security standards.

1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Document Control
This document replaces, in its entirety, the Information Security Policy, dated January 6, 2025.

3. Purpose
This policy establishes the Commonwealth’s enterprise information security program to protect the
confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Commonwealth IT resources and data in accordance with
applicable laws, regulations, and recognized security standards.

4. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network (hereinafter
referred to as "agencies").

5. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

   5.1 Information Security Governance
   The Office of Administration, Office for Information Technology (OA/OIT) Enterprise
   Information Security Office (EISO) shall establish, maintain, and continuously improve the
   Commonwealth's enterprise information security program.

   The Commonwealth's information security program shall align with recognized security
   standards and frameworks, including applicable National Institute of Standards and
   Technology (NIST) publications and other industry-recognized cybersecurity practices, as
   appropriate.

   Information security requirements and controls shall be selected, implemented, and maintained
   using a risk-based approach that considers business objectives, legal and regulatory
   requirements, data sensitivity, system criticality, threats, vulnerabilities, and potential impacts
   to Commonwealth operations.

   Agencies shall comply with applicable Commonwealth information security policies and
   standards issued by OA/OIT.

   Agencies are responsible for implementing applicable security requirements, maintaining
   compliance with Commonwealth information security policies and standards, and ensuring
   that agency-managed systems, services, and information are protected commensurate with
   risk.

   In the absence of a specific Commonwealth policy or standard addressing a particular security
   requirement, agencies shall implement appropriate security measures based on recognized
   industry best practices and applicable NIST guidance until Commonwealth requirements are
   established.

   Agencies shall protect Commonwealth IT resources and data throughout their lifecycle,
   including planning, acquisition, development, implementation, operation, maintenance, and
   disposal.

   Agencies shall implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to
   protect Commonwealth IT resources and data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure,
   disruption, modification, or destruction.

   Agencies shall take timely corrective action to address identified noncompliance, security
   findings, vulnerabilities, audit findings, control deficiencies, and risks.

    5.2 Supporting Information Security Policies, Standards, Procedures, and Guidelines
   This policy establishes the high-level governance requirements for the Commonwealth's
   enterprise information security program. In addition to this policy, OA/OIT maintains several
   other security related policies and standards that collectively define the Commonwealth's
   information security requirements. Agencies shall comply with all applicable Commonwealth
   IT policies and standards.

   5.3 Compliance and Enforcement
   Agencies shall comply with this policy and supporting information security standards and
   procedures.

   Agencies shall follow the enterprise IT policy exception process when an exception to applicable
   policy or standard is required.

   Appropriate action may be taken when non-compliance with Commonwealth policies or
   Management Directives results in, or is suspected of resulting in, loss, damage, or unauthorized
   disclosure of information.

6. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

7. Exception from Policy
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this policy, a request for a policy
exception shall be submitted via the IT policy exception process. Refer to IT Policy Governance
Policy for guidance.
