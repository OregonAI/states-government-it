# IT Configuration Management Policy (Commonwealth of Pennsylvania IT Policy: IT Configuration Management Policy)

## At a glance

Information Technology Policy of the Office of Administration for agencies under the Governor's jurisdiction. Printed purpose: This policy establishes requirements for secure and consistent configuration management of Commonwealth IT resources, aligned with recognized standards, to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Commonwealth information systems.

## Full text

                   IT Configuration Management Policy

           Effective Date:                                        Category:
           October 1, 2026                                        Security

           Scheduled Review:
           October 2027

1. Authority
Executive Order 2016-06, Enterprise Information Technology Governance

2. Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for secure and consistent configuration management of
Commonwealth IT resources, aligned with recognized standards, to protect the confidentiality,
integrity, and availability of Commonwealth information systems.

3. Scope
This policy applies to all offices, departments, boards, commissions, and councils under the
Governor’s jurisdiction and any other entity connecting to the Commonwealth Network
(hereinafter referred to as "agencies").
4. Policy
For definitions of terms found within this document, refer to the IT Policy Glossary.

This policy applies to all Commonwealth IP-enabled hardware and/or software assets.
Agencies shall ensure compliance with this policy and the associated IT Configuration
Management Technology Standard within twelve (12) months of the effective date. Agencies
that are unable to comply after the twelve (12) month implementation period shall document the
non-compliance through a policy exception and approved IT Risk Acknowledgement Form, in
accordance with the enterprise IT policy waiver and risk acknowledgement processes.

   4.1 Configuration Identification and Baselines
   Baseline configurations shall reflect secure-by-default principles and implement least
   functionality appropriate to the system’s purpose and operational requirements.

   Agencies shall align their baseline configurations with Commonwealth-approved

benchmarks as defined in the IT Configuration Management Technology Standard and
applicable regulatory or statutory requirements (e.g., CJIS, IRS, PCI), where required.

Baseline configurations shall be:

•   Reviewed at least annually and upon significant system, environmental, or threat
    landscape changes; and
•   Protected from unauthorized modification.

Approved baselines shall serve as the authoritative reference point for configuration change
management, monitoring, and compliance validation.

4.2 Configuration Change Management
Configuration changes shall comply with the established change management policy and
processes detailed in the Change Management Policy.

As part of the change management process, agencies are responsible for ensuring
configuration changes are assessed based on their security impact and Risk.

Agencies shall restrict configuration and change privileges to authorized personnel based on
defined roles and responsibilities. Access shall be granted in accordance with the principle of
least privilege and shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued appropriateness.

4.3 Monitoring and Auditing
Agencies are recommended to use automated tools, where feasible, to monitor configuration
compliance and detect unauthorized changes.
Identified deviations from approved baseline configurations shall be documented, assessed
for risk, and remediated within the same timeframes established in the Commonwealth
Enterprise Patching Policy for the applicable asset classification.
Agencies shall maintain configuration compliance metrics and provide reporting as required
by OA/IT.

4.4 Asset and Configuration Inventory & Classification
Agencies shall maintain authoritative inventories of all IP-enabled hardware and/or software
assets subject to Commonwealth configuration management requirements.

Where possible configuration items shall be documented within a configuration management
database (CMDB) or other enterprise tools linking assets to:
•   Approved baselines
•   System owners
•   Operational environments
•   Interconnections
•   Change history

Inventories shall be updated during installation, removal, and modification activities.

   4.5 Configuration Documentation and Record Retention
   Agencies shall retain configuration documentation and related change records in accordance
   with applicable records retention schedules in accordance with the Records Management
   Policy.

   4.6 Third-Party Service Providers Managing Cloud Configurations
   The requirements of this policy extend to third-party providers, contractors, and cloud
   services that host or handle Commonwealth data and systems. Agencies must ensure that
   external service providers are managing configurations with equal rigor and in alignment
   with this policy.

5. Contact
Questions or comments may be directed via email to OA, IT Policy.

6. Exceptions
In the event an agency chooses to seek an exception from this policy, a request for a policy
waiver shall be submitted via the enterprise IT policy waiver process. Refer to IT Policy
Governance Policy for guidance.
