I. AUTHORITY

   Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

   The State of Idaho must continually embrace up-to-date telecommunication practices
   and technology to receive maximum benefit and affordability of services. Statewide
   contracts for telecommunication systems, as well as local and long-distance services are
   centrally maintained and represent considerable savings when utilized across State
   agencies.

III. DEFINITIONS

   There are no definitions for this policy.

IV. POLICY

   State agencies are required to use the Office of Information Technology Services (ITS)’s
   central statewide system, contracts and services as well as local and long-distance
   service contracts for voice and other telecommunication services. State agencies shall
   share systems and services whenever determined feasible and cost effective by the
   Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) in conjunction with ITA.

   The Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) shall assign a SME to review and
   approve the purchase of telecommunications equipment.

V. EXEMPTION PROCESS

   Refer to ITA Policy P1010 (Information Technology Policies, Standards, and Guidelines
   Framework).

VI. PROCEDURE REFERENCE

   There are no procedure references for this policy.

VII. CONTACT INFORMATION

   For more information, contact the ITA Staff at (208) 605-4064.

VIII. INTENT

   The Office of Information Technology Services (ITS) shall coordinate and manage:

   1. Purchase and Installation of telecommunications systems and services;
   2. Manage move/add/changes to telecommunications systems for all agencies and
      institutions statewide.
