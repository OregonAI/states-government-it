I. AUTHORITY

   Authority: Idaho Code § 67-831 through § 67-833

II. ABSTRACT

   This policy establishes a structured framework for aligning IT strategies with
   organizational goals, ensuring efficient resource allocation, risk management,
   compliance, and adaptability to technological changes.

III. DEFINITIONS

   There are no definitions for this policy.

IV. POLICY

   State agencies shall submit an annual Agency IT Plan to ITA by July 15 each year. This
   plan should outline feasible and cost effective IT initiatives and/or projects that align with
   the agency’s business plan, budget requests, and technology goals.

   The IT Plan must document IT initiatives and/or projects on a four (4) year planning
   horizon with annual updates to account for legislative mandates, budgets, funding
   sources, and emerging technologies. The plan should also note material changes to
   prior year plans.

   ITA and the Division of Financial Management (DFM) will review the IT Plan for
   alignment with statewide IT strategies. ITA may select specific projects for formal review
   and approval, per ITA Policy P2030 (Information Technology Large-Scale Review) .

   If new IT initiatives arise outside of the annual plan submission, agencies must promptly
   update their project list. Any separately submitted projects may require additional details,
   as requested by ITA.

   The IT plan must be submitted electronically via the Office of Information Technology
   Services (ITS) online system.

V. EXEMPTION PROCESS

   Refer to ITA Policy P1010 (Information Technology Policies, Standards, and Guidelines
   Framework).

VI. PROCEDURE REFERENCE

   Criteria for the annual Agency IT Plan and plan development are detailed in ITA
   Guideline G110 (Agency IT Plan). Project profiles are addressed in ITA Policy P2030
   (Information Technology Large-Scale Project Review) and ITA Guideline G210 (IT
   Project Profile).

VII. CONTACT INFORMATION

   For more information, contact the ITA Staff at (208) 605-4064.
