## Full text

IT-02 Information Technology Strategic Investment Planning

State of Ohio Administrative Policy
No: IT-02 - Information Technology
Effective: April 15, 2026
Issued By: Kathleen C. Madden, Director

I. Purpose
This policy addresses state requirements for information technology (IT) strategic investment planning, which ensures alignment with the priorities of the Administration, as well as IT-related budgeting and purchasing requirements. All defined terms are capitalized and a link to the glossary of definitions is found in section IV.

II. Scope
This policy applies to all state Agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies). The director of budget and management may prescribe participation in the IT Strategic Investment planning process from Agencies that are typically exempt from the policy authority of the state chief information officer (CIO) as part of the process for establishing capital and operating budgets. In such cases, the director of budget and management must establish such participation through memorandum.

III. Policy
Agencies must deliberately plan IT program funding requests. Such IT Strategic Investment Plans must form the basis for budget requests for IT-related efforts. The IT Strategic Investment and Architecture team (the SIA team) within the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) Office of Information Technology (OIT), must administer a state IT Strategic Investment planning program in which Agencies must participate.

A. IT Strategic Investment Plan Submission
Agencies must prepare and submit an IT Strategic Investment Plan each fiscal year in conjunction with the development of budget requests. IT Strategic Investment Plans must be submitted in the form and manner prescribed by the SIA team. Each Agency’s plan must become a part of the Enterprise Planning Portfolio (EPP), which will be used to identify opportunities for shared services, collaboration, technology standardization, and volume purchases.
1. IT Strategic Investment Plans must contain those strategic initiatives, ideas, and/or projects that align with the EPP Decision Making Framework (refer to Section VI for the location of the EPP Decision Making Framework).
2. Agencies must review and, if needed, refresh IT Strategic Investment Plans quarterly. Agencies must also promptly revise the IT Strategic Investment Plan after any changes in IT program funding or scope, such as, but not limited to:
a. Changes in funding allocation or allotment;
b. Changes in the IT Project Portfolio; or
c. Changes in the Application and IT Infrastructure Portfolio.
3. Also on a quarterly basis, Agencies must prioritize newly added IT Strategic Investments using the standard Criteria for Prioritization and Rating Scale (refer to Section VI).
4. If the submitting Agency has a project within their IT Strategic Investment Plan that qualifies as a "major project," as defined in Ohio Administrative Policy IT-16 Major Project Governance, then the Agency must also follow the requirements outlined in Ohio Administrative Policy IT-16.

B. Alignment with Administration Priorities
In consultation with the governor, lieutenant governor, director of budget and management, and director of DAS, the state CIO must identify priorities for the state and may require Agencies to incorporate such priorities into their IT Strategic Investment Plan.
1. The SIA team must communicate the Administration’s priorities to Agencies (refer to Section VI for additional resources).
2. Agencies must use this information during portfolio prioritization.

C. Alignment with the Operating Budget and Capital Budget
In consultation with the director of budget and management, the state CIO may review and approve IT Strategic Investment Plans as part of an Agency’s overall operating and capital budget requests. IT Strategic Investment Plans must serve as the budget narratives for Agency IT program funding requests for both operating and capital budgets.

D. Alignment with Purchasing
Agencies must ensure that requests for IT purchases are made in accordance with their IT Strategic Investment Plans and state purchasing statutes, rules, and policies (including Ohio Administrative Policies PM-01 Purchasing Goods and Services and PM-02 Emergency Purchases).
1. The SIA team must review Agency IT purchase requests to help validate alignment with IT Strategic Investment Plans.
2. Based on the nature of the request, the SIA must identify the appropriate DAS Service Owner and ensure they participate in the review process (e.g., artificial intelligence, cloud, data governance, enterprise shared solutions, information security and privacy, infrastructure services, etc.). The Service Owner input will help to ensure alignment with state policies and standards prior to purchasing decisions.

E. State IT Strategic Investment Process
The SIA team is responsible for the following statewide support activities:
1. Administering Ohio's IT strategic investment and architecture process, including investment planning, services planning (e.g., identification of shared service opportunities or shared service enhancements), identifying collaboration opportunities and conducting multi-Agency working groups or innovation committees, analyzing current and future IT needs, and providing support for Agencies in terms of training and consultation.
2. Maintaining an IT strategic investment and architecture framework and process, instructing Agencies as to the requirements, and providing timely notification of any relevant changes (refer to Section VI for additional resources).
3. Overseeing the completion of an Agency’s IT Strategic Investment Plan and ongoing updates.
4. Providing appropriate Agency personnel with access to automated tools.

F. Procedures
Agencies must designate an IT strategic investment planning coordinator and provide their contact information to the SIA team (refer to Section VII Inquiries).

IV. Definitions
All defined terms are capitalized within the policy and the online glossary of definitions can be found at IT Policy Glossary - Department of Administrative Services.
