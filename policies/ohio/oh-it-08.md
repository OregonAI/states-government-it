## Full text

IT-08 Website Standardization

State of Ohio Administrative Policy
No: IT-08 - Information Technology
Effective: April 29, 2025
Issued By: Kathleen C. Madden, Director

I. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish website standardization, content management, and security requirements for Agency websites. The intent of these requirements is to create a degree of consistency and predictability among Public-facing Web Pages, while still providing the flexibility to design secure web pages that fit the unique needs of Agencies. All defined terms are capitalized and a link to the glossary of definitions is found in section IV.

II. Scope
This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies). The majority of the requirements outlined within this policy apply only to Public-facing Web Pages. However, the following sections apply to Public-facing Web Pages as well as Agency web-facing applications: Section III.K. Browser Compatibility, Section III.L. Transport Layer Security (TLS) Certificates, Section III.P. Identification and Authentication, and Section III.Q. Website and Web-Facing Application Security.
In accordance with Ohio Revised Code (ORC) Section 125.183, the requirements related to Covered Applications outlined in Section III.C. of this policy apply to state agencies, which are defined as every organized body, office, or agency established by the laws of this state for the exercise of any function of state government, other than any state-supported institution of higher education, the courts, or any judicial agency. State agency also includes the general assembly, any legislative agency, and the capitol square review and advisory board (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies in section III.C.).

III. Policy
Agencies shall align with the website design and content management standards defined by the InnovateOhio Platform team when developing and maintaining Public-facing Web Pages on State-controlled Websites.

A. User Experience Digital Toolkit
Agencies shall utilize the InnovateOhio Platform Homepage - Ohio Design system to design their public facing web pages. The toolkit includes the following:
1. Style Guide (e.g., accessibility, color palette, typography, dependencies, etc.)
2. User Interface Controls (e.g., ads grid, accordions, arrows and popovers, badges, breadcrumbs, buttons, card carousel, checkboxes, labels, left and main navigation, maps, etc.)
3. Content Components (e.g., charts, icons, lists, tables, etc.)
4. Page Examples (e.g., form, landing, login, password reset, etc.)
5. Layout (e.g., cards, dividers, form, grid system, etc.)
6. Page Errors (e.g., 404-page, 500 page, etc.)

B. Branding
Each Agency shall have both Agency and Ohio.gov branding. This branding shall be consistent on all Agency pages.
1. Branding shall comply with the style in effect on the Ohio.gov homepage.
2. Agencies are permitted to use their own logos and branding. Agencies with subordinate Agencies shall use the subordinate Agency brand in addition to a link to the Agency home page.
3. Agency branding shall be linked to the home page of the Agency’s website. Subordinate Agency branding shall link to the subordinate Agency home page.

C. Social Media
The use of Social Media shall align with the following requirements:
1. Agencies shall only use State-approved Social Media, which includes, Facebook, Flickr, GovDelivery, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WordPress, YouTube, and X.
2. The download and/or use of any unauthorized social media, including Covered Applications as defined in ORC Section 125.183, is prohibited.
a. All Covered Applications must be immediately removed from all State-Owned or leased devices.
b. Exceptions to the Covered Applications prohibition shall only be considered for Law Enforcement Purposes or Security Purposes and must be authorized by the state agency director and the state chief information security officer or their designees. For any proposed uses, the agency must document the measures that will be taken to mitigate against the security and privacy risks associated with the use of a Covered Application. Examples of possible mitigation strategies include:
i. Accessing the Covered Application from a secure device that does not connect to the state’s network or wireless services.
ii. Ensuring that the device that is used to access the Covered Application does not store or transmit Confidential Data.
c. Any requests for exception shall be submitted to the Ohio Department of Administrative Services (DAS) Office of Information Security and Privacy (OISP) via the Security Exception Request form. The form is located within the IT Enterprise Services Portal under the “Services & Products/
Security Services” category.
3. Agencies shall take measures to prevent the downloading, installation, or use of a Covered Application.
4. Agencies shall monitor their use of Social Media and State-Owned or leased devices to ensure compliance with this policy.
5. Agencies’ Social Media content coordinators shall be reminded, at least annually, of the requirements regarding prohibited and appropriate use as outlined within Ohio Administrative Policy IT-04, “Use of Internet, E-mail and Other IT Resources.”
6. Third party tracking from Social Media links, for example, social network sharing buttons, widgets or plug-ins, shall not be permitted unless explicitly approved by the Agency. If Social Media links are authorized by the Agency to track user activities, then this shall be disclosed in the website privacy statement.
7. Links to Social Media shall appear in the Social Media column of the State Directory, State Directory - Ohio.gov - Official Website of the State of Ohio.

D. Search Feature
Each Agency shall have a search feature available and when technically feasible, utilize the InnovateOhio Platform search engine.
1. The search feature shall have the capability to perform searches within the Agency website.
2. The search bar appearance and functionality shall be consistent across all Agency web pages.

E. Navigation
Each Agency shall provide consistent navigation on each web page.
1. The primary navigation bar shall be implemented on all Agency web pages.
2. The primary navigation bar shall be populated with Agency-specific links and shall remain consistent across all web pages of a division or department.
3. Primary and secondary navigation shall be text-based; images or other formats shall not be used.
4. All images with hyperlink capability shall include an alternative text (alt text) description that conveys the action that will be initiated when the user clicks on the link (e.g., destination).
5. Navigation within self-contained or embedded content, such as interactive animations, video, or data visualizations, is permitted but should only be used within the context of the component.

F. Content Area Design
The layout and organization of each web page content area shall remain primarily at the discretion of the Agency, department, or division; Agencies shall refer to the InnovateOhio Platform Homepage - Ohio Design system for content and user experience best practices.

G. Website Content
Agencies shall comply with the following website content requirements. Each Agency shall maintain an accurate contact page containing the following information:
1. A general phone number that is answered by a person during business hours.
2. A media relations phone number (if available).
3. A general phone number that is answered by a person during business hours.
4. An e-mail address or submission form for general concerns.
5. A physical mailing address.
6. Contact pages shall be labeled "Contact."

H. Privacy Notice
Agency websites shall provide a hyperlink to a privacy notice on every web page. At a minimum, the privacy notice shall include the following elements:
1. The privacy notice shall describe the types of personal information the Agency collects through the website and why it is necessary.
2. The privacy notice shall provide an explanation of how the user's personal information is collected, including directly, such as through web applications and online forms, and indirectly, such as through website usage analytical tools.
3. The privacy notice shall disclose how the Agency uses personal information collected through the website, including secondary uses and public record requirements.
4. Agencies shall inform website users that they may have rights under ORC sections 149.43, 1347.08, 1347.09, and 1347.15 with respect to inspecting, obtaining, correcting, and disputing personal information the Agency collects or maintains through the website.
5. ORC Section 1347.15 requires each Agency to post on its’ website the policies adopted in accordance with that law. The privacy notice shall have a section labeled “Confidential Personal Information.” The section will include links to the Agency’s administrative rules, policies and procedures issued in accordance with ORC 1347.15. If an Agency does not maintain any confidential personal information under ORC 1347.15, it shall include a statement to that fact instead.
6. Agencies shall provide website users with an Agency point of contact who addresses privacy questions. The contact information shall include the mailing address and at least one of the following: a telephone number, e-mail address or contact form.
7. The privacy notice shall be kept up to date to accurately reflect Agency information practices. The privacy notice shall display the latest revision date.
8. Privacy notices shall be consistent with all applicable laws that regulate the collection, use and maintenance of personal information.
9. Each Agency's privacy notice link shall be labeled as "privacy" or "privacy notice." (Refer to the footer on Ohio.gov homepage for an example of labeling.)

I. Content Management
Agencies shall keep content up to date. Agencies shall develop a process for selecting and training content coordinators who are responsible for maintaining Agency website content.

J. Mobile Ready
Agencies shall utilize InnovateOhio Platform templates and components, available at Homepage - Ohio Design system, to ensure a responsive user experience on any device (e.g., mobile phones, tablets).

K. Browser Compatibility
Agency websites and web-facing applications shall render properly in the current, supported version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.

L. Transport Layer Security (TLS) Certificates
All publicly accessible, State-controlled Websites and web-facing applications shall only provide information and services through a hypertext transfer protocol secure (https) connection, encrypted using TLS certificates.
1. TLS certificates may be obtained, renewed, and revoked using the DAS Office of Information Technology (OIT) Digital Certificate Provisioning service.
2. TLS certificates may also be obtained from an approved vendor.
3. Website sessions shall be encrypted using current, supported TLS protocols.

M. Website Accessibility
Agency websites shall comply with the accessibility requirements outlined in Ohio Administrative Policy IT-09, “Website Accessibility.”

N. Domain Name Standardization
Each Agency is required to have an Ohio.gov domain in accordance with the requirements outlined in Ohio Administrative Policy IT-06, "Ohio.gov Standardization & Registration of Domain Names."

O. Website Standardization Requirement Guidance
For additional website standardization requirement guidance, Agencies shall refer to the InnovateOhio Platform User Experience Digital Toolkit web page, Homepage- Ohio Design system.

P. Identification and Authentication
Where Identification and Authentication are required for website functionality, Agencies shall leverage InnovateOhio Platform solutions.

Q. Website and Web-Facing Application Security
Agencies shall ensure that the following security requirements are satisfied when maintaining a public-facing website or web-facing application:
1. Information collected from users shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.
2. Vulnerability scans shall be routinely conducted by the DAS Office of Information Security and Privacy (OISP) and the results communicated to system and business owners. Critical or high severity vulnerabilities shall be promptly remediated or mitigated.
3. All public-facing websites or web-facing applications shall generate and store audit records. All audit records shall be stored for at least 30 days, longer if mandated by state or federal regulation, and on a system separate from the web host system.
4. Where applicable, Agencies shall ensure that public-facing websites and web-facing applications comply with the security and privacy requirements provided by regulating entities (e.g., Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS), IRS Publication 1075, Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), etc.).
5. Where collecting payment information, ensure compliance with regulatory requirements (e.g., Payment Card Industry (PCI), National Automated Clearing House Association (NACHA), etc.).

R. InnovateOhio Platform Responsibilities
The InnovateOhio Platform team shall:
1. Oversee the State of Ohio’s digital presence and partner with Agencies to ensure their websites align with statewide requirements;
2. Maintain digital toolkit standards and resources;
3. Support and provide Agencies with access to a website content management tool;
4. Communicate any substantive changes in website standards or content management to Agencies and content coordinators in a timely manner; and
5. Work with the DAS OISP to institute denial of service protections.

IV. Definitions
All defined terms are capitalized within the policy and the online glossary of definitions can be found at IT Policy Glossary - Department of Administrative Services.
