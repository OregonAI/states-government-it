IT-18 Information Security & Privacy Incident Response

State of Ohio Administrative Policy
No: IT-18 - Information Technology
Effective: November 5, 2025
Issued By: Kathleen C. Madden, Director

I. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to require Organizational Users and Agencies to report and respond to suspected Information Security and Privacy Incidents (Security Incident). The State of Ohio investigates and responds to Security Incidents to protect State Data, the public, and Agencies and to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
All defined terms are capitalized and a link to the glossary of definitions is found in section IV. In addition, references to National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Special Publication 800-53, “Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations,” Incident Response (IR) family identifiers and control numbers are provided in parentheticals next to requirements, where applicable.

II. Scope
This policy applies to all state agencies, boards, and commissions under the authority of the Governor (collectively referred to as Agency or Agencies).

III. Policy
The Ohio Department of Administrative Services (DAS) Office of Information Security and Privacy (OISP) and Agencies must implement and manage a Security Incident response capability that aligns with the requirements outlined in this policy as well as the associated State of Ohio IT Standard ITS-SEC-06. The statewide and Agency capabilities must be designed to ensure that all suspected Security Incidents are addressed with due diligence.

A. Security Incident Reporting
As detailed in State of Ohio IT Standard ITS-SEC-06, Security Incident reporting requirements include:
1. All Organizational Users must immediately report suspected Security Incidents to their Agency’s security team in accordance with agency procedures.
If the Agency security team is unavailable, the Organizational User must report the incident to the DAS Customer Service Center (CSC) by phone: 877-644-6860 or email: csc@ohio.gov. The reporting Organizational User must clearly state, “I am reporting a Security Incident.” [IR-6 and IR-6(1)]
Organizational Users must follow Agency Security Incident response plans and consult with their managers, deputy director, or Agency incident response team before taking any action to respond to a Security Incident.
2. Agency security teams must immediately report any Security Incidents to DAS OISP by contacting the DAS CSC by phone: 877-644-6860 or email: csc@ohio.gov. In addition, the Agency representative must:
a. Clearly state, “I am reporting a Security Incident and need to start an initial report.”
b. Provide an initial report, with the best available information, to the CSC.
c. Submit ongoing updates to DAS OISP. [IR-6 and IR-6(1)]

B. Security Incident Response Efforts
DAS and Agencies must share responsibility for Security Incident response efforts as determined by the distribution of Data, systems, and service management responsibilities (refer to State of Ohio IT Standard ITS-SEC-06 for additional details).

C. DAS Security Incident Response Roles and Responsibilities
DAS OISP oversees and monitors Security Incident response efforts for State Agencies. DAS OISP must also coordinate incident response activities for Significant Incidents and provide statewide incident response support, including tools, processes, and training.

D. Agency Security Incident Response Roles and Responsibilities
Agencies must report Security Incidents to DAS OISP and maintain a Security Incident response capability that partners with DAS OISP throughout the Security Incident lifecycle, including providing any requested information related to Security Incidents to DAS OISP.

E. Ransomware
An Agency experiencing a Ransomware incident may not pay or otherwise comply with a ransom demand.

F. Legal Compliance
DAS OISP works with Agency Security Incident response and legal teams, and, when appropriate, consults with external legal breach counsel to:
1. Determine whether a security incident triggers a cybersecurity or privacy legal requirement, such as security breach notification under Ohio Revised Code Section 1347.12 or other similar state or federal laws.
2. Take appropriate actions to comply with applicable cybersecurity and privacy regulations relating to the Security Incident.

IV. Definitions
All defined terms are capitalized within the policy and the online glossary of definitions can be found at IT Policy Glossary - Department of Administrative Services.
