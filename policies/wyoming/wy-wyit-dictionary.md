## At a glance

The IT Policy Dictionary policy: the statewide document that every WyIT policy points to for its definitions (each says 'All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary'). This file is the dictionary's own cover document; the definitions are in the lettered files it links, held as the wy-wyit-dictionary-* documents.
