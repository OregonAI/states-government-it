# Data Sharing Agreement Documentation Governance (WyIT 2014)

## At a glance

Data Sharing Agreement
Documentation Governance
WyIT.2014

 Policy Owner           Department of Enterprise Services, Data Privacy
 Policy Contact         Pat Potter, Deputy Chief Data Officer
 Policy Approver        Wyoming Data Governance Committee (WDGC)
 Effective Date         September 12, 2025
 Next Review Date       April 1, 2027
 Policy Version         2026-09.1

Scope:
​
This policy establishes comprehensive governance requirements for all data sharing arrangements
between Wyoming state agencies and external entities, and defines standard requirements for Data
Sharing Agreements (DSAs) and similar documents (e.g., Information Exchange Agreements, Non-
Disclosure Agreements, Memoranda of Understanding) to ensure consistency, comprehensiveness,
and compliance across all state agencies.

This policy addresses the critical need for systematic governance of data sharing activities and
standardized data sharing frameworks that protect state information assets while enabling appropriate
inter-agency collaboration, third-party partnerships, and federal data exchanges. It provides
comprehensive legal, technical, and procedural safeguards that ensure compliance with Wyoming
State law, federal privacy regulations, and industry security standards

Authority
This policy is promulgated under the authority of Wyoming State Statute 9-2-2906 and its subsections,
the Department of Enterprise Technology Services is required to adopt and enforce information
technology standards, and promote efficient use of information technology throughout state
government.

Specifically, this policy is further promulgated under the authority of the below listed Wyoming State
Statute subsections.

   ●​ Implements and maintains an information technology governance program for state agencies
      (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (ii));
   ●​ Establishes and enforces data security and privacy policies and standards for the state data
      infrastructure Establishes review criteria for the agencies information technology projects,
      procurements and purchases. (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (iii)); and

                                                     ​
Adopts information technology policies and standards and ensures agency compliance with those
policies and standards (W.S. 9-2-2906,(b), (vi)).

Audience
This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff (collectively
referred to as "agencies"). This policy is also applicable to consultants, affiliates, third party vendors
and temporary employees that use or store state data.

Policy Statements:
 1.​   GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK
       1.1.​ All data sharing arrangements between agencies and external entities, or between
             agencies involving sensitive information must be governed by formal written agreements
             meeting the requirements of this policy. ​
               [1], [2], [3], [4]

       1.2.​   Agencies may implement supplemental policies to augment this governing policy as
               needed. Agencies may create agency-specific policies that meet the outlined scope,
               purpose, and requirements, as stated in this policy. ​
               [5], [2], [6]

       1.3.​   Data sharing arrangements must comply with information classification requirements.​
               [4]

       1.4.​   Prior to establishing any data sharing arrangement, agencies must conduct risk
               assessments that consider data classification levels, applicable privacy requirements,
               security implications, and compliance obligations.
               [1], [5], [4], [6]

       1.5.​   All data sharing arrangements involving personally identifiable information (PII) or other
               sensitive data must include mandatory consultation with the Agency Official for Privacy
               (AOP), ensuring privacy impact assessments are completed and privacy safeguards are
               implemented
               [5], [2], [7]

       1.6.​   Data sharing agreements require approval from agency heads or their designated
               representatives, the Office of the Attorney General, and the OCIO.
               [1], [2]​

                                                      2

                                                      ​
      1.7.​   Both the ETS Security Office and Data Office may suspend agreements that pose
              compliance risks.
              [1], [5], [2] ​

2.​   REQUIRED AGREEMENT ELEMENTS
      2.1.​ Parties and Roles

               2.1.1.​      Identify Data Owner(s) and Data Controller(s) as applicable;​

               2.1.2.​      Identify Data Processor(s) and Data Recipient(s); and​

               2.1.3.​      Each party shall designate a Data Steward responsible for agreement
                            oversight.
              [1], [8], [9], [10], [6]

      2.2.​   Identify all specific data sets or types of information that are included in the sharing
              agreement.

              This could be broad (e.g., "all public safety incident reports") or narrow (e.g., "traffic
              citation data for Highway 25 corridor analysis").
              [11], [1], [12], [10], [6]

      2.3.​   Clearly state the period over which the data sharing will occur. This could be a one-time
              transfer, a specific date range, or an ongoing arrangement with regular updates.
              [11], [1], [12], [6], [10]

      2.4.​   Specify authorized locations for data storage, processing, and transmission, i.e., only
              within the State of Wyoming as opposed to the United States.
              [1, [13], [12], [6], [9]

      2.5.​   Clearly state the specific purposes for which the shared data can be used (e.g., "traffic
              safety analysis for Highway 25 improvement project" rather than "transportation
              planning").

              This helps prevent data from being used for purposes not originally intended or agreed
              upon.
              [1], [12], [9], [6]

      2.6.​   Clearly state which group/division/section within the receiving organization(s) will have
              access to the shared data.
              [1], [6], [10]

                                                      3

                                                  ​
 2.7.​   Clearly state what data processing or analysis types are permitted under the agreement
         (e.g., statistical analysis, trend identification, reporting) and explicitly prohibit
         unauthorized uses such as re-identification or profiling.
         [1], [12], [9], [6]

 2.8.​   Specify whether the receiving party is prohibited from sharing the data further or using it
         beyond the stated purpose.

         If onward sharing or expanded use is permitted, define the specific conditions, approval
         requirements, and compliance obligations.
         [1], [8], [9], [12], [10]

 2.9.​   All data sharing agreements must specify:​

          2.9.1.​      Data classification levels;​

          2.9.2.​      Specific data types requiring special handling under federal law (PII, HIPAA,
                       FERPA, etc.);​

          2.9.3.​      Data subject to exemptions under W.S. §16-4-203 of the Wyoming Public
                       Records Act; and​

          2.9.4.​      Any data requiring enhanced protections, for example honoring donor
                       agreements, or other legal restrictions.
         [1], [8], [4], [6], [12]

2.10.​   All data sharing agreements must specify secure transfer methods and approved
         protocols that meet or exceed the standards established in WyIT 2005, Information
         Residency and Transportation, including:​

         2.10.1.​      Encryption requirements for data in transit;​

         2.10.2.​      Prohibited transfer methods (unencrypted email, FTP, etc.); and​

         2.10.3.​      Alternative transfer procedures for system failures.
         [1], [8], [13], [15], [10]

2.11.​   All data sharing agreements must specify security safeguards appropriate to the data
         classification level, including storage security, access controls, physical security, network
         security, key management, access monitoring, and access reviews.
         [1], [8], [15], [16], [8], [12], [10]​

                                                      4

                                                             ​
      2.12.​   All data sharing agreements must specify data handling requirements, including
               retention obligations, disposal requirements, return procedures, and verification
               requirements for disposal or return completion.
               [1], [8], [17], [12], [6], [10]

      2.13.​   All data sharing agreements must include incident response procedures, including
               notification timelines, coordination procedures, and recovery responsibilities.
               [1], [8], [18], [19], [20], [12], [6], [10]

      2.14.​   If either party requires auditing and monitoring provisions, agreements must specify
               audit scope, frequency, responsible parties, and compliance verification procedures.
               [1], [8], [12], [6], [10]

      2.15.​   Termination conditions and data handling post-termination. Specify termination triggers,
               notification requirements, data return or destruction procedures, verification of
               completion, and timeline for post-termination data handling.
               [1], [8], [17], [6], [10]

3.​    LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT​

       3.1.​   Agencies must maintain a comprehensive inventory of all data sharing agreements.
               This inventory shall, at at minimum, include the:​

                3.1.1.​        Name of each agreement;​

                3.1.2.​        Inclusive dates of each agreement;

                3.1.3.​        Program(s) and / or system(s) that the agreement relates to; ​

                3.1.4.​        Date of the last review of each agreement;​

                3.1.5.​        Destruction requirements of each agreement; and

                3.1.6.​        Any relevant notes about each agreement.
               [1], [6]

       3.2.​   Agreements shall be reviewed at least once every 2 years, or at a frequency stated in
               the agreement, not to exceed 2 years. Agreement modifications must follow the same
               approval processes as initial agreements.
               [1], [5], [6]

       3.3.​   The agency shall review agreements promptly in the event of any significant changes,
               such as:

                3.3.1.​        Changes in agency leadership (e.g., appointment of a new director);

                3.3.2.​        Changes in data being shared;

                                                             5

                                                         ​

               3.3.3.​      When the date ranges are no longer valid;

               3.3.4.​      Statutory or regulatory updates that may impact the agreement; and/or

               3.3.5.​      Changes in operational circumstances, such as:

                  3.3.5.1.​          New, rewritten, or merged system​

                  3.3.5.2.​          Change in hosting environment, etc
                         [1], [5]​

      3.4.​   Agencies shall terminate agreements in accordance with the termination provisions
              contained within each agreement.

              Legacy agreements established before this policy's effective date require supplemental
              termination procedures including:

               3.4.1.​      Secure data destruction or return;

               3.4.2.​         Documentation of compliance with destruction requirements; and

               3.4.3.​      Notification to relevant stakeholders.​

              Agencies must ensure proper closure documentation for audit purposes.
              [1], [5], [17]

4.​   SECTION FOUR - IMPLEMENTATION AND COMPLIANCE
      4.1.​ Data Sharing Agreement Verification Process - Agency Responsibilities​

               4.1.1.​      Submit documentation to the ETS Data Office, unless the CDO or designee is
                            part of the governance;

               4.1.2.​      Demonstrate compliance through structure; and

               4.1.3.​      Maintain verification records.
              [1], [6], [10]

      4.2.​   Data Sharing Agreement Verification Process - ETS Data Office Responsibilities​

               4.2.1.​      Review submissions for completeness and compliance with all requirements
                            specified in Sections 4-7 of this policy;

               4.2.2.​      Provide written acknowledgment specifying approval status, any required
                            corrections, and guidance for addressing identified deficiencies; and

                                                         6

                                                    ​
               4.2.3.​     Monitor ongoing compliance through coordination with agencies and
                           established oversight procedures.
              [1], [6], [10]

      4.3.​   Data Sharing Agreement Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement​

               4.3.1.​     Agencies shall implement monitoring mechanisms to ensure ongoing
                           compliance with data sharing agreement terms, including: periodic
                           assessments of data use, security controls, and privacy protections.

                           Non-compliance issues must be addressed through corrective action plans
                           developed in coordination with the ETS Data Office.
                           [1], [5], [6]​

               4.3.2.​     Suspected violations of data sharing agreements or associated data breaches
                           must be reported upon discovery through established incident response
                           procedures, with coordination between agencies, the Agency Official for
                           Privacy (AOP), and the ETS Data Office as appropriate.
                           [1], [2], [18], [7], [20]​

               4.3.3.​     The Chief Data Officer reserves the authority to request and review agency
                           data sharing agreement inventories through the ETS Data Office to ensure
                           compliance with this policy and statewide data governance standards.
                           [1], [5]

Compliance & Exceptions:
Compliance
Employees who violate this policy may be subject to appropriate disciplinary action up to and including
discharge as well as both civil and criminal penalties. Non-employees, including, without limitation,
contractors, may be subject to termination of contractual agreements, denial of access to IT resources,
and other actions as well as both civil and criminal penalties.

Exceptions
Requests for exceptions to this policy shall be reviewed by the Chief Information Security Officer
(CISO) and the Chief Information Officer (CIO). Departments requesting exceptions shall provide such
requests to the CIO in accordance with WyIT.3002 Policy Exception Request.

The request should specifically state the scope of the exception along with justification for granting the
exception, the potential impact or risk attendant upon granting the exception, risk mitigation measures
to be undertaken by the IT Department, initiatives, actions and a time-frame for achieving the minimum
compliance level with the policies set forth herein. The CIO shall review such requests; confer with the
requesting department.

Any exception to established policy shall be defined and tracked as a risk to the state and established
mitigating controls monitored by the agency.
