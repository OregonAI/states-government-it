Scope:
This policy establishes a comprehensive framework for protecting individual privacy rights and ensuring
transparent and ethical data practices across all state agencies in Wyoming. This policy does not
supersede the requirements of Wyoming Statute 16-4-201 through 205 Wyoming Public Records Act
(WPRA).

This policy addresses the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of personal information by Wyoming
state agencies, ensuring compliance with privacy principles and legal obligations while maintaining
transparency in government data practices.

This policy serves as the state baseline. Agencies may implement more restrictive policies to meet
applicable state and federal regulations or agency-specific requirements, provided they do not deviate
substantially from this policy. In lieu of agency-specific policies, this policy shall serve as the governing
standard.

Authority
This policy is promulgated under the authority of Wyoming State Statute 9-2-2906 and its subsections,
the Department of Enterprise Technology Services is required to adopt and enforce information
technology standards, and promote efficient use of information technology throughout state
government.

Specifically, this policy is further promulgated under the authority of the below listed Wyoming State
Statute subsections.

   ●​ Implements and maintains an information technology governance program for state agencies
      (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (ii));
   ●​ Establishes and enforces data security and privacy policies and standards for the state data
      infrastructure Establishes review criteria for the agencies information technology projects,

                                                               ​
       procurements and purchases. (W.S 9-2-2906,(b), (iii)); and

Adopts information technology policies and standards and ensures agency compliance with those
policies and standards (W.S. 9-2-2906,(b), (vi)).

Audience
This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff (collectively
referred to as "agencies"). This policy is also applicable to consultants, affiliates, third party vendors
and temporary employees that use or store state data.

Policy Statements:
 1.​   POLICY PRECEDENCE AND AGENCY IMPLEMENTATION
       1.1.​ The agency may create agency-specific policies that meet the outlined scope, purpose,
             and requirements as stated in this policy. In lieu of agency-specific policies that meet
             the outlined scope, purpose, and requirements as stated in this policy, this policy shall
             serve as the governing policy.
             [1], [2], [3]​

       1.2.​   Agencies may implement supplemental policies to augment this governing policy as
               needed. This policy does not supersede any State or Federal regulation and does not
               override, waive, or provide exemption from the requirements of the Wyoming Public
               Records Act (Wyoming Statute 16-4-201 through 205). Agencies remain fully obligated
               to comply with all legal requirements while implementing appropriate privacy safeguards
               for exempt information.
               [1], [2], [3]

 2.​   LEGAL COMPLIANCE AND CONSENT
       2.1.​ Agencies shall adhere to all applicable federal and state laws and regulations governing
             data collection, storage, usage, protection, and destruction.
               [2], [4], [5], [6], [8], [9], [10], [11], [3]

       2.2.​   Agencies shall collect personal information or data only when:
                2.2.1.​ Authorized or required by Wyoming Statute, or
                2.2.2.​ Voluntary consent is obtained for programs not otherwise authorized.
               [2], [4], [14], [12] ​

       2.3.​   For statutory collections, agencies must provide clear notice of legal authority.
               [2], [5], [13], [8], [12]​

                                                      ​
3.​   TRANSPARENCY
      3.1.​ Agencies shall maintain an inventory of the following for each published report, data
            visualization, analytical product, or public-facing data release that contains or is derived
            from personal information:​

               3.1.1.​      Sources of collected data;​

               3.1.2.​      Purposes for which data are being collected;​

               3.1.3.​      Date of publication or release; and​

               3.1.4.​      Intended audience and distribution method. ​

              Note: When multiple publications derive from identical source material, agencies may
              create a single inventory entry referencing all related publications.​
              [2], [4], [14], [5], [3]​

      3.2.​   Agency websites publishing analytics or data reports shall include or link to the above
              data.
              [15], [16]​

4.​   PURPOSE LIMITATION AND DATA MINIMIZATION
      4.1.​ Data collection shall be limited to specific, legitimate purposes of fulfilling legal
            obligations or serving the public interest.​
              [14]   ​

      4.2.​   Only the minimum necessary data shall be collected to achieve the stated purposes.​
              [9]​

      4.3.​   Data shall not be used for incompatible purposes without authorization by law or
              individual consent.​
              [2], [4], [14], [9], [12]​

5.​   ACCOUNTABILITY AND OVERSIGHT
      5.1.​ Each agency's Agency Privacy Officer (AOP) shall oversee policy compliance and serve
            as a point of contact for privacy concerns. ​
              [17], [16]​

6.​   PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY AND ENGAGEMENT
      6.1.​ Agencies shall engage with the public to raise awareness about data collection practices
            and individual rights.​
            [13]​

       6.2.​   Accessible channels shall be provided for individuals to request information, file

Compliance & Exceptions:
Compliance
Employees who violate this policy may be subject to appropriate disciplinary action up to and including
discharge as well as both civil and criminal penalties. Non-employees, including, without limitation,
contractors, may be subject to termination of contractual agreements, denial of access to IT resources,
and other actions as well as both civil and criminal penalties.

                                                        ​
Exceptions
Requests for exceptions to this policy shall be reviewed by the Chief Information Security Officer
(CISO) and the Chief Information Officer (CIO). Departments requesting exceptions shall provide such
requests to the CIO in accordance with WyIT.3002 Policy Exception Request.

The request should specifically state the scope of the exception along with justification for granting the
exception, the potential impact or risk attendant upon granting the exception, risk mitigation measures
to be undertaken by the IT Department, initiatives, actions and a time-frame for achieving the minimum
compliance level with the policies set forth herein. The CIO shall review such requests; confer with the
requesting department.

Any exception to established policy shall be defined and tracked as a risk to the state and established
mitigating controls monitored by the agency.
