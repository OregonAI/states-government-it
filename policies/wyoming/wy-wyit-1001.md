## Full text

 AGENCY CONTROLLED INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
  (IT) CHANGE MANAGEMENT AND MAINTENANCE
                                           WyIT - 1001

 PURPOSE: To ensure agencies managing internal IT infrastructure maintain an IT
 change management practice; minimizing risk to agency business and maintaining
 compliance with statewide security policies, standards, and procedures.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

IV.    CHANGE MANAGEMENT

       A. Requirements

          ● Must establish a process for change management for IT operations and
            services to ensure requested changes are thoroughly checked and assessed
            technically and from a business perspective. The process should include:
            ○ Change classification types with varying response levels.

                                                                               WyIT 1001          Page 1

           ○ Defined roles and responsibilities for participants.
           ○ Procedure process workflow.
           ○ Key performance indicators of success or failure.
         ● One-week notice to ETS is required for all changes. The notice must include:
           ○ Change description,
           ○ Point of contact, and
           ○ Time of execution.

         Email notice should be emailed to the distribution group ets-change@wyo.gov.

V.    MAINTENANCE REQUIREMENTS

      A. Agency Controlled Maintenance

         ● Schedule, perform, document, and review maintenance records of
           maintenance on technology components according to manufacturer or
           vendor specifications and requirements.
         ● System owners are required to explicitly approve the removal of the
           information system or system components from facilities for off-site
           maintenance or repairs. Equipment must be sanitized prior to removal.
         ● Following maintenance, check all potentially impacted security controls to
           verify that they are still functioning properly following maintenance or repair
           actions.

      B. Vendor Controlled Maintenance

         ● Monitor and retain all affiliate-provided maintenance and diagnostic activities.
         ● Allow the use of affiliate diagnostic tools as consistent with policy and
           documented in the security plan for the information system.
         ● Employ strong authenticators in the establishment of affiliate maintenance
           and diagnostic sessions.
         ● Terminate session and network connections when affiliate maintenance is
           completed.
         ● Document the security plan for the information system, the policies and
           procedures for the establishment and use of affiliate maintenance and
           diagnostic connections.

VI.   PERSONNEL REQUIREMENTS

                                                                       WyIT 1001              Page 2

    A. Establish a process for authorizing maintenance personnel authorization.

    B. Maintain a current list of authorized personnel available to ETS upon request.

    C. Ensure non-escorted personnel performing maintenance have required access
       authorizations.

    D. Designate personnel with required access authorizations and technical
       competence to supervise the maintenance activities of personnel who do not
       possess required access authorizations.
