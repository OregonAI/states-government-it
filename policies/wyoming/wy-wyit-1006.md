## Full text

        ACTIVE DIRECTORY (AD) PRIVILEGED ACCESS
                                            WyIT 1006

 PURPOSE: The purpose of this policy is to outline general provisions for privileged
 access controls on Information Technology Resources and enumerates general roles and
 responsibilities for the Enterprise, Schema and Domain Administrators roles within Active
 Directory (AD). The following applies to any active Directory within the State. (WyIT 3001,
 General Security / Acceptable Use).

 I.​ APPLICABILITY

        This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards and commissions staff
        (collectively referred to as ‘agencies). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
        affiliates and temporary employees.

II.​ DEFINITIONS​

         All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.​ EXCEPTIONS​
      ​
      Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by the WyIT 3002,
      Security Exception Request policy.​

 IV.​    GENERAL STANDARDS​

         A.​ Privileged access shall only be granted upon completion or verification of
             background checks that meet or exceed the investigative requirements for the
             highest level of data the user will have access to.​

                                                                                  WyIT 1006​      Page 1

      B.​ User IDs and passwords for privilege access shall be separate and distinct
          from user’s regular domain user accounts and shall not be used for
          non-privileged operations.​

      C.​ Contractors/vendors shall not be given privileged access to production
          systems unless negotiated as part of a Statement of Work (SOW) or
          maintenance agreement.​

      D.​ If privileged access is granted to complete a specific task, privileged access
          will be revoked once the task is complete.​

      E.​ Privileged users shall comply with all State of Wyoming IT security policies,
          standards and statutes.​

V.​   ACTIVE DIRECTORY (AD) ENTERPRISE ADMINISTRATOR
      ACCOUNT​

      A.​ At least one permanent primary and one permanent secondary account shall
          be created.​

      B.​ Other than permanent accounts, assignment of Enterprise Administrator
          privileges will be driven by a specific task or function, and shall fall under the
          oversight of the agency’s Change Advisory Board (CAB).​

      C.​ Roles and Responsibilities:​

         1.​ Maintain the AD at the forest level.​

         2.​ Maintain the AD forest trusts.​

         3.​ Maintain the integrity of the AD security boundary.​

         4.​ Monitor and audit the activities of Domain Administrators.​

         5.​ Perform only those tasks assigned and for which privileged access was
             granted.​

         6.​ Maintain documentation in accordance with the State or agency retention
             schedule.​

         7.​ Cooperate and coordinate with the CAB for the orderly and expedient

                                                                               WyIT 1006 ​     Page 2

               execution of change requests.

VI.​    ACTIVE DIRECTORY (AD) SCHEMA ADMINISTRATOR ACCOUNT

        A.​ Privileges will be assigned when a modification to the schema is needed.​

        B.​ Assignment of privileges will be performed by the Enterprise Administrator and
            shall fall under the oversight of the agency’s CAB.​

        C.​ Roles and Responsibilities:​

           1.​ Maintain the Active Directory schema.​

           2.​ Ensure the schema supports the integrity of the AD security boundary.​

           3.​ Perform only those tasks assigned and for which privileged access was
               granted.​

           4.​ Submit documentation of schema changes to the CAB.​

           5.​ Maintain documentation in accordance with the State or agency retention
               schedule.​

           6.​ Cooperate and coordinate with the CAB for the orderly and expedient
               execution of change requests.​

VII.​   ACTIVE DIRECTORY (AD) DOMAIN ADMINISTRATOR ACCOUNT

        A.​ At least one permanent primary and one permanent secondary account shall
            be created, and shall be monitored by the Enterprise Administrator.​

        B.​ Other than permanent accounts, assignment of Domain Administrator
            privileges will be driven by volume and urgency of daily operations or specific
            tasks and functions. Additional Domain Administrator accounts shall be
            monitored by the Enterprise Administrator.​

        C.​ Roles and Responsibilities:​

           1.​ Maintain the day-to-day operation of domain trees that populate the forest.​

           2.​ Perform only those tasks assigned and for which privileged access was
               granted.​

                                                                               WyIT 1006 ​    Page 3

         3.​ Submit completed non-routine and emergency change requests to the
             CAB.​

         4.​ Maintain documentation in accordance with the State or agency retention
             schedule.​

         5.​ Provide monitoring and reporting of anomalous activities.​

         6.​ Maintain audit trails and event logs of security related activities.​

         7.​ Provide on-site support during business hours and on call support after
             business hours.​

      8.​ Supervise delegated administrative tasks to non-privileged users.​
