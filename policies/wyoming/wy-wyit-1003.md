# Contingency Planning (WyIT 1003)

## Full text

                          CONTINGENCY PLANNING
                                           WyIT - 1003

 PURPOSE: To ensure agencies managing internal IT infrastructure can sustain
 available IT resources and information systems during a service disruption.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

IV.    PLAN DEVELOPMENT

       A. Requirements

       A contingency plan must be developed for all information system(s) containing the
       following:

       ● Identifies essential missions, business functions, and associated contingency
         requirements;
       ● Provides recovery objectives, restoration priorities, and metrics;

      ● Addresses contingency roles, responsibilities, and assigned individuals with
        contact information;
      ● Addresses maintaining essential missions and business functions despite an
        information system disruption, compromise, or failure;
      ● Addresses eventual, full information system restoration without deterioration of
        the security safeguards originally planned and implemented and
      ● Reviewed and approved by the CIO and information system’s owner
        management annually

      B. Agency Responsibilities

      ● Distribute copies of contingency plans to key contingency personnel, identified
        by name and/or by business role.
      ● Coordinate contingency planning activities with incident handling activities.
      ● Annually update the contingency plan to address changes to the business
        owner’s mission, information system, or environment of operation and problems
        encountered during contingency plan implementation, execution, or testing.
      ● Communicate contingency plan changes to key contingency personnel
        identified by name and/or by business role.
      ● Protect the contingency plan from unauthorized disclosure and modification.

V.    TRAINING

      A. Provide contingency training to information system users consistent with
         assigned roles and responsibilities.
      B. Ensure designated personnel receive biannual contingency training upon
         assuming a role or responsibility and when required by information system
         changes.

VI.   TESTING

      A. Testing Requirements

      ● Annually test the contingency plan for the information system, as determined by
        the mission-critical nature of the business system(s).
      ● Use strategic and tactical planning during testing to simulate a production
        information system to determine the effectiveness of the plan and the
        organizational readiness to execute the plan.

                                                                     WyIT 1003             Page 2

       ● Review the contingency plan test results and initiate corrective actions as
         needed.
       ● Coordinate contingency plan testing with organizational elements responsible
         for related plans; plans related to contingency plans for information systems
         include, for example, Business Continuity Plans, Disaster Recovery Plans,
         Continuity of Operations Plans, Crisis Communications Plans, Critical
         Infrastructure Plans, Cyber Incident Response Plans, and Occupant Emergency
         Plans.

VII.   ALTERNATE SITES

       A. Establish an alternative storage site with the following requirements:

       ● Necessary agreements to permit the storage and retrieval of information system
         backup information.
       ● Information security safeguards equivalent to the primary site.
       ● Separate from the primary storage site to reduce susceptibility to the same
         threats.
       ● Log of potential accessibility problems to the alternate storage site, in the event
         of an area-wide disruption or disaster, with an outline of explicit mitigation
         actions.

       B. Establish an alternate processing site with the following requirements:

       ● Necessary agreements which:
         ○ Permit the transfer and resumption of the information system operations for
             essential missions/business functions consistent with recovery time and
             recovery point objectives when the primary processing capabilities are
             unavailable.
         ○ Provide priority-of-service provisions according to business objectives and
             availability requirements.
       ● List of equipment and supplies required to transfer and resume operations at the
         alternate processing site.
       ● Ensure contracts are in place to support delivery to the site within the
         agreed-upon period for transfer/resumption.
       ● Information security safeguards equivalent to that of the primary site.
       ● Separated from the primary processing site to reduce susceptibility to the same
         threats.

        ● Log of potential accessibility problems to the alternate storage site, in the event
          of an area-wide disruption or disaster, with an outline of explicit mitigation
          actions.

VIII.   BACKUPS

        A. Conduct required backups of information system(s) defined by the frequency
           consistent with recovery time and recovery point objectives. These should
           include:

           ● User-level information, and
           ● System-level information,

        B. Information system documentation should:

           ● Include security-related documentation;
           ● Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of backup information at
             storage locations.

        C. Backup information should be tested to verify media reliability and information
           integrity.

 IX.    RECOVERY AND RECONSTITUTION

        A. Provide for the recovery and reconstitution of the information system to a known
           state after a disruption, compromise, or failure.

        B. Provide that the information system implements transaction recovery for systems
           that are transaction-based.
