## Full text

                          INCIDENT RESPONSE
                                       WyIT - 3010

 PURPOSE: This policy ensures that IT properly identifies, contains, investigates,
 remedies, reports, and responds to security incidents.

  I.   APPLICABILITY

       This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
       (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
       affiliates, and temporary employees.

 II.   DEFINITIONS

       All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.   EXCEPTIONS

       Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by the WyIT - 3002 -
       Security Exception Request policy.

 IV.    INCIDENT RESPONSE TRAINING

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Provide incident response training to information system users consistent with
          assigned roles and responsibilities:
          ○ Within 30 days of assuming an incident response role or responsibility.
          ○ When required by information system changes and annually thereafter.
        ● Incorporate simulated events into incident response training to facilitate
          effective response by personnel in crisis situations.

        ● Employ automated mechanisms to provide a more thorough and realistic
          incident response training environment.

  V.    INCIDENT RESPONSE TESTING

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Test the incident response capability for the information system annually or
          when a significant change has been made to the environment, using the CIO’s
          pre-approved process, to determine the incident response effectiveness and
          document the results.
        ● Coordinate incident response testing with affiliate contacts responsible for
          related plans such as Business Continuity Plans, Contingency Plans, Disaster
          Recovery Plans, Continuity of Operations Plans, Crisis Communications Plans,
          Critical Infrastructure Plans, and Occupant Emergency Plans.

 VI.    INCIDENT HANDLING

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Implement an incident handling capability for security incidents that includes
          preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication, and recovery.
        ● Coordinate incident handling activities with contingency planning activities.
        ● Incorporate lessons learned from ongoing incident handling activities into
          incident response procedures, training, and testing/exercises, and implement
          the resulting changes accordingly.

VII.    INCIDENT MONITORING

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

        ● Employ automated mechanisms to assist in the tracking of security incidents
          and in the collection and analysis of incident information.

VIII.   INCIDENT REPORTING

        All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

      ● Require personnel to report suspected security incidents to the CIO Service
        Desk immediately.

IX.   INCIDENT RESPONSE ASSISTANCE

      All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

      ● Provide an incident response support resource integral to the incident
        response capability that offers advice and assistance to users of the
        information system for the handling and reporting of security incidents.

X.    INCIDENT RESPONSE PLAN

      All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy shall:

      ● Develop an incident response plan that:
        ○ Provides the affiliate with a roadmap for implementing its incident response
          capability.
        ○ Describes the structure of the incident response capability.
        ○ Provides a high-level approach for how the incident response capability fits
          into the overall ecosystem.
        ○ Meets the unique requirements of the affiliate, which relate to mission, size,
          structure, and functions.
        ○ Defines reportable incidents.
        ○ Provides metrics for measuring the incident response capability within the
          affiliate.
        ○ Defines the resources and management support needed to effectively
          maintain and mature an incident response capability.
        ○ Is reviewed and approved by the CIO annually or when there has been a
          significant change to the environment.

      ● All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy
        shall distribute copies of the incident response plan based on role and need to
        know.

      ● All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy
        shall:

         ○ Review the incident response plan annually or when there has been a
           significant change to the environment.

      ● All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy
        shall:
        ○ Update the incident response plan to address system changes or problems
            encountered during plan implementation, execution, or testing.

      ● Communicate incident response plan changes to the CIO.

      ● All applicable agencies and/or affiliates identified in the scope of this policy
        shall:
        ○ Protect the incident response plan from unauthorized disclosure and
            modification.

REFERENCES: This policy has no supporting references to websites, other policies,
or federal or state statutes.
