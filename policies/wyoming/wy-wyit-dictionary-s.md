# IT Policy Dictionary: Definitions S (IT Policy Dictionary, Definitions - S)

## At a glance

The Definitions - S part of the IT Policy Dictionary: the definitions of IT terms used in the State of Wyoming policies and standards (WyIT), which every WyIT policy incorporates by reference. Per its Applicability section it applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards and commissions staff and to consultants, affiliates and temporary employees.
