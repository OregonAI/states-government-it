# Local Administrative Rights (WyIT 3019)

## At a glance

WyIT 3019, Local Administrative Rights: a statewide policy in the Enterprise Technology Services policy library. Per the Applicability section of the file it applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards and commissions staff and to consultants, affiliates and temporary employees.

## Full text

             LOCAL ADMINISTRATIVE RIGHTS
                                          WyIT 3019

​
PURPOSE: This policy describes conditions for granting administrative rights.​

 I.​ APPLICABILITY

     This policy applies to all Executive Branch agencies, boards, and commissions staff
     (collectively referred to as “agencies”). This policy is also applicable to consultants,
     affiliates, and temporary employees.

II.​ DEFINITIONS

     All definitions for this policy are located in the Policy Dictionary.

III.​ EXCEPTIONS

     Any exceptions provided to the following policy must abide by the WyIT - 3002 -
     Security Exception Request policy.

IV.​ REQUIREMENTS

     ●​ Administrative rights allow individuals to change configuration settings and
        install software on a given machine. Incorrect machine configuration can lead to
        performance problems, machine downtime, lost productivity, higher support
        costs, or exposure to malware and other security exploits.

     ●​ Given the serious consequences of mishandling or abuse of administrative
        rights, these rights will only be granted when deemed essential for job function
        or performance, such as:
        ○​ A CIO approved requirement to download and install specific types of

              software or make system configuration changes.
          ○​ An enterprise-approved solution or software title must have an administrative
              rights access level to run on a given machine.
       ●​ Local, non-LAPS administrator accounts should not be utilized unless approved
          by the ETS Security team.
       ●​ Approved, non-LAPS local accounts will only be used in the following situations:
          ○​ Inaccessibility to the system due to lost domain association.
          ○​ Initial configuration of the system.
          ○​ Approved, non-LAPS local accounts will adhere to the following restrictions:
              ■​ Must follow policy WyIT - 3006 - Identification and Authentication.
              ■​ Must be unique to each machine.

V.​    APPLYING FOR ADMINISTRATIVE RIGHTS ACCESS

       Agency designated security contacts, shall use the Security Exception Form located
       in the Customer Portal. The CIO reserves the right to deny the application if it does
       not represent a clear business need or if the applicant has a documented history of
       violating security policy. Local administrative access will be evaluated based on
       major changes to the environment or annually, whichever is soonest.

VI.​   COMPLIANCE

       Failure to comply with or knowingly circumvent this policy will result in
       disciplinary actions through the appropriate Department of Administration
       and Information, Human Resources process.

REFERENCES: This policy has no supporting references to websites, other policies, or
federal or state statutes.
