# Hosting-Customization Policy (MaineIT Hosting-Customization Policy)

## At a glance

Identifies OIT hosting and customization options and the policies relevant to each hosting and customization scenario.

## Full text

                                           Maine State Government
                                 Dept. of Administrative & Financial Services
                                   Office of Information Technology (OIT)

                                 Hosting-Customization Policy
1.0 Purpose

    This policy identifies Office of Information Technology (OIT) hosting and customization options.
    The purpose of this policy is to notify OIT personnel involved with hosting and customization
    decisions about the policies which are relevant for their particular hosting and customization
    scenario.

2.0 Definitions

   2.1 COTS- Commercial Off-The-Shelf application.

3.0 Applicability

    This policy applies to the Executive Branch.

4.0 Responsibilities

   4.1 Associate CIO, Applications: Analyzes the application landscape and judges the suitability of
       hosting option.

   4.2 Associate CIO, Infrastructure: Presents OIT hosting capability and fit for any particular
       application.

                                            Hosting-Customization Policy

   5.1 Custom Applications (OIT-Hosting) – All State of Maine policies, toolset requirements, and
       standards apply.

   5.2 Custom Applications (Remote-Hosting) – All Application policies apply. Infrastructure
       provisioning (how the infrastructure is equipped) policies do not apply.

   5.3 COTS Applications (OIT-Hosting) – The specifics of the Application provisioning (how the
       application is built and distributed) do not apply but the general Application Deployment
       Certification requirements must be met (for example: Accessibility and Security Testing is
       required but the language the application is written in is not specified as long as it runs on
       the infrastructure hosted by OIT).

   5.4 COTS Applications (Remote-Hosting) – Application Deployment Certification is required.
       Remote Hosting requirements must be met. The Accessibility4 and Security-related policies
       must be enforced regardless of the application provider or hosting provider. This allows a
       variety of languages and platforms to be used.

   5.5 OIT-Housing is a special instance of Remote Hosting, where equipment resides in an OIT
        data center; OIT provides the physical security, uninterrupted electricity, climate
        control, rack space, and Internet connectivity (including network segmentation). The
        OIT Housing Vendor provides everything else.

   5.6 Interfaces and integration are the responsibility of OIT in partnership with the vendors.

   5.7 OIT-insourcing of a Remote-Hosted application must be preceded by migration to OIT-
       compliant technology. This is intended solely for highly unusual events (such as hosting
       vendor bankruptcy, etc.) where Remote-Hosting is no longer feasible.
