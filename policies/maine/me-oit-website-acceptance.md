# Website Acceptance Policy (MaineIT Website Acceptance Policy)

## At a glance

How eGovernment Services ensures that new web content conforms to the State Web Standards and the Accessibility Policy.

## Full text

                                       Maine State Government
                             Dept. of Administrative & Financial Services
                                  Office of Information Technology

                               Website Acceptance Policy
1.0 Purpose

   The Office of eGovernment (eGov) Services will ensure that new web content will conform to
   State Web Standards and Accessibility Policy.

   The purpose of this policy is to document and clarify responsibilities and processes regarding
   the conformance of agency websites that are the target of significant content augmentation and
   modification.

2.0 Definitions

  2.1 Agency Website: A set of interconnected web pages that are generally co-located in a folder
      and are collectively administered in the interest of an agency.

  2.2 Semi-autonomous State Agency: An agency created by an act of the Legislative Branch
      that is not a part of the Executive Branch. This term does not include the Legislative Branch,
      Judicial Branch, Office of the Attorney General, Office of the Secretary of State, Office of the
      State Treasurer and Audit Department.

3.0 Applicability

   This policy applies to all websites, regardless of where the websites are hosted, of Executive
   Branch and semi-autonomous State agencies. This policy also applies to those websites owned
   by other governmental branches that are hosted on computer devices operated by the Office of
   Information Technology.

4.0 Responsibilities

  4.1 Chief Information Officer of the State of Maine - Title 5, Maine Revised Statutes, Chapter 163
      §19731, Section 1, Paragraph B authorized the Chief Information Officer to “set policies and
      standards for the implementation and use of information and telecommunications
      technologies, including privacy and security standards and standards of the Federal
      Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), for information technology.”

  4.2 Under the direction of the CIO, the eGov Services Office shall

     4.2.1    Develop procedures and processes including:

        4.2.1.1 Establishing the criteria to be used in validating web content
        4.2.1.2 Developing the processes by which those criteria will be applied in the review of
                web content in a manner that serves the dual purposes:
        (i) Improving policy conformance of State web content
        (ii) Educating agency staff engaged in web activities as the processes are undertaken.

     4.2.2   Create the Website Review Committee, composed of web coordinators, for validating
             web content conforms with State policy.

  4.3 InforME – (for content to be hosted in that environment)

     4.3.1   Develop a website test area, closely resembling the State’s web environment, within
             which new content can be validated before publication to the Internet.

     4.3.2   Assign temporary test locations for requestors charged with developing new websites
             or redesigning old ones. All requests must be authorized by the agency’s Web
             Coordinator.

     4.3.3   Work with the Website Review Committee, eGov Services and the agency Web
             Coordinator in the web site review and remediation process.

     4.3.4   Provide the Internet hosting location within the State’s website for validated
             websites.

     4.3.5   Delete the test environment once the validation process is complete.

  4.4 Agency Web Coordinator

     4.4.1   As the agency’s representative, the Web Coordinator has primary responsibility for
             achieving web and accessibility policy conformance.

     4.4.2   Initiating the validation process for new websites and for websites being redesigned.

     4.4.3   Migrating validated content to the production location.

     4.4.4   Obtaining an appropriate test location where new content can be validated.

  4.5 Agency Information Technology Directors will communicate the importance of web and
      accessibility policy conformance to non-technical agency staff.

5.0 Directives

   The Office of eGovernment Services with input from the Web Coordinators group, InforME, the
   Agency Information Technology Director and the Information Technology Accessibility
   Committee will adopt and implement standards, procedures, and best practices that will
   achieve the goals of this policy.
