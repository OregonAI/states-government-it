# Digital Media Protection Policy (WVOT-PO1011)

## At a glance

Standards, procedures and restrictions for executive branch employees who use devices to connect to a WVOT-supported network to store, back up, relocate or otherwise access enterprise data.

 ​1.​   ​PURPOSE​

        ​1.1.​     ​ his policy defines standards, procedures, and restrictions for Executive​
                   T
                   ​Branch employees who use devices to connect to a WVOT-supported​
                    ​network, in order to store, back-up, relocate, or otherwise access​
                     ​enterprise data in a safe, secure manner.​

 ​2.​   ​SCOPE​

        ​2.1.​     ​ his​ ​policy​ ​applies​ ​to​ ​all​ ​departments​ ​(including​ ​agencies,​ ​boards,​
                   T
                   ​authorities,​ ​and​ ​commissions)​ ​within​ ​the​ ​Executive​ ​Branch​ ​of​ ​WV​ ​State​
                    ​Government,​​excluding​​constitutional​​officers,​​the​​WV​​Board​​of​​Education,​
                     ​the​​WV​​Department​​of​​Education,​​and​​the​​county​​boards​​of​​education​​that​
                      ​have access to or use State-provided resources.​

        ​2.2.​     ​ here​​there​​are​​policies​​in​​place​​which​​provide​​less​​restrictions​​or​​security​
                   W
                   ​than​ ​this​ ​policy,​ ​those​ ​policies​ ​will​ ​be​ ​superseded​ ​by​ ​this​ ​policy.​ ​In​
                    ​instances​ ​where​ ​existing​ ​state​ ​and​ ​federal​ ​laws,​​regulations,​​and​​policies​
                     ​are more restrictive than this policy, more restrictive provisions will prevail.​

 ​3.​     ​POLICY​

         ​3.1.​   ​ he​ ​security​ ​safeguards​ ​may​ ​vary​ ​by​ ​device​ ​type,​ ​but​ ​in​ ​all​ ​cases​ ​must​
                  T
                  ​comply​ ​with​​the​​requirements​​set​​forth​​in​​this​​policy.​​This​​document​​is​​not​
                   ​all-inclusive​ ​and​ ​management​ ​has​ ​the​ ​authority​ ​and​ ​discretion​ ​to​
                    ​appropriately​ ​address​ ​any​ ​unacceptable​ ​behavior​ ​and/or​ ​practice​ ​not​
                     ​specifically mentioned herein.​

         ​3.2.​   ​ or​ ​the​​purposes​​of​​this​​policy,​​“Digital​​Media”​​includes​​any​​technology​​or​
                  F
                  ​service that contains state owned media (disk, cloud service, etc.)​

         ​3.3.​   ​ mployees​ ​are​ ​prohibited​ ​from​ ​using​ ​personally​ ​owned​ ​media​ ​on​
                  E
                  ​state-owned​ ​devices​ ​or​ ​to​ ​store​ ​State​ ​data.​ ​This​ ​includes​ ​both​ ​personal​
                   ​usb devices, sd cards, and personal cloud storage (Dropbox, etc.)​

         ​3.4.​   ​ he​​WVOT​​reserves​​the​​right​​to​​disable​​the​​ability​​to​​connect​​to​​removable​
                  T
                  ​media, USB devices, and personal cloud storage.​

         ​3.5.​   ​ gencies​​may​​prohibit​​flash​​drive​​use​​at​​any​​time​​in​​order​​to​​protect​​data.​
                  A
                  ​This​​prohibition​​should​​be​​implemented​​through​​policy,​​training,​​and/or​​use​
                   ​of technical controls (e.g. port blocking control).​

         ​3.6.​   ​All media must be encrypted using NIST FIPS 140 approved technology.​

         ​3.7.​   ​ mployees​​are​​prohibited​​from​​using​​removable​​media,​​USB​​devices,​​and​
                  E
                  ​personal​ ​cloud​ ​storage​ ​that​ ​does​ ​not​ ​have​ ​adequate​ ​protection​
                   ​mechanisms​ ​to​ ​store​ ​or​ ​transmit​ ​sensitive​ ​data​ ​(e.g.,​ ​Protected​ ​Health​
                    ​Information,Sensitive​ ​Personally​ ​Identifiable​ ​Information,​ ​Federal​ ​Tax​
                     ​Information (FTI)).​

         ​3.8.​   ​ mployees​ ​must​ ​not​ ​uninstall​ ​or​ ​deactivate​ ​any​ ​security​ ​controls​ ​loaded​
                  E
                  ​onto the media device by the WVOT, or its designee.​

         ​3.9.​   ​ mployees must immediately report all security incidents or suspected​
                  E
                  ​incidents of unauthorized data access, data loss, and/or disclosure to​​the​
                   ​Privacy Officer portal:​
                    ​https://wv.accessgov.com/technology/Forms/Page/incident-reporting/inci​
                     ​dent-reporting/1​

        ​3.10.​   ​ he​ ​WVOT​ ​will​ ​establish​ ​audit​ ​trails​ ​or​ ​logs​ ​in​ ​all​ ​situations​ ​it​ ​deems​ ​is​
                  T
                  ​warranted.​​The​​resulting​​records​​will​​allow​​tracking​​of​​any​​data​​movement,​
                   ​and​ ​the​ ​resulting​ ​reports​ ​may​ ​be​ ​used​ ​for​ ​investigation​ ​of​ ​possible​
                    ​breaches​​and/or​​misuse.​​The​​WVOT​​has​​the​​right​​to​​monitor​​all​​employee​
                     ​access​ ​and/or​ ​connection​ ​to​ ​the​ ​State​ ​network​ ​in​ ​order​ ​to​ ​identify​ ​and​
                      ​analyze unusual usage patterns or other activity.​

              ​3.11.1.​   ​ nder​ ​normal​ ​circumstances​ ​users​ ​must​ ​store​ ​all​ ​data​ ​on​ ​WVOT​

                          ​managed​ ​State​ ​servers​​or​​storage​​(e.g.​​state​​approved​​file​​storage​
                           ​(Ex.​ ​Google​ ​Drive).​ ​If​ ​an​ ​agency​ ​elects​ ​to​ ​use​ ​a​ ​non-WVOT​

                  ​media,​ ​that​ ​content​ ​and​ ​media​ ​must​ ​be​ ​handled​ ​and​ ​stored​ ​in​ ​a​​manner​

              ​3.12.1.​   ​ emovable​ ​media​ ​must​ ​be​ ​physically​ ​protected​ ​against​ ​loss,​
                          R
                          ​damage, abuse, or misuse when used, stored, and in transit.​

        ​3.13.​   ​ hen​ ​confidential​​information​​is​​physically​​transported​​it​​shall​​be​​done​​so​
                  W
                  ​in a secure manner and only by personnel specifically authorized to do so.​

        ​3.14.​   ​ here​ ​information​ ​is​ ​transferred​ ​to​ ​media,​ ​that​ ​media​ ​shall​ ​be​ ​stored​

                  ​securely​ ​within​ ​a​ ​controlled​ ​area,​ ​and​ ​access​ ​to​ ​that​ ​media​ ​shall​ ​be​

                    ​media​​is​​no​​longer​​needed​​to​​store​​or​​transport​​system​​information​​it​​must​

                   ​template is available on the WVOT Policies and Procedures webpage.​

        ​3.16.​   ​ mployees​ ​will​ ​contact​ ​an​ ​immediate​ ​supervisor​ ​and/or​ ​the​ ​Chief​

     ​4.3.​   ​ igital​ ​Media​ ​–​ ​Digital​ ​media​ ​are​ ​any​ ​media​ ​that​ ​are​ ​encoded​ ​in​ ​a​
              D
              ​machine-readable​ ​format.​ ​Digital​ ​media​ ​can​ ​be​ ​created,​ ​viewed,​
               ​distributed, modified and preserved on digital electronics devices.​

     ​4.4.​   ​ ederal​ ​Tax​ ​Information​ ​(FTI)​ ​--​ ​FTI​ ​may​ ​consist​ ​of​ ​returns​ ​or​ ​return​
              F
              ​information​​and​​may​​contain​​personally​​identifiable​​information​​(PII).​​FTI​​is​
               ​any​ ​return​ ​or​ ​return​ ​information​ ​received​ ​from​ ​the​ ​IRS​ ​or​ ​secondary​
                ​source,​ ​such​ ​as​ ​SSA,​ ​Federal​ ​Office​ ​of​ ​Child​ ​Support​ ​Enforcement​ ​or​
                 ​Bureau​ ​of​ ​Fiscal​ ​Service.​ ​FTI​ ​includes​ ​any​ ​information​ ​created​ ​by​ ​the​
                  ​recipient that is derived from return or return information.​

     ​4.5.​   ​ egally​ ​Protected​ ​Data​ ​/​ ​Information​ ​–​ ​Personally​ ​identifiable​ ​information​
              L
              ​(See​ ​definition​ ​below)​ ​of​ ​any​ ​kind,​​such​​as​​personal,​​financial,​​academic,​
               ​or​ ​health​ ​related​ ​data,​ ​which​​is​​protected​​by​​privacy​​and/or​​security​​laws.​
                ​Protected​ ​Health​ ​Information​​(See​​definition​​below​​-​​e.g.​​medical​​records,​
                 ​diagnosis​ ​information,​ ​etc.)​ ​Laws​ ​are​ ​divided​ ​into​ ​categories,​ ​federal​​and​
                  ​state,​ ​which​ ​govern​ ​the​ ​handling​ ​of​ ​certain​ ​types​ ​of​ ​sensitive​ ​information​
                   ​that​ ​must​ ​be​ ​protected​ ​by​ ​those​ ​authorized​ ​to​ ​use​ ​it.​ ​PHI,​ ​PII,​ ​FTI​ ​are​
                    ​examples of highly sensitive and regulated data.​

     ​4.6.​   ​ ffice​​of​​Primary​​Responsibility​​(OPR)​​-​​The​​primary​​section​​within​​WVOT​
              O
              ​that​ ​takes​ ​the​ ​lead​ ​on​ ​a​ ​specific​ ​policy​ ​or​​procedure.​ ​Usually​​the​​WVOT​
               ​service provider/owner that the policy is related to or governs.​

     ​4.7.​   ​ ersonally​ ​Identifiable​ ​Information​ ​(PII)​ ​–​ ​protected​ ​and​ ​non-protected​
              P
              ​information that can be used to identify, locate, or contact an individual.​

     ​4.8.​   ​ rotected​ ​Health​ ​Information​ ​(PHI)​ ​–​ ​Information,​ ​including​ ​demographic​

              ​data,​ ​that​ ​relates​ ​to:​ ​an​ ​individual’s​ ​past,​ ​present​ ​or​ ​future​ ​physical​ ​or​

                ​the​​past,​​present,​​or​​future​​payment​​for​​the​​provision​​of​​health​​care​​to​​the​
                 ​individual,​ ​and​ ​that​ ​identifies​ ​the​ ​individual​ ​or​ ​for​ ​which​ ​there​ ​is​ ​a​

                   ​includes​ ​many​​common​​identifiers​​(e.g.,​​name,​​address,​​birth​​date,​​Social​
