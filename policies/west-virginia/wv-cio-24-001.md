# Cloud Data Retention (CIO-24-001)

## At a glance

The Office of Technology's retention policy for end-user account data in the cloud (the file is titled 'Retention Policy for Google Email, Chat, and Personal Drive Data'); it cites W. Va. Code 5A-6-4 as the authority to set enterprise IT standards.

## Full text

                          West Virginia Office of Technology Policy
                          Retention Policy for Google Email, Chat, and
                          Personal Drive Data
                                                                              Effective Data: 03/15/2024
                                                                              Policy Number: CIO-24-001

Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a uniform retention policy for end-user account data stored in
the cloud.

W.Va. Code §5A-6-4 authorizes the Chief Information Officer (CIO) to establish information technology
governance and enterprise standards to support a unified approach to information technology across
state government.

Scope
This policy applies to all user accounts created and managed by the WV Office of Technology.

Policy
Agencies own the data created by or originating with the Agency, data transferred to the Agency from a
third party, and all data that is the output of computer processing or other electronic manipulation of
any data that was created by or in any way originated with the Agency, whether such data or output is
stored on the Agency’s hardware, WVOT’s hardware or exists in any system owned, maintained or
otherwise controlled by either the Agency or by WVOT.

The WVOT, as an IT service provider for Agencies, may hold or have access to data owned by the Agency.
If Agencies delete their data stored outside the scope of this policy, WVOT only retains it for backup
periods defined by the agency and provided to OT to support operational resiliency and IT service
continuity. WVOT additionally makes available when requested certain long term storage or legal hold
services for data that must be frozen or retained.

WVOT does not backup data stored on state owned computers.

User account data includes data associated with employee accounts, such as data stored in an employee
personal cloud drive, an employee’s email account, or chats. This data is owned by the Agency. Agencies
may inspect or review data associated with an employee’s personal drive, chat, or email account at any
time by requesting that service from WVOT.

Should an Agency require a more restrictive retention period for chats/instant messages for user email,
the Agency may request a custom retention period through the self-service portal. The Agency must
include the justification (e.g. cite to federal law requiring different retention or business case) with the
request. If an agency requires a more restrictive retention period, it must be applied to the entire
agency.

When an account deprovision request is made, the agency may request that another employee be given
access to the deprovisioned account to save any data associated with the account in another location,
monitor the inbox, or perform any other functions necessary for business continuity. This request must
be made within 30 days of the deprovisioning request.

FOR ACCOUNTS CONTAINING DATA THAT MAY BE THE SUBJECT OF LITIGATION OR WHICH MAY
OTHERWISE NEED TO BE PRESERVED, AGENCIES MUST REQUEST A LEGAL HOLD TO BE PLACED ON THE
ACCOUNT. Agency legal counsel or agency leadership may request a legal hold on any user account that
needs to be preserved. The legal hold will remain on the account until the Agency notifies the WVOT the
hold may be released. WVOT will bill the Agency for the cost of providing this service.

Relevant Authority
W.Va. Code §5A-6-4
