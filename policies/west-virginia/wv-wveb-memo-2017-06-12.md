# Memo to reaffirm privacy policies (BRIM Memorandum, June 12, 2017)

## At a glance

The memorandum of the Director of the Board of Risk and Insurance Management to the Chief of Staff and Cabinet Secretaries, dated June 12, 2017, saying that after Executive Order 3-17 moved the State Privacy Office to the Board the current Executive Branch Privacy Policies and Procedures remain in effect. The page says the policies were reaffirmed by it.

## Full text

Memorandum
TO:            Chief of Staff and Cabinet Secretaries

FROM:          Mary Jane Pickens, Director – Board of Risk and Insurance
               Management, Deputy Cabinet Secretary – Department of
               Administration

DATE:          June 12, 2017

RE:            West Virginia Executive Branch Privacy Policies

CC:            Privacy Management Team

        On May 18, 2017, the West Virginia State Privacy Office was moved by Executive Order
3-17 from the Health Care Authority to the Board of Risk and Insurance Management (BRIM).

        The current Executive Branch Privacy Policies and Procedures will remain in effect and
are available on the Privacy website at http://www.privacy.wv.gov. Work is underway to review
and update policies as needed.

       The Board of Risk and Insurance Management is appreciative of your support of the
privacy program and the State Privacy Office for their efforts to safeguard our citizens and
employees’ personally identifiable information.

       If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact Sallie Milam, Chief Privacy
Officer at 304-766-2646, extension 57624.
