# Reporting Requirements for Public Agencies (NCDIT: Reporting Requirements for Public Agencies)

## At a glance

The State CIO's May 29, 2025 memorandum on the annual report public agencies owe under G.S. 143-805.

## Full text

                                                                                          Josh Stein
                                                                                           Governor

                                                                                       Teena Piccione
                                                                         Secretary and State Chief Information Officer

Memorandum

To:         Public Agency CIOs and IT Directors

From:       Teena Piccione, Secretary and State Chief Information officer

CC:         Dylan Frick, NCDIT Secretary for External Affairs
            Angela Morando, NCDIT Legislative Liaison

Subject: Public Agency Reporting Requirements Under N.C.G.S. 143-805

Date:       May 29, 2025

On Dec. 20, 2024, the N.C. Department of Information Technology issued a memo outlining a requirement
under N.C.G.S. 143-805 that mandates public agencies to report to the State Chief Information Officer
details about unauthorized incidents of viewing and attempted viewing of pornography on government
networks and devices.

This memo provides further information about who is required to report, what information is required, and
how and when you should submit your reports.

Who Must Report

All North Carolina public agencies, as defined by N.C.G.S. 143-805(g)(5), are required to report.

Required Information

You or someone you delegate should submit the following information on behalf of your agency:

      •   The number of incidents involving unauthorized viewing and attempted viewing of pornography on
          networks and devices (e.g., laptops, desktops, and mobile devices) belonging to public agencies

      •   Whether the individuals involved were employees, elected officials, appointees, or students

      •   Whether incidents occurred on public agency-controlled devices

You only need to submit confirmed incidents, whether they were observed, found using content
filters, or discovered another way. Please see N.C.G.S. 143-805 for more information.

FY 2025 Reporting Period

      •   The reporting period for FY 2025 (July 1, 2024, to June 30, 2025) is July 1 to Aug. 1, 2025.

      •   No action is necessary if you have nothing to report.

      •   Submit information via NCDIT’s reporting website at it.nc.gov/805. Please note: the webpage is
          currently available only for you to view. You will not be able to submit information until July 1.

We appreciate your cooperation and support in this important effort. If you have any questions about the
reporting process, please send an email to ditcommunications@nc.gov.

Thank you for your partnership and service to the people of North Carolina.

                                                    it.nc.gov
