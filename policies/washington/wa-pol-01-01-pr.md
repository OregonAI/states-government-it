Using the Waiver/Exemption Request Procedure

This Procedure is meant to be used as a companion guide to the Electronic
Waiver/Exemption Request Form. It includes some general tips on navigating and routing
the form and details on the information you will need to complete the form.

To go directly to the field-by-field description for the type of request you need to submit
(Exemption, Initial, Extension or Closure), click the link for the request type in the Table of
Contents.

If you have any questions or feedback on the Electronic Waiver/Exemption Request Form or
this Procedure, please contact the Enterprise IT Policy Office.

General Information

Save and Resume

When completing a Waiver or Exemption Request Form, you can save your progress at any
point to resume later. This is particularly helpful if you need input from multiple subject
matter experts for completion of the request.

The Save and Resume feature will also make gathering the necessary signatures easier.
When you save the form, you will be given the option to email a link to the in-progress form.
You can also save the link for sharing. The data will be saved in the linked form for 30 days
from the date of creation. Please note that all data entered will be lost after 30 days. To
preserve your partially completed form beyond the 30 days, simply create a new link using
the Save and Resume feature. Once you do this, the previously created link will no longer
work.

Note: Most of the fields in the request forms are required. If a field is not required, that will be
noted in this guide.

It is possible to navigate through the form without completing the required fields, but all
required fields must be completed before submitting. This is an important consideration if
your process for collecting signatures is to route the form to the signatories to sign and
submit the form.

Collecting Necessary Signatures

The waiver or exemption request form must be signed by the agency head (or the person
delegated with signing authority in the Annual Certification) and the CIO (or equivalent).
Request forms that are submitted without the required signatures, or with signatures from
anyone other than the required parties, will not be accepted.

If both signatories are not able to sign the form together, the Save and Resume feature
(described above) is a useful feature for collecting signatures asynchronously. To do this, the
recommended steps are:

       1. Click Save and Resume to create a link to the partially completed form.
       2. Send the link to the first signatory.

Policy Scope and Applicability
Some policies/standards have sections that outline scope and applicability. Over time, these
sections will be added to all new policies and standards when they undergo revision.

Scope: The scope section identifies which technology/use cases are in- or out-of-scope of
the policy. If a technology or use case is out-of-scope, there is no need to submit a waiver or
exemption request.

Applicability: The applicability section details who is required to comply with the policy.

Exemptions
Background
Until recently, there was no option for exemption from policy compliance. All deviation from
policy has instead been addressed with time-limited, conditional waivers. In reviewing our
waiver metrics, we realized that there are very specific edge cases that were generating a
large number of waivers and, due to the specific circumstances, had no clear path to
compliance. We have begun examining these use cases more closely and, when
appropriate, we are establishing criteria under which exemptions may be issued.

                                                                                              5

Exemption Process
Tip: If you are not sure whether you meet the criteria for an exemption, you can submit an
unsigned exemption request for policy staff to review. If it is determined that an exemption
is warranted, you will then be asked to submit a formal, signed exemption request. If an
exemption is not an option, you will then need to proceed with a waiver request.

Exemptions will be considered for applications that are in scope of the policy and satisfy the
requirements for exemption based on criteria that are specific to each policy/standard. Not
all policies/standards offer the option of an exemption. If there are no criteria for exemption
listed in the policy, an exemption is not an option for that policy.

If your exemption request is approved, an official letter of exemption will be issued to your
agency in the same way waiver letters are currently issued. Additionally, although there will
be no ongoing conditions related to an exemption, agencies will be asked to attest to their
continued qualification for the exemption in the Annual IT Policy Certification that is due in
September of each year. Because the conditions under which exemptions are issued are
subject to change, it is necessary to verify continued qualification. For example, if an
exemption is issued based on the number of users of a system, that exemption will cease to
apply if the number of users grows beyond the number allowed under the exemption
criteria.

Form Instructions by Request Type

After providing information about your agency, you will be asked to identify the type of
request you are making. The request type options are:

   •   Exemption – Select this option if your agency qualifies for an exemption based on the
       exemption criteria outlined in the specific policy. For more information, see the
       Exemptions section in this guide.
   •   Initial – Select this option if your agency does not have a current waiver for the policy
       for which a waiver is needed.
   •   Extension – Select this option if your agency has a current (active or expired) waiver
       and needs to extend it due to an inability to reach compliance during the original
       waiver period. Extensions will be issued at the discretion of the policy owners and
       State Chief Information Officer and are not guaranteed. We encourage agencies to
       do their best to achieve compliance within the time frame of the initial waiver.
   •   Closure – Select this option if your agency has a current (active or expired) waiver and
       compliance, including all conditions, has been met.

Some of the fields in the waiver request form are specific to the request type, so it’s
important to select the correct option.

Note: This guide is broken out by request type. This allows you to walk through the
form field-by-field for the specific request you are submitting. Consequently, there is
duplicate information in the guide due to some sections being required for multiple
request types.

    To request an extension of a waiver, you will need to provide information about the

          The condition due date
