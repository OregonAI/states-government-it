 EA-2: Establishing an Enterprise Service

SUBMITTING AGENCY NAME:

NAME OF PROPOSED ENTERPRISE SERVICE:

PROPOSED BUSINESS OWNER:

DESCRIPTION OF THE SERVICE:

ENTERPRISE SERVICE CONSIDERATIONS:

1. Is this a current service?

                                  Yes              No

         a. If yes, what is the justification for elevating this to an enterprise service.

         b. If not a current service, what is the business problem to be solved or benefit
            gained by creating a new enterprise service?

2. Provide Links to laws, policies and standards, or regulatory requirements that apply
   across agencies, if any.

3. What is the current or planned ongoing funding or rate model?

                                                                                                1

4. Are investments in the service necessary to bring it up to an enterprise service
   level? If yes, have funds been identified and are there planned project(s)?

5. What may trigger a change in the funding or rate model after the service is
   established?

6. Who is the expected customer base? For example: statewide or agencies with an
   industry focus.

7. How does this align with the WaTech roadmap and the strategic action plan goals?

8. Can the enterprise service scale to meet the maximum customer demand? If not,
   what changes are needed to the platform, hardware, software or what additional
   resources are required for the service to scale.

9. Describe how the proposed enterprise service provides a cost model or rate that
   provides lower costs as volume increases.

 TIMELINE FOR ADOPTION:

   1. Describe the planned pace of adoption.

   2. Total planned duration for adoption by all agencies in scope.

   3. Barriers to adoption.

   4. Define triggers for agency adoption. Examples could include agency investments
      for maintenance, enhancements, replacement, or acquisition of a similar system.
      Explain the overall strategy for adoption.
