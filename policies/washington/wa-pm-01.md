    1. WaTech will consider the investment size, risk, and expected impact on residents
       and operations to determine whether it qualifies as a major Information
       Technology (IT) investment requiring approval and oversight.

    2. Agencies will submit investment details to WaTech in accordance with PM-01-
       01-PR IT Investments Approval and Oversight Procedure for every IT investment
       with:

            a. Total cost that includes a combined level of effort of more than $500,000
                OR,

            b. Duration longer than 6 months.

    3. WaTech will make the final determination if the investment is or is not a major
       project subject to applicable oversight processes.

            a. WaTech will assess the project profile for risks and complexity and assign a
               Tier to all major projects subject to oversight processes in alignment with
               the Tiered Oversight Requirements Standard.

            b. WaTech will share all required and recommended best practice
               documentation and processes applicable to the assigned Tier with the
               agency.

    4. Agencies will follow procedures WaTech establishes for the review, approval,
       and oversight of major IT investments.

5. WaTech will employ Lean principles to implement this policy, continuously
   improving the procedure and related materials.

6. State agencies and WaTech will make critical documentation publicly available
   using the reporting tools provided by WaTech, in accordance with established
   procedures and PM01-04-S Tiered Oversight and Reporting Standard.
