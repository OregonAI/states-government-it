# Information Technology Readiness Budget Category Descriptions (Statewide Procedure 107-004-180_PR, Budget Category Descriptions)

## At a glance

Listed in the EIS policy library under 107-004-180; defines the budget categories agencies use in IT funding requests. The workbook and transmittal form in the same library are forms and are left out.
