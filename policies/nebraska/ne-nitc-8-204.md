# 8-204. Email. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 8-204)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 8-204, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102), in the Information Security Policy of chapter 8, whose scope is all IT systems for which the state has administrative responsibility (8-102).

## Full text

                                                                                Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                           Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-204. Email.
    (1) Users of the state email system must not set up rules, or use any other methodology, to
automatically forward emails to a personal or other account outside of the state network unless
approved by the state information security officer and, if applicable, the agency information
security officer.
    (2) HIGH IMPACT or MODERATE IMPACT data must not be sent by email, or stored in
the email system, unless it has been encrypted using technology approved by the state
information security officer and, if applicable, the agency information security officer.
