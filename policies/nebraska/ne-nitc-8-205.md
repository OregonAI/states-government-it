# 8-205. Portable storage devices. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 8-205)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 8-205, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102), in the Information Security Policy of chapter 8, whose scope is all IT systems for which the state has administrative responsibility (8-102).

## Full text

                                                                               Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                                          Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-205. Portable storage devices.
   (1) HIGH IMPACT or MODERATE IMPACT data must not be stored on portable storage
devices unless it has been encrypted using OCIO-approved technology.
     (2) Portable storage devices must not be left in a vehicle unattended.
