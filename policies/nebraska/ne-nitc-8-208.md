# 8-208. External service providers. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 8-208)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 8-208, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102), in the Information Security Policy of chapter 8, whose scope is all IT systems for which the state has administrative responsibility (8-102).

## Full text

                                                                    Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                               Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-208. External service providers.
    All external service providers with access to HIGH IMPACT or MODERATE IMPACT
information must have a written agreement that includes the minimum security requirements
necessary for the protection of this information. The state information security officer may
inspect these external service provider arrangements to ensure compliance with state policies and
requirements.
