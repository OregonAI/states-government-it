# 8-903. Data inventory. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 8-903)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 8-903, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102), in the Information Security Policy of chapter 8, whose scope is all IT systems for which the state has administrative responsibility (8-102).

## Full text

                                                                    Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                               Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-903. Data inventory.
    Each agency shall identify and classify all information according to this policy. Each agency
shall maintain an inventory of where HIGH IMPACT and MODERATE IMPACT information
reside, so those environments can be assessed for security adequacy.
