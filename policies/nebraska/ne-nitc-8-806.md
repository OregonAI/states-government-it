# 8-806. Security deficiencies. (NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines 8-806)

## At a glance

NITC Technical Standards and Guidelines section 8-806, adopted by the Commission under Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-516 and applying to state agencies, boards and commissions (1-102), in the Information Security Policy of chapter 8, whose scope is all IT systems for which the state has administrative responsibility (8-102).

## Full text

                                                          Nebraska Information Technology Commission
                                                                     Technical Standards and Guidelines

8-806. Security deficiencies.
   All security deficiencies reported or identified in any security review, scan, assessment, or
analysis must be documented in the state or agency plan of action and milestones report. These
gaps must be managed to mitigation, remediation, or approved risk acceptance.
