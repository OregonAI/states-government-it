# RCW 39.34.240 - Data requests - When written agreement required (RCW 39.34.240)

## At a glance

Requires a written agreement, conforming to the policies of the Office of Cybersecurity, before one public agency requests category 3 or higher data from another. The sibling of RCW 39.26.340 (agency to contractor), from the same act (2021 c 291). Cited by WaTech policies DATA-03 and POL-01. One section of the interlocal cooperation chapter.

## Full text

RCW 39.34.240
Data requests—When written agreement required.
(1) If a public agency is requesting from another public agency category 3 or higher data, as defined in policy established in accordance with RCW 43.105.054, the requesting agency shall provide for a written agreement between the agencies that conforms to the policies of the office of cybersecurity.
(2) Nothing in this section shall be construed as limiting audit authorities under chapter 43.09 RCW.
[ 2021 c 291 s 6.]
