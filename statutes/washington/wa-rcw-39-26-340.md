# RCW 39.26.340 - Data-sharing agreements - When required (RCW 39.26.340)

## At a glance

Requires a written data-sharing agreement before an agency shares category 3 or higher data with a contractor, conforming to the data-sharing policies the Office of Cybersecurity sets under RCW 43.105.054. Cited by WaTech policies DATA-03 and DATA-04. One section of the state procurement chapter (the rest is left out, see the left-out notes).

## Full text

RCW 39.26.340
Data-sharing agreements—When required.
(1) Before an agency shares with a contractor category 3 or higher data, as defined in policy established in accordance with RCW 43.105.054, a written data-sharing agreement must be in place. Such agreements shall conform to the policies for data sharing specified by the office of cybersecurity under the authority of RCW 43.105.054.
(2) Nothing in this section shall be construed as limiting audit authorities under chapter 43.09 RCW.
[ 2021 c 291 s 5.]
