# Code of Laws, Section 11-35-820. Creation of Information Technology Management Office (Consolidated Procurement Code) (S.C. Code § 11-35-820)

## At a glance

OWNER DECISION 2026-11-13: a provision in law that is not itself about IT is in scope, as a section slice, when its own focus is the governance of state government IT. The slice is one section of Title 11, Chapter 35, the South Carolina Consolidated Procurement Code, which is not IT law as a whole. Section 11-35-820 creates, within the Division of Procurement Services, the Information Technology Management Office headed by the Information Technology Management Officer, which 'is responsible for administering all procurement and contracting activities undertaken for governmental bodies involving information technology', and requires that 'all procurements involving information technology, and any pre-procurement and post-procurement activities in this area, must be conducted in accordance with the regulations promulgated by the board.' It is the only section of the chapter whose own subject is IT: Section 11-35-1580, 'Information technology procurements', is printed as repealed (2019 Act No. 41, eff May 13, 2019), and the other sections that name information technology (the definitions in 11-35-310, and the general procurement rules that list it among supplies, services and construction) apply to IT along with everything else and are not taken.

## Full text

SECTION 11-35-820. Creation of Information Technology Management Office.
There is created within the Division of Procurement Services, the Information Technology Management Office to be headed by the Information Technology Management Officer. The office is responsible for administering all procurement and contracting activities undertaken for governmental bodies involving information technology in accordance with this chapter, and may establish a training and certification program in accordance with Section 11-35-1030. All procurements involving information technology, and any pre-procurement and post-procurement activities in this area, must be conducted in accordance with the regulations promulgated by the board.
HISTORY: 1981 Act No. 148, SECTION 1; 1997 Act No. 153, SECTION 1; 2006 Act No. 376, SECTION 15; 2019 Act No. 41 (S.530), SECTION 13, eff May 13, 2019.
Editor's Note
2019 Act No. 41, SECTION 80, provides as follows:
"SECTION 80. This act takes effect upon approval by the Governor and applies to solicitations issued after that date."
Effect of Amendment
2019 Act No. 41, SECTION 13, in the first sentence, substituted "Division of Procurement Services" for "board", inserted the second sentence, and in the third sentence, deleted ", except as otherwise provided in this code by specific reference to the Information Technology management Office" at the end.
