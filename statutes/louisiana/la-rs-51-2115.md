# R.S. 51:2115. Prohibition on contracting with a provider (R.S. 51:2115)

## At a glance

A public body may not contract for managed security services with an unregistered provider. Page address built from the Legislature's own Title 51 table of contents (Laws_Toc.aspx), which lists each section's Law.aspx?d= link; the label and section line in the fetched bytes were checked.

## Full text

RS 51:2115
§2115. Prohibition on contracting with a provider
A public body shall not enter into a contract for managed security services with a provider that has not registered with the secretary of state or has failed to renew its registration with the secretary of state. Such a contract shall be null and void.
Acts 2020, No. 117, §2, eff. Feb. 1, 2021.
