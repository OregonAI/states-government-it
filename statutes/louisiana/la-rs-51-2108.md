# R.S. 51:2108. Reporting (R.S. 51:2108)

## At a glance

Relieves a state entity that receives cybersecurity information shared under the Chapter of the reporting requirements of 6 U.S.C. 1508: it concerns state entities only. Page address built from the Legislature's own Title 51 table of contents (Laws_Toc.aspx), which lists each section's Law.aspx?d= link; the label and section line in the fetched bytes were checked.

## Full text

RS 51:2108
§2108. Reporting
No state entity that receives cybersecurity information shared pursuant to the provisions of this Chapter shall be subject to the reporting requirements of 6 U.S.C. 1508.
Acts 2019, No. 187, §1.
