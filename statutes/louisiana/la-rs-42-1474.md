# R.S. 42:1474. Exceptions (R.S. 42:1474)

## At a glance

Exceptions to the covered-application prohibition: networks open to the public. Page address built from the Legislature's own Title 42 table of contents (Laws_Toc.aspx), which lists each section's Law.aspx?d= link; the label and section line in the fetched bytes were checked.

## Full text

RS 42:1474
§1474. Exceptions
The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to networks that are open and available for public access.
Acts 2023, No. 308, §1, eff. June 13, 2023.
