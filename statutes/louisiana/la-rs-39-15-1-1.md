# R.S. 39:15.1.1. Criminal history information; access to federal tax information (R.S. 39:15.1.1)

## At a glance

Authorizes the Office of Technology Services to run criminal history records checks of its current and prospective employees, contractors and subcontractors, and directs the commissioner of administration to make rules. ## Full text

RS 39:15.1.1
§15.1.1. Criminal history information; access to federal tax information
The division of administration, office of technology services, is authorized to perform criminal history records checks of current and prospective employees, contractors, and subcontractors in accordance with the procedures provided in R.S. 15:587.5. Pursuant to this authorization and to implement the requirements of R.S. 15:587.5, the commissioner of administration shall promulgate rules and regulations with regard to this matter.
Acts 2017, No. 147, §4, eff. June 12, 2017.
