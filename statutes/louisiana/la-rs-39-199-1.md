# R.S. 39:199.1. Alternative procedures (R.S. 39:199.1)

## At a glance

Alternative procedures: an agency need not follow the IT procurement Part in the circumstances the section lists. Page address built from the Legislature's own Title 39 table of contents (Laws_Toc.aspx), which lists each section's Law.aspx?d= link; the label and section line in the fetched bytes were checked.

## Full text

RS 39:199.1
§199.1. Alternative procedures
Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, an agency shall not be required to follow the procedures provided in this Part or the Louisiana Procurement Code for procurement of software and the hardware used to support the software if the alternative procedures to be used by the agency are approved by the Joint Legislative Committee on Technology and Cybersecurity and the specific procurement is approved by the Joint Legislative Committee on the Budget.
Acts 2020, No. 273, §1.
