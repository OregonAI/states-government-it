## Full text

RS 39:15.1.2
§15.1.2. Criminal history information; access to restricted data
The division of administration, office of technology services, is authorized to perform criminal history records checks of current and prospective employees, contractors, and subcontractors in accordance with the procedures provided in R.S. 15:587.6. Pursuant to this authorization and to implement the requirements of R.S. 15:587.6, the commissioner of administration shall promulgate rules and regulations with regard to this matter.
Acts 2017, No. 147, §4, eff. June 12, 2017.
