# 18-102 - Government information technology (A.R.S. 18-102)

## At a glance

Makes the department of administration responsible for government information technology functions as prescribed in chapter 1.

## Full text

18-102. Government information technology
The department is responsible for government information technology functions as prescribed in this chapter.
