# Board; reports, Neb. Rev. Stat. 84-1205.05 (Neb. Rev. Stat. section 84-1205.05)

## At a glance

The State Records Board "shall provide annual reports to the Executive Board of the Legislative Council and Nebraska Information Technology Commission" on the portal and electronic access to public records: the oversight reporting line of the state portal to the IT Commission. Owner scope decision 2026-11-13 (IT governance provision of a records chapter).

## Full text

84-1205.05. Board; reports.
The board shall provide annual reports to the Executive Board of the Legislative Council and Nebraska Information Technology Commission on its activities pursuant to sections 84-1205 to 84-1205.03. The report submitted to the executive board shall be submitted electronically.
Source: Laws 1997, LB 590, § 11; Laws 1998, LB 924, § 55; Laws 2012, LB719, § 14; Laws 2012, LB782, § 230; Laws 2012, LB880, § 5.
