# Electronic authentication; substitution for signature (NMSA 1978, 14-3-15.2)

## At a glance

Section 14-3-15.2 of the Public Records Act, one sentence: 'Whenever there is a requirement for a signature on any document, electronic authentication that meets the standards promulgated by the commission may be substituted.' In the Public Records Act 'commission' means the state commission of public records (14-3-2), so the section makes the standards of that commission the test for substituting electronic authentication for a signature. The focus is IT governance: the section names the state commission of public records (14-3-2(C)) as the body whose standards decide when electronic authentication may replace a signature, and the electronic signature rule 1.12.7 NMAC (State Commission of Public Records and State Records Administrator) is issued under it. The sections around it, on records in general, are not in this document. ## Full text

14-3-15.2. Electronic authentication; substitution for signature.
   Whenever there is a requirement for a signature on any document, electronic
authentication that meets the standards promulgated by the commission may be
substituted.

History: Laws 1995, ch. 27, § 1.
