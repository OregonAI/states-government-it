# Records Reproduction Act, section 4 (proposed technical standard) (MCL 24.404 (Records Reproduction Act, Act 116 of 1992))

## At a glance

The procedure for the technical standards on electronic records storage and reproduction: the board approves, disapproves or revises a proposed standard within 60 days, the department must first seek advice from the department of information technology and from county, municipal and information-technology-industry representatives (24.404(2)), and standards take effect only after publication in the Michigan register (24.404(3)). Owner scope decision 2026-11-13 (adjacent subject whose focus is governance of IT). Section 24.403 (a law that references the act incorporates the approved reproduction methods, for reproductions made by private persons) is not seeded: it does not govern state IT.

## Full text

 24.404 Proposed technical standard.
 Sec. 4.
   (1) The board shall, within 60 days of receipt of a proposed technical standard from the
 department, approve, disapprove, or revise the proposed technical standard.

    (2) Before submitting a proposed technical standard to the board under this section, the
 department shall seek advice and comment from the department of information technology
 and at least 1 representative from each of the following entities:
    (a) County government.
    (b) City, township, or village government.
    (c) The information technology industry.
    (3) Proposed and final technical standards shall be published in the Michigan register. A
 technical standard shall not take effect before its publication in the Michigan register.
 History: Add. 2004, Act 574, Imd. Eff. Jan. 3, 2005
 Compiler's Notes: For transfer of powers and duties of department of history, arts, and libraries regarding
