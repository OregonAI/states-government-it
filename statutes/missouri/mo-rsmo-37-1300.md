# Section 37.1300, RSMo: GIS advisory council, definitions (Section 37.1300, RSMo)

## At a glance

Definitions for the Missouri GIS Advisory Council (sections 37.1300 to 37.1320, Chapter 37, effective 28 August 2026, L. 2026 S.B. 959): council, geographic information system, geospatial and the Missouri spatial data information service (MSDIS). OWNER DECISION: geospatial data infrastructure is seeded as state data governance housed in ITSD; see left_out.

## Full text

Title IV EXECUTIVE BRANCH
Chapter 37
Effective - 28 Aug 2026
37.1300. GIS advisory council — definitions. — For the purposes of sections 37.1300 to 37.1330*, the following terms mean:
(1) "Council", the Missouri GIS advisory council established in section 37.1310;
(2) "Geographic information system (GIS)", a computer system for capturing, storing, checking, and displaying data related to positions on the Earth's surface that enables easily seeing, analyzing, and understanding patterns and relationships;
(3) "Geospatial", relating to or denoting data that is associated with a particular location;
(4) "Missouri spatial data information service" or "MSDIS", Missouri's primary spatial data clearinghouse responsible for collecting and distributing vector data, aerial photography, and light detection and ranging elevation data that are generated, updated, and funded by state, local, and regional agencies and governments.
(L. 2026 S.B. 959)
*Section 37.1330 does not exist.
---- end of effective 28 Aug 2026 ----
