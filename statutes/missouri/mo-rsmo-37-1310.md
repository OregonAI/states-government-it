# Section 37.1310, RSMo: GIS advisory council established, duties (Section 37.1310, RSMo)

## At a glance

Establishes the Missouri GIS Advisory Council (sections 37.1300 to 37.1320, Chapter 37, effective 28 August 2026, L. 2026 S.B. 959) within the information technology services division of the Office of Administration, to assist and advise the state on a statewide geospatial data infrastructure. OWNER DECISION: see left_out.

## Full text

Title IV EXECUTIVE BRANCH
Chapter 37
Effective - 28 Aug 2026
37.1310. GIS advisory council established, duties. — There is hereby established within the office of administration the "Missouri GIS Advisory Council", which is charged with assisting and advising the state in ensuring the availability, implementation, and enhancement of a statewide geospatial data infrastructure common to all jurisdictions. The council shall be established within the information technology services division of the office of administration. The council shall serve to support the existing geospatial infrastructure within the state of Missouri and strive toward immediate access to statewide geospatial data for all citizens of this state, especially life-safety entities including NG9-1-1.
(L. 2026 S.B. 959)
