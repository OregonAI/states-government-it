# Participation in technology consolidation projects. (R.C. 126.506)

## At a glance

Each state agency shall take part in the IT consolidation projects the state chief information officer implements under R.C. 125.18 and shall keep a list of its IT assets and their costs in the format the director of administrative services specifies. One section of chapter 126, seeded as its own page.

## Full text

Section 126.506 | Participation in technology consolidation projects.
Effective: October 16, 2009
Latest Legislation: House Bill 1 - 128th General Assembly
(A) Each state agency shall participate in information technology consolidation projects implemented by the state chief information officer under section 125.18 of the Revised Code.
(B) At the direction of and in the format specified by the director of administrative services, each state agency shall maintain a list of information technology assets possessed by the agency and associated costs related to those assets.
Available Versions of this Section
October 16, 2009 – House Bill 1 - 128th General Assembly
