# Long-range information technology capital projects (Mont. Code Ann. 2-17-560 and 2-17-561)

## At a glance

IT funding and oversight: balances of long-range IT capital projects approved by the legislature are reappropriated until the projects are complete, and funds appropriated to executive branch agencies for such projects may not be encumbered until project and security plans are approved by the chief information officer and the budget director when the appropriation act so directs.

## Full text

Montana Code Annotated 2025
TITLE 2. GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE AND ADMINISTRATION
CHAPTER 17. PROPERTY AND SYSTEMS DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT
Part 5. Information Technology -- Internet Privacy

2-17-560. Reappropriation of long-range information technology capital project funds. The remaining balances for long-range information technology capital projects previously approved by the legislature and identified as long-range information technology capital projects in an appropriation act are reappropriated for the purposes of the original appropriation until the projects are completed.
History: En. Sec. 18, Ch. 3, Sp. L. May 2007.

2-17-561. Approval required. Amounts appropriated by the legislature to executive branch agencies, other than the university system, for long-range information technology capital projects may not be encumbered until project and security plans are approved by the chief information officer and the budget director if the legislature directs these approvals as a condition on the appropriations in the bill making the appropriations.
History: En. Sec. 19, Ch. 3, Sp. L. May 2007.
