# Addition of nonlicensed raster-based datasets of publicly funded Kentucky locations to the Geospatial Data Warehouse -- Exceptions -- Confidentiality (KRS 42.744)

## At a glance

Requires entities that create or purchase aerial imagery, LiDAR and other raster datasets with public funds to give a copy to the Commonwealth Office of Technology's Division of Geographic Information Systems for the secure Geospatial Data Warehouse. OWNER DECISION: see KRS 42.650.

## Full text

42.744 Addition of nonlicensed raster-based datasets of publicly funded Kentucky
     locations to the Geospatial Data Warehouse -- Exceptions -- Confidentiality.
(1)   All entities in Kentucky that create or purchase digital ortho-rectified aerial
      imagery, remotely sensed imagery, LiDAR, digital elevation models, or any other
      form of nonlicensed raster-based datasets of locations in Kentucky using public
      funds, in whole or in part, shall provide a copy of the information to the
      Commonwealth Office of Technology, Division of Geographic Information
      Systems, without cost, in order to allow the Commonwealth Office of Technology
      to effectively discharge its statutory responsibility to maintain an accurate and
      complete central statewide geographic information clearinghouse for official state
      use. The imagery provided to the Commonwealth Office of Technology shall be
      added to Kentucky's secure Geospatial Data Warehouse for official government use
      only.
(2)   All entities in Kentucky that create or purchase digital ortho-rectified aerial
      imagery, remotely sensed imagery, LiDAR, digital elevation models, or any other
      form of raster-based datasets of locations in Kentucky using public funds, in whole
      or in part, shall create or purchase that information in a nonlicensed format
      whenever such a format is available.
(3)   Subsection (1) of this section shall not apply to roads, land parcels, structure
      locations, or other vector-based datasets acquired with public funding.
(4)   The Commonwealth Office of Technology shall not disclose to the general public or
      make available for distribution, download, or purchase any data that an entity
      providing data under subsection (1) of this section has requested remain
      confidential.
               Effective: July 14, 2022
               History: Amended 2022 Ky. Acts ch. 229, sec. 6, effective July 14, 2022. -- Amended
                   2021 Ky. Acts ch. 81, sec. 7, effective June 29, 2021. -- Amended 2018 Ky. Acts ch.

                   effective July 12, 2012. -- Repealed and reenacted 2009 Ky. Acts ch. 12, sec. 14,
                   effective June 25, 2009. -- Created 2006 Ky. Acts ch. 206, sec. 1, effective July 12,
                   2006.
               Formerly codified as KRS 11.518.
