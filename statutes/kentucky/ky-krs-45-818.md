# Status report on information technology systems (KRS 45.818)

## At a glance

Requires the executive director of the Commonwealth Office of Technology to give the Capital Projects and Bond Oversight Committee a quarterly status report on information technology systems not yet completed that the General Assembly authorized by line item. Seeded although it sits in the budget chapter (45) because the section is about information technology only.

## Full text

45.818 Status report on information technology systems.
The executive director of the Commonwealth Office of Technology shall provide to the
Capital Projects and Bond Oversight Committee at its January, April, July, and October
regular meetings a status report on any information technology system not yet completed
which received line item authorization by the Kentucky General Assembly or was
authorized pursuant to KRS 45.760(7), excluding systems of an institution as defined
under KRS 164.001. The committee shall prescribe data elements to be included in the
quarterly status reports.
              Effective: June 25, 2009
              History: Amended 2009 Ky. Acts ch. 78, sec. 34, effective June 25, 2009. -- Amended
                  2005 Ky. Acts ch. 85, sec. 67, effective June 20, 2005. -- Created 2002 Ky. Acts ch.
                  328, sec. 1, effective July 15, 2002.
