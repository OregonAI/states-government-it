# Oklahoma Statutes Title 62, Section 34.33: Agreements to administer information technology and telecommunications infrastructure and security (62 O.S. 34.33)

## At a glance

OWNER DECISION 2026-11-13 (a provision whose own focus is the governance of state IT): 'The Office of Management and Enterprise Services is authorized to enter into a multi-year agreement to acquire land, develop, design, construct and furnish facilities necessary for the administration of the state's information technology and telecommunications infrastructure and security. Such action shall not be subject to The Oklahoma Central Purchasing Act.' The section also caps the computer-ready space at ten percent of the facility and at 9,000 square feet and lets OMES use fees, appropriations and federal funds to finance it. It is the last section of the range OMES names (62 O.S. 34.11.1 through 34.33); the first seeded range stops at 34.31.2 because 34.32 is not in force in the printed version.

## Full text

§62-34.33. Agreements to administer information technology and
telecommunications infrastructure and security.
      The Office of Management and Enterprise Services is authorized to enter into a multi-
year agreement to acquire land, develop, design, construct and furnish facilities necessary
for the administration of the state's information technology and telecommunications
infrastructure and security. Such action shall not be subject to The Oklahoma Central

Oklahoma Statutes - Title 62. Public Finance                                                   Page 80

Purchasing Act. The area of the facility authorized by this section dedicated for computer-
ready space shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total square footage of the entire
facility, but under no circumstances shall exceed nine thousand (9,000) square feet. The
Office of Management and Enterprise Services is authorized to use existing and future
funds from fees, appropriations and federal funds, as necessary, to finance such facilities.
Added by Laws 2007, c. 205, § 1, eff. Sept. 1, 2007. Renumbered from § 41.5x of this title by
Laws 2009, c. 441, § 64, eff. July 1, 2009. Amended by Laws 2012, c. 304, § 365.
