# Wis. Stat. 19.68 - Collection of personally identifiable information from Internet users (Wis. Stat. s. 19.68)

## At a glance

In scope by owner decision 2026-11-13 (a provision whose own focus is the governance of state IT): 'No state authority that maintains an Internet site may use that site to obtain personally identifiable information from any person who visits that site without the consent of the person from whom the information is obtained.' It binds state agencies' websites; Internet protocol addresses are excepted. One section of subchapter IV of chapter 19 (personal information practices); the rest of the subchapter is general privacy practice and is left out (see left_out).

## Full text

19.68 Collection of personally identifiable information from Internet users. No state authority that maintains an Internet site may use that site to obtain personally identifiable information from any person who visits that site without the consent of the person from whom the information is obtained. This section does not apply to acquisition of Internet protocol addresses.
