# Geographic information systems; state policy coordination (K.S.A. 74-99f07)

## At a glance

Requires all state departments, agencies, commissions and boards to cooperate with the GIS policy board on state GIS policy.

## Full text

74-99f07. Geographic information systems; state policy coordination. All state departments, agencies, commissions and boards shall cooperate with the Kansas geographic information systems policy board and the state geographic information systems officer in implementing the initiatives of Kansas one map.
History: L. 2012, ch. 174, § 7; June 7.
